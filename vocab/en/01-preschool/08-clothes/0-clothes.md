# Clothes

Every morning: *Get dressed! Put on your socks.* Every night: *Put on your pajamas.*
`shoe`, `shirt`, `coat`, `put on` and `take off` were already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | clothes, pants · wear, get |
| 2 | dress, skirt, sock, hat, jacket, sweater, T-shirt, shorts, boot, glasses |
| 1 | pajamas, glove, pocket, scarf, underwear |

Fixed phrase: **get dressed**.

## How to use them

### 1. Words that are always plural

Some clothes have two legs or two sides, so they're always plural:

| always plural | so use |
|---|---|
| pants, shorts, pajamas, glasses | *Your pants **are** wet.* |
| clothes | *Your clothes **are** dirty.* |

❌ *a pant*, ❌ *my glasses is*.

### 2. `put on` / `take off` vs `wear`

| action (a moment) | state (all day) |
|---|---|
| **put on** — *Put on your jacket.* | **wear** — *Wear your hat.* |
| **take off** — *Take off your boots.* | *I wear glasses.* |

### 3. Getting dressed

*Get dressed!* = put on your clothes. `get` is a very common verb that you'll see in many phrases later.

### 4. Pairs

One of a pair: *a sock, a boot, a glove* → both: *socks, boots, gloves*.

## Watch out

- **US vs UK**: pants / trousers, pajamas / pyjamas, sweater / jumper. In the UK, *pants* means underwear!
- **glasses** for your eyes; *a glass* (for drinking) is a different word.

## Practice

1. Your shorts ___ blue. (is / are)
2. ___ your coat. It's cold! (Put on / Wear)
3. I ___ glasses every day. (put on / wear)
4. Fix it: *Where is my pant?*

Answers: 1. are · 2. Put on · 3. wear · 4. Where are my pants?
