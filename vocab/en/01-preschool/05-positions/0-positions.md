# Positions

*Where is the ball?* — *Under the bed!* Position words say where things are.
`in`, `on`, `at`, `up`, `down`, `out` and `over` were already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | under · outside, back |
| 2 | in front of, next to, behind, between, near, into, around · inside, away · stay |
| 1 | through |

## How to use them

### 1. Where is the ball?

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **in** the box | **on** the box | **under** the box |
| **behind** the box | **in front of** the box | **next to** the box |
| **near** the box | **between** the boxes | **around** the box |

Position word + **the** + thing: *The ball is **under the** bed.*

### 2. `between` takes two things

*The box is **between** the chair **and** the table.*

### 3. Being somewhere vs going somewhere

| place (no movement) | movement |
|---|---|
| *It's **in** the box.* | *Put it **into** the box.* |
| | *Walk **around** the table.* |
| | *Look **through** the window.* |

### 4. `inside` / `outside` on their own

- With a place: *outside **the** house*
- Alone: *Let's go outside.* · *Come inside.* — ❌ Let's go to outside.

### 5. `away` and `back`

- **away** = to its place, or far from here: *Put your toys away.*
- **back** = return: *Come back!*

## Watch out

- ✅ *in front of* — ❌ *in the front of*
- **back** here means *return*. The body part *back* is in Stage 0 (Body).

## Practice

1. The cat is ___ the bed. (under / into)
2. Sit ___ me. (next to / next)
3. Let's go ___. (outside / to outside)
4. Fix it: *Stand in the front of the door.*

Answers: 1. under · 2. next to · 3. outside · 4. Stand in front of the door.
