# Toys & Games

Play is a child's job. *Let's play! My turn! You win!*
`toy`, `ball`, `book`, `play`, `draw` and `throw` were already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | game, park · find · fun |
| 2 | turn, doll, block, picture, paper · hide, build, share, win, dance |
| 1 | crayon, balloon, bubble, slide, swing, sand, puzzle · paint, blow |

Fixed phrases: **hide and seek**, **my turn**.

## How to use them

### 1. Play + what?

| play … | example |
|---|---|
| play + game | *Let's play a game.* · *Let's play hide and seek.* |
| play **with** + toy | *Play with your doll.* |
| play **in / at** + place | *Play in the sand.* · *Play at the park.* |

### 2. Taking turns

*My turn!* · *Your turn.* — `turn` here is a noun: the time when it's your go.

### 3. `can't`

**can't** = can not: *I can't find it.*

### 4. `fun`

`fun` describes something you enjoy: *This is fun!*
❌ *This is funny* — *funny* (Stage 0) means it makes you laugh.

### 5. At the park

*Go down the slide.* · *Push me on the swing.* · *Play in the sand.*

## Watch out

- **paint** a picture (with a brush) vs **draw** a picture (with a pen or crayon).
- **slide** and **swing** are nouns here (the park things), but they're also verbs.

## Practice

1. Let's play ___ the park. (at / with)
2. Play ___ your blocks. (with / at)
3. That game was ___! I loved it. (fun / funny)
4. Fix it: *I can not to find it.*

Answers: 1. at · 2. with · 3. fun · 4. I can't find it.
