# Animals

Picture books, the farm, the zoo: animals are everywhere in a child's world, along with their sounds.
`dog` was already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | animal, cat, bird, fish · say |
| 2 | cow, pig, horse, sheep, duck, rabbit, mouse, bear, lion, elephant, monkey, snake · zoo, farm, pet · fly, swim, feed · cute, pretty |
| 1 | tiger, giraffe, frog, bee, butterfly, spider · bite · moo, woof, meow, quack |

## How to use them

### 1. Animal sounds

| animal | says |
|---|---|
| cow | moo |
| dog | woof |
| cat | meow |
| duck | quack |

*What does the cow say?* — *The cow **says** moo.*

### 2. Irregular plurals

| one | many |
|---|---|
| cat | cats |
| sheep | **sheep** (no change) |
| fish | **fish** (no change) |
| mouse | **mice** |

### 3. Talking about all animals of a kind

Use the plural with no *the*:

- *Birds fly.* · *Fish swim.* · *Monkeys jump.*
- ❌ *The birds fly.* (= only those birds)

### 4. Asking with `does`

*Does the dog bite?* · *What **does** the cow say?*
After `does`, the verb has no `-s`: ✅ *Does it bite?* — ❌ Does it bites?

### 5. Where animals live

- **pet**: lives at home — cat, dog, fish, rabbit
- **farm**: cow, pig, horse, sheep, duck
- **zoo**: lion, tiger, elephant, monkey, giraffe

## Watch out

- **bear** is the animal; **teddy bear** (Stage 0) is the toy.
- **chicken** (Stage 0) is both the bird and the meat.
- **cute** is for small and sweet things; **pretty** for nice-looking things.

## Practice

1. The cat says ___.
2. one mouse, two ___
3. ___ swim. (Fish / The fishes)
4. Fix it: *Does the dog bites?*

Answers: 1. meow · 2. mice · 3. Fish · 4. Does the dog bite?
