# Time of Day

A child's day has a rhythm: wake up, breakfast, play, nap, dinner, bedtime.
The words for that rhythm come before clocks and calendars (Stage 2).

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | morning, afternoon, evening, night, day · today, tomorrow, yesterday, later |
| 2 | nap · soon, tonight · late, early · before, after |
| 1 | bedtime |

Fixed phrases: **good afternoon, good evening, see you, have a good day**.

## How to use them

### 1. Parts of the day

| | when | greeting |
|---|---|---|
| morning | wake up → lunch | *Good morning.* |
| afternoon | lunch → dinner | *Good afternoon.* |
| evening | dinner → bed | *Good evening.* |
| night | sleep | *Good night.* (bye only) |

- **in the** morning / afternoon / evening
- **at** night

### 2. Today, tomorrow, yesterday

No preposition before them:

- ✅ *See you tomorrow.* — ❌ See you on tomorrow.
- *Yesterday I **was** sick.* (`was` = past of `is`)
- **tonight** = this evening / night: *Tonight we have cake.*

### 3. Before and after

*Wash your hands **before** lunch.* · *Play **after** dinner.*

### 4. Late, early, soon, later

| | means |
|---|---|
| early | before the usual time: *You are up early.* |
| late | after the usual time: *It's late.* |
| soon | in a short time: *Dinner is soon.* |
| later | not now, some time after: *Later, okay?* |

## Watch out

- **Good night** is only for leaving or going to bed. To greet someone in the evening, say **Good evening**.
- **late** (adjective) ≠ **later** (= after now).

## Practice

1. I wake up ___ the morning. (in / at)
2. We sleep ___ night. (in / at)
3. See you ___ tomorrow. (on / — )
4. Fix it: *Good night!* (you meet a friend at 7 pm)

Answers: 1. in · 2. at · 3. — (nothing) · 4. Good evening!
