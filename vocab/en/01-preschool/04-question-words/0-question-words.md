# Question Words

Around age three, children ask *why?* about everything. Question words let you ask about anything.
`what`, `where` and `why` were already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| word | asks about | example |
|---|---|---|
| what | a thing | *What is your name?* |
| who | a person | *Who is that man?* |
| where | a place | *Where is daddy?* |
| when | a time | *When is lunch?* |
| why | a reason | *Why are you sad?* |
| how | the way / state | *How are you?* |
| which | a choice | *Which cup is yours?* |
| whose | owner | *Whose shoe is this?* |

Importance: **3** for what, who, where, when, why, how · **2** which · **1** whose.

Also: **fine** (adjective) · **yours**.

## How to use them

### 1. Word order

Question word → `be` → the person or thing:

| question word | be | subject |
|---|---|---|
| Where | is | daddy? |
| Who | is | that man? |
| How | are | you? |

❌ *Where daddy is?*

### 2. `How` + one more word

- *How **old** are you?* → age: *I'm three.*
- *How **many** fingers?* → number: *Five.*

### 3. *How are you?*

The usual answer: *I'm fine, thank you.* It's a greeting, not a real health question.

### 4. `what` vs `which`

- **what** = open question: *What is it?*
- **which** = pick from a few you can see: *Which one?*

### 5. `your` vs `yours`

- **your** + noun: *your cup*
- **yours** alone: *Which cup is **yours**?* (like *my* / *mine* in Stage 0)

## Watch out

- **whose** (owner) and **who's** (= who is) sound the same: *Whose shoe?* ≠ *Who's there?*

## Practice

1. ___ is lunch? — At twelve. (Where / When)
2. ___ is that woman? — My grandma. (Who / What)
3. Which bag is ___? (your / yours)
4. Fix it: *Where mommy is?*

Answers: 1. When · 2. Who · 3. yours · 4. Where is mommy?
