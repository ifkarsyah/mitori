## How to use them

### 1. `the` for things there's only one of

*the sun, the moon, the sky, the sea*

- ✅ *The sun is hot.* — ❌ Sun is hot.
- ✅ *Look at the sky.*

### 2. Weather with `it`

English needs a subject, so weather uses **it**:

- *It's raining.* · *It's cold.*
- ❌ *Is raining.*

### 3. Uncountable nature words

No *a*, no `-s`: **grass, snow, rain, wind, sand, water**

- ✅ *The grass is green.* — ❌ The grasses are green.

### 4. Irregular plural

**leaf → leaves**: *The leaves are red and yellow.*

### 5. `beautiful` and `pretty`

Both mean nice to look at. **beautiful** is stronger: *A beautiful flower.* *A beautiful mountain.*

## Watch out

- **sea** = the water · **beach** = the sand next to it.
- **rain** and **snow** are also verbs: *It's raining.* *It's snowing.*
- **garden** (UK) is also *yard* (US) for the grass around a house.

## Practice

1. ___ moon is round. (A / The)
2. ___ raining. (Is / It's)
3. one leaf, two ___
4. Fix it: *Sun is hot.*

Answers: 1. The · 2. It's · 3. leaves · 4. The sun is hot.
