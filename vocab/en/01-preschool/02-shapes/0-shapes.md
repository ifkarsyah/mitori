# Shapes

After colors, children learn shapes by drawing and building: *Draw a circle. Make a star.*

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | make |
| 2 | shape, circle, square, star, heart, line · round |
| 1 | triangle |

## How to use them

### 1. Shapes are nouns

They take **a** and plural `-s`:

- *It's **a** circle.* · *Draw **a** star.*
- *Circle**s** and square**s**.*

### 2. `circle` vs `round`

| noun (the shape) | adjective (describes) |
|---|---|
| *Draw **a circle**.* | *The ball is **round**.* |

❌ *The ball is circle.*

### 3. A shape + a color

Color comes first, then the shape: *a **red** triangle*, *a **big** heart*.

### 4. `draw` vs `make`

- **draw** = with a pen or crayon: *Draw a line.*
- **make** = create with your hands or things: *Make a circle with your hands.*

## Watch out

- **star** is a shape and also the light in the night sky (Nature).
- **heart** is a shape and also a body part.
- **square** is also a place in a town (later stage).

## Practice

1. Draw ___ circle. (a / an)
2. The moon is ___. (circle / round)
3. a ___ (red star / star red)
4. Fix it: *Two triangle.*

Answers: 1. a · 2. round · 3. red star · 4. Two triangles.
