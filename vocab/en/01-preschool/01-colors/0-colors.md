# Colors

Colors are one of the first ways a child describes the world: *the **red** ball, the **big** dog*.
In English, a color word is an **adjective**: it describes a noun.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | red, blue, green, yellow, black, white |
| 2 | orange, pink, purple, brown, gray |

Also: **color** (noun), **and** (joins two colors).
`red` was already learned in Stage 0 (*A red apple.*).

## How to use them

### 1. Asking about a color

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| What color is it? | It's red. |
| What color is your bag? | It's pink. |
| What color are your shoes? | They're black. |

`It's` = `it is`, `they're` = `they are`.

### 2. Two places for a color

A color goes **before the noun** or **after `be`**:

| before the noun | after `be` |
|---|---|
| a **blue** ball | The ball **is blue**. |
| **black** shoes | My shoes **are black**. |

- ✅ a blue ball — ❌ a ball blue
- Colors never take plural `-s`: ✅ blue shoes — ❌ blues shoes

### 3. More than one color

- Two colors: *yellow **and** black*
- Three or more: *red, blue, **and** green*

## Watch out

- **orange** is both a fruit and a color.
  *The orange is orange.* = the fruit (noun) has the color (adjective).
- **a / an** depends on the next sound, not on the noun:
  *a blue cup*, but *an orange cup* (vowel sound **o**).
- **US vs UK spelling**: color / colour, gray / grey. This list uses US spelling.

## Practice

1. What color is milk? → It's ___.
2. The banana is ___.
3. ___ orange cup. (a / an)
4. Fix it: *I have a bag pink.*

Answers: 1. white · 2. yellow · 3. an · 4. I have a pink bag.
