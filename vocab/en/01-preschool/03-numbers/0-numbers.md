# Numbers 0–20

Children count fingers, toes, and birthdays. *One, two, three!* · *I'm three years old.*
`one`, `two` and `ten` were already learned in Stage 0.

## Words

| | | | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 zero | 1 one | 2 two | 3 three | 4 four |
| 5 five | 6 six | 7 seven | 8 eight | 9 nine |
| 10 ten | 11 eleven | 12 twelve | 13 thirteen | 14 fourteen |
| 15 fifteen | 16 sixteen | 17 seventeen | 18 eighteen | 19 nineteen |
| 20 twenty | | | | |

Importance: **3** for 1–10 and 20 (used every day), **2** for 0 and 11–19.

Also: **number, year** (nouns) · **count** (verb) · **old** (adjective) · **from**.

## How to use them

### 1. Spelling traps

- 11 and 12 are special: **eleven**, **twelve**.
- 13–19 add **-teen**, but some change: three → **thir**teen, five → **fif**teen, eight → eigh**teen** (one *t*).

### 2. Plural after numbers

One thing = no `-s`. Two or more = `-s`:

- *one cookie* · *four cookie**s***
- *two hand**s*** · *ten finger**s***

### 3. Saying your age

- ✅ *I'm three **years old**.* or *I'm three.*
- ❌ *I have three years.*
- Ask: *How old are you?*

### 4. Counting

- *Count **to** ten.*
- *Count **from** one **to** twenty.*

## Watch out

- **-teen vs -ty**: thir**teen** (13) and thir**ty** (30, later) sound close. *-teen* is stressed at the end: thir-**TEEN**.
- **zero** is often said "**oh**" in phone numbers (later stage).

## Practice

1. 15 = ___
2. 12 = ___
3. I have two ___. (hand / hands)
4. Fix it: *I have five years.*

Answers: 1. fifteen · 2. twelve · 3. hands · 4. I'm five years old.
