# Vehicles

Children love things that move: *Look, a big truck! A red fire truck!*

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | car, bus, train, plane · drive |
| 2 | bike, boat, truck, taxi, road · ride · by |
| 1 | fire truck, police car, ambulance, helicopter, tractor, wheel |

## How to use them

### 1. Drive, ride, or fly?

| verb | used with | example |
|---|---|---|
| **drive** | car, bus, truck, taxi | *Daddy drives the car.* |
| **ride** | bike, horse | *Ride your bike.* |
| fly (Stage 1 Animals) | plane, helicopter | *The plane flies high.* |

### 2. `by` + vehicle

How you travel: **by** + vehicle, no *a / the*:

- ✅ *We go by bus.* · *by car* · *by train*
- ❌ *by the bus*

But with **take**, use *the*: *Let's take **the** bus.*

### 3. `-s` after he / she / it

*Daddy drive**s**.* · *The bus ha**s** big wheels.*
❌ *Daddy drive the car.*

## Watch out

- **plane** = **airplane** (US) = **aeroplane** (UK).
- **truck** (US) = **lorry** (UK).
- **bike** is the everyday word for *bicycle*.
- **fire truck** and **police car** are two words but one thing.

## Practice

1. We go to school ___ bus. (by / with)
2. My sister ___ a bike. (rides / drives)
3. Mommy ___ the car. (drive / drives)
4. Fix it: *We go by the train.*

Answers: 1. by · 2. rides · 3. drives · 4. We go by train.
