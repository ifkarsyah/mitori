# Opposites

Children learn describing words in pairs: *big and small*, *hot and cold*.
Learning both sides at once doubles your words for the same effort.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | small, short, new, fast, hard, same, different, easy · too · but, an |
| 2 | young, slow, heavy, light, dry, full, loud, quiet, high, low |

## How to use them

### 1. The pairs

New words from this topic in **bold**; the rest are from earlier topics.

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| big ↔ **small** | long ↔ **short** | tall ↔ **short** | old ↔ **new** |
| old ↔ **young** | **fast** ↔ **slow** | **heavy** ↔ **light** | soft ↔ **hard** |
| **easy** ↔ **hard** | wet ↔ **dry** | empty ↔ **full** | **loud** ↔ **quiet** |
| **high** ↔ **low** | **same** ↔ **different** | hot ↔ cold | clean ↔ dirty |

### 2. One word, two opposites

| word | about | opposite |
|---|---|---|
| short | length | long |
| short | people | tall |
| old | things | new |
| old | people | young |
| hard | touch | soft |
| hard | difficulty | easy |

### 3. `too` = more than you want

- *My bag is **too** heavy.* (I can't carry it.)
- *Too loud!*

### 4. `but` joins two different ideas

*Grandpa is old, **but** I am young.*

### 5. `a` or `an`

Use **an** before a vowel sound: *an old book*, but *a new book*.

### 6. `the same`

`same` almost always has **the**: *It's **the** same.* — ❌ It's same.

## Watch out

- **little** (Stage 0) and **small** mean the same size, but *little* sounds softer, often cute: *a little mouse*.

## Practice

1. My cup is ___. (empty ↔ ?)
2. Grandpa is old. The baby is ___.
3. ___ old book (a / an)
4. Fix it: *It's same.*

Answers: 1. full · 2. young · 3. an · 4. It's the same.
