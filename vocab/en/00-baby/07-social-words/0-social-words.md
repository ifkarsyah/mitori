# Social Words

These are the words that make you polite and get things done: *hi*, *please*, *thank you*, *more*, *again*.
A child uses them long before they can make full sentences.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | hi, hello, bye, yes, no, sorry, more, this, that · please, again, here, there, now |
| 2 | mine, wow · up, down |
| 1 | uh-oh, ouch · over, tight · sweetie |

Fixed phrases: **good morning, good night, thank you, excuse me, all done, good job, be careful, I love you**.

## How to use them

### 1. Hello and goodbye

| meeting | leaving |
|---|---|
| hi (casual) | bye / bye-bye (casual) |
| hello | |
| good morning | good night (before bed) |

`Good night` is only for leaving or going to bed — never for meeting someone.

### 2. The magic words

| say | when |
|---|---|
| **please** | asking: *Milk, please.* |
| **thank you** | receiving: *Thank you, mommy.* |
| **sorry** | you did something wrong: *Sorry, I fell.* |
| **excuse me** | before you interrupt or pass someone |

*No, thank you.* is the polite way to say no.

### 3. Near and far

| | near me | far from me |
|---|---|---|
| thing | **this** — *What is this?* | **that** — *I want that.* |
| place | **here** — *Come here.* | **there** — *Look over there.* |

### 4. Asking for more, saying done

- *More milk, please.* · *Again! Again!*
- *All done!* = finished.

### 5. `my` vs `mine`

- **my** + noun: *my ball*
- **mine** alone: *That's **mine**!*

## Watch out

- **sorry** vs **excuse me**: *sorry* after a problem, *excuse me* before one.
- **uh-oh** (something went wrong), **ouch** (pain), **wow** (surprise) are sounds, not sentences.

## Practice

1. Someone gives you a cookie. You say: ___.
2. It's bedtime. You say: Good ___. (morning / night)
3. The ball is far away. "I want ___ ball." (this / that)
4. Fix it: *That's my!*

Answers: 1. Thank you · 2. night · 3. that · 4. That's mine!
