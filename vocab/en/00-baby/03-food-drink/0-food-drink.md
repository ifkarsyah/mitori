# Food & Drink

Eating happens several times a day, so food words come early: *milk*, *water*, *Lunch is ready!*

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | food, water, breakfast, lunch, dinner, time |
| 2 | milk, juice, bread, rice, egg, apple, cake, meat, chicken, birthday |
| 1 | banana, orange, cookie, cheese, soup, snack, butter, candy |

Also: **eat, drink, do** (verbs) · **hot, ready, sweet, red, careful, yummy, yucky** (adjectives) · **with, for, mmm**.

## How to use them

### 1. Things you can count, and things you can't

| countable: *a* + plural `-s` | uncountable: no *a*, no `-s` |
|---|---|
| **an** apple, two apple**s** | milk |
| **an** egg, eggs | water, juice |
| **a** cookie, cookies | bread, rice |
| **a** banana, bananas | cheese, meat, butter |

- ✅ *I want water.* — ❌ I want a water.
- ✅ *I like eggs.* — ❌ I like egg.

### 2. Meals

Meals usually have no *a / the*:

- *Breakfast is ready.* · *Come eat dinner.* · *Chicken **for** dinner.*
- `time` makes a meal or activity *now*: *Lunch time! Snack time!*

### 3. Asking and offering

- Ask with **please**: *Apple juice, please.*
- Offer with **Do you want…?**: *Do you want a banana?*

### 4. How it tastes

*yummy* 😋 · *yucky* 🤢 · *sweet* (candy, cake) · *hot* (temperature)

## Watch out

- **chicken** is both the animal and the meat. As meat it's uncountable: *Chicken for dinner.*
- **orange** is a fruit here. The color *orange* is in Stage 1.
- **yummy / yucky** are children's words. Adults say *delicious* / *disgusting* (later stages).
- **Happy birthday!** is a fixed greeting.

## Practice

1. I want ___ water. (a / — )
2. Do you want ___ apple? (a / an)
3. I like ___. (egg / eggs)
4. Fix it: *I drink a milk.*

Answers: 1. — (nothing) · 2. an · 3. eggs · 4. I drink milk.
