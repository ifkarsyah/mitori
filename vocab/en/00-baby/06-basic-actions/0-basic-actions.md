# Basic Actions

Most of what a baby hears is instructions: *Come here. Sit down. Don't run!*
That makes verbs the backbone of early speech.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | go, come, sleep, wake up, walk, play, look, see, give, take, have, open, wait, stop, read, can |
| 2 | sit, stand, run, fall, hear, listen, touch, hold, close, wash, put on, take off, sing |
| 1 | jump, push, pull, throw, catch, brush, draw, clap, wave |

Also: **dog, coat, song, button, rope** (nouns) · **at**.

## How to use them

### 1. Telling someone what to do

Use the verb on its own. Add **Don't** to stop someone:

- *Sit down.* · *Wait for me.* · *Clap your hands.*
- *Don't run!* · *Don't touch that.*

### 2. Opposite pairs

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| go ↔ come | give ↔ take | open ↔ close |
| push ↔ pull | sit ↔ stand | put on ↔ take off |

- **go** = away from here · **come** = toward here: *Come here!*
- **give** = from me to you · **take** = from you to me: *Give it to me. Take this.*

### 3. `look` vs `see`, `listen` vs `hear`

| you try | it just happens |
|---|---|
| **look at** — *Look at me.* | **see** — *I see a dog.* |
| **listen to** — *Listen to mommy.* | **hear** — *Can you hear it?* |

### 4. `can`

**can** + verb = able to: *I can jump.* · *Can you hear it?*
No `-s`, no `to`: ✅ *He can run.* — ❌ He cans run. ❌ He can to run.

## Watch out

- **fall** is irregular in the past: *it **fell***.
- **wake up / stand up / sit down** — the small word matters: *Stand up* ≠ *Stand*.
- **have** for owning: *I have a ball.* · *She **has** a ball.*

## Practice

1. ___ here! (Go / Come)
2. ___ at me. (Look / See)
3. Can you ___ the dog? It's loud. (hear / listen)
4. Fix it: *He can to jump.*

Answers: 1. Come · 2. Look · 3. hear · 4. He can jump.
