# Home Objects

A baby's world is the home: the bed, the door, the cup, the toys on the floor.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | home, house, room, door, bed, book, phone |
| 2 | window, chair, table, floor, light, cup, bottle, bath, toilet, toy, ball, TV, shoe, key, box, bag |
| 1 | plate, spoon, bowl, towel, diaper, blanket, pillow, teddy bear |

Also: **use, change, turn on, turn off, pick up** (verbs) · **empty, warm** (adjectives) · **not, out** · **let's, our, in, of**.

## How to use them

### 1. `in` or `on`

- **in** = inside something: *Put it **in** the box.*
- **on** = on top of a surface: *Put it **on** the table.* · *Sit **on** the chair.*

### 2. Two-word verbs

Some verbs are a verb + a small word, and they mean something new:

| verb | example |
|---|---|
| turn on | *Turn on the light.* |
| turn off | *Turn off the TV.* |
| pick up | *Pick up your toys.* |

The small word can also go after `it`: *Turn **it** off.* · *Pick **it** up.*

### 3. `home` vs `house`

- **house** = the building: *This is our house.*
- **home** = where you live. *Go home* has no *to*: ✅ *Let's go home.* — ❌ Let's go to home.

### 4. `Let's` and `Don't`

- **Let's** + verb = "we will do it together": *Let's read a book.*
- **Don't** + verb = "do not": *Don't sit on the floor.*

## Watch out

- **of** connects a container and what's in it: *a bowl **of** rice*.
- **light** here is the lamp (noun). *light* = not heavy is in Stage 1.
- **US vs UK**: diaper / nappy.

## Practice

1. Put the cup ___ the table. (in / on)
2. Turn ___ the light. It's dark. (on / off)
3. Let's go ___. (home / to home)
4. Fix it: *Pick up it.*

Answers: 1. on · 2. on · 3. home · 4. Pick it up.
