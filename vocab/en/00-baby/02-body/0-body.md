# Body

Parents name body parts all day: *Wash your **hands**. Open your **mouth**. Close your **eyes**.*
The first ones to learn are the parts you use and talk about most.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | head, eye, ear, mouth, hand, leg, foot |
| 2 | body, hair, face, nose, tooth, arm, finger |
| 1 | tongue, neck, tummy, back, knee, toe, bottom, skin |

Also: **hurt, show, put, lie** (verbs) · **long, little, soft** (adjectives) · **where** · **on, one, two, ten**.

## How to use them

### 1. Say whose body part it is

English uses **my / your** with body parts, where many languages use *the*:

- ✅ *Wash **your** hands.* — ❌ Wash the hands.
- ✅ ***My** leg hurts.* — ❌ The leg hurts.

### 2. Two of the same

Most body parts come in pairs, so you'll often hear the plural:

| one | two | |
|---|---|---|
| eye | eyes | add `-s` |
| hand | hands | add `-s` |
| tooth | **teeth** | irregular |
| foot | **feet** | irregular |

*I have **two** ears. Ten little toes.*

### 3. When something hurts

`hurt` is a verb here, and it takes `-s` after *my leg / it*:

- *My tummy **hurts**.* · *It **hurts**!*

### 4. Asking where

- *Where **is** your nose?* (one)
- *Where **are** your eyes?* (two)

## Watch out

- **hair** is one mass, not countable: *Your hair **is** long.* — ❌ Your hairs are long.
- **back** here is the body part. *Come back* (return) is a different word, in Stage 1.
- **tummy** is a child's word. Adults say *stomach* (later stage).

## Practice

1. Brush your ___. (tooth / teeth)
2. My knee ___. (hurt / hurts)
3. Where ___ your ears? (is / are)
4. Fix it: *Wash the hands.*

Answers: 1. teeth · 2. hurts · 3. are · 4. Wash your hands.
