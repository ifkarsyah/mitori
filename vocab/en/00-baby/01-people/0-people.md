# People

The first words a child hears are the people around them: *mom*, *dad*, *baby*.
Then come the small words that point at people: *I*, *you*, *he*, *she*.

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | mom, dad, baby, family, boy, girl, man, woman, friend, people, name |
| 2 | brother, sister, grandma, grandpa |
| 1 | mommy, daddy |

Also: **be** (verb) · **big, nice, funny, tall** (adjectives) · **I, me, you, he, she, we, they, my, your, it, a, the, what, to, many**.

## How to use them

### 1. Pointing at people

| person | doing the action | it's mine/yours |
|---|---|---|
| me | **I** am hungry. | **my** cup |
| you | **You** are my friend. | **your** shoes |
| a boy / man | **He** is my dad. | |
| a girl / woman | **She** is my mom. | |
| me + others | **We** go home. | |
| other people | **They** are playing. | |

When someone does something *to* you, `I` becomes **me**: *Give it **to me**.* — ❌ Give it to I.

### 2. The verb `be`

`be` changes with the person:

| | full | short |
|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** | I'm |
| you / we / they | you **are** | you're |
| he / she / it | he **is** | he's |

*My brother **is** big.* · *We **are** a family.*

### 3. `a` or `the`

- **a** = any one, the first time you mention it: *She has **a** ball.*
- **the** = that one, we both know which: ***The** baby is sleeping.*

### 4. Names

*What is your name?* → *My name is Ana.*

## Watch out

- **people** is already plural: *Many people **are** here.* — ❌ peoples, ❌ people is.
- **mommy / daddy** are what small children say. Adults say **mom / dad**.
- **US vs UK**: mom / mum, mommy / mummy. This list uses US spelling.

## Practice

1. ___ am hungry. (I / me)
2. Give it to ___. (I / me)
3. My sister ___ tall. (am / is / are)
4. Fix it: *Many people is here.*

Answers: 1. I · 2. me · 3. is · 4. Many people are here.
