# Needs & Feelings

Before a child can talk about the world, they need to say how they are: *I'm hungry. I'm tired. I want milk.*

## Words

| importance | words |
|---|---|
| 3 | hungry, tired, happy, sick, cold, okay, good, bad · love, like, want, need, feel · help |
| 2 | thirsty, sad, angry, scared, hurt, wet, dirty, clean · cry, laugh, smile · photo, shirt |
| 1 | sleepy · hug, kiss |

Also: **why**.

## How to use them

### 1. Feelings use `be`, not `have`

- ✅ *I**'m** hungry.* — ❌ I have hungry.
- ✅ *Are you tired?* — ❌ Do you have tired?

| I'm | You're | He's / She's |
|---|---|---|
| I'm cold. | You're clean. | She's sad. |

### 2. Want, need, like, love

All four take a thing right after them:

| | example | strength |
|---|---|---|
| like | *I like it.* | 🙂 |
| love | *I love you.* | 😍 |
| want | *I want milk.* | wish |
| need | *I need help.* | must have |

### 3. `feel` and `look`

- **feel** = how you are inside: *I feel sick.*
- **look** = how you seem to others: *You look tired.*

### 4. Asking why

*Why are you sad?* · *Why are you crying?*

## Watch out

- **hurt** as a feeling is an adjective: *Are you hurt?* As a verb it's *My leg hurts* (Body).
- **hug / kiss** are nouns here: *Give me **a** hug.*
- **okay** is very common in speech: *Are you okay? — I'm okay.*

## Practice

1. I ___ hungry. (have / am)
2. You look ___. (tired / tiredly)
3. I ___ help! (need / am)
4. Fix it: *I have cold.*

Answers: 1. am · 2. tired · 3. need · 4. I'm cold.
