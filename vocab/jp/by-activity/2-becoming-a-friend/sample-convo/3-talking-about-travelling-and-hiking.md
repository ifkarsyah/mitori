# Talking about travelling and hiking

*At a café with まい after the Saturday run.*

## The Conversation

### Part 1: Summer Holiday

**まい:** 夏休み、どこか行った？
*Did you go anywhere for the summer holiday?*

**私:** うん、北海道に行った。
*Yeah, I went to Hokkaido.*

**まい:** いいなあ！どうだった？
*Lucky! How was it?*

**私:** すごく楽しかった。ご飯がおいしかった。特に、ラーメン。
*It was really fun. The food was delicious. Especially the ramen.*

**まい:** 札幌のラーメン、おいしいよね。何日ぐらい？
*Sapporo ramen is so good. How many days?*

**私:** 五日。でも、短かった。
*Five days. But it was short.*

**まい:** インドネシアも行ってみたいな。
*I'd like to go to Indonesia too.*

**私:** ぜひ！バリはきれいだよ。
*You should! Bali is beautiful.*

### Part 2: Hiking

**まい:** 山は好き？私、よく山登りに行くんだ。
*Do you like mountains? I often go hiking.*

**私:** 「やまのぼり」…hiking？
*"Yamanobori"... hiking?*

**まい:** そうそう、hiking。
*Yes, hiking.*

**私:** 好き！でも、日本の山はまだ行ったことない。
*I like it! But I haven't been to a Japanese mountain yet.*

**まい:** じゃあ、高尾山がいいよ。東京から電車で一時間ぐらいで、初めての人でも簡単。
*Then Mt. Takao is good. It's about an hour by train from Tokyo, and easy even for first-timers.*

**私:** 一時間！近いね。
*An hour! That's close.*

**まい:** 紅葉の季節は、すごくきれいだよ。ちょっと混むけど。
*In autumn-leaves season it's really beautiful. It gets a bit crowded, though.*

### Part 3: Making a Plan

**私:** いつ行く？
*When shall we go?*

**まい:** 11月はどう？
*How about November?*

**私:** いいね。何を持って行けばいい？
*Sounds good. What should I bring?*

**まい:** 水と、お弁当と、歩きやすい靴。
*Water, a packed lunch, and shoes that are easy to walk in.*

**私:** わかった。楽しみ！
*Got it. Can't wait!*

## To Learn

### Template Phrases

- [holiday]、どこか行った？ → うん、[place]に行った。: Did you go anywhere for [holiday]? → Yeah, I went to [place].
- どうだった？ → すごく楽しかった。: How was it? → It was really fun.
- 特に、[thing]。: Especially [thing].
- [place]も行ってみたいな。: I'd like to try going to [place] too.
- まだ行ったことない。: I haven't been yet.
- [place]から電車で[time]ぐらい。: About [time] by train from [place].
- いつ行く？ → [month]はどう？: When shall we go? → How about [month]?
- 何を持って行けばいい？: What should I bring?

### Grammar Note

- **どこか**: "somewhere". どこ (where) + か. 何か = something, 誰か = someone.
- **～てみたい**: "want to try ~". 行ってみたい.
- **で for transport**: 電車で = by train, バスで = by bus.
- **～でも**: "even ~". 初めての人でも簡単 = easy even for first-timers.
- **～ばいい？**: "should I ~?". 持って行けばいい？ = what should I bring?
- **～やすい**: "easy to ~". 歩きやすい靴 = shoes easy to walk in. Add to the ます-stem.
