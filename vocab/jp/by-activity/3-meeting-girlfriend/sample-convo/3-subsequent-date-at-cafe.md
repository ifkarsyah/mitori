# Date at a café: dropping keigo

*A few dates in. あや asks to stop using です/ます, so from here on it's casual and 俺.*

## The Conversation

### Part 1: Meeting

**あや:** お待たせ！
*Sorry I'm late!*

**私:** ううん、俺も今来たところ。
*No, I just got here too.*

**あや:** 今日も寒いね。
*It's cold again today.*

**私:** うん。温かいもの飲もう。
*Yeah. Let's get something warm to drink.*

### Part 2: Switching to Casual

**あや:** ねえ、そろそろ敬語やめない？
*Hey, shall we stop using keigo now?*

**私:** 「けいご」…です、ます？
*"Keigo"... desu, masu?*

**あや:** そうそう。もっと普通に話したいな。
*Yes. I want to talk more normally.*

**私:** わかった。じゃあ、あやって呼んでもいい？
*OK. Then can I call you Aya?*

**あや:** うん、いいよ。
*Yeah, sure.*

### Part 3: Weekend Plans

**あや:** 週末、何してる？
*What are you doing this weekend?*

**私:** 特に予定はないよ。
*No plans in particular.*

**あや:** じゃあ、映画見に行かない？見たい映画があるの。
*Then want to go see a movie? There's one I want to see.*

**私:** いいね、何の映画？
*Sure, what movie?*

**あや:** 恋愛映画。フェルディは、恋愛映画大丈夫？
*A romance movie. Are you OK with romance movies?*

**私:** うん、大丈夫。日本語、ちょっと難しいかもしれないけど。
*Yeah, fine. The Japanese might be a bit hard, though.*

**あや:** 字幕あるところ探すね。
*I'll look for a place with subtitles.*

**私:** ありがとう。優しいね。
*Thanks. That's sweet of you.*

## To Learn

### Template Phrases

- お待たせ！ → ううん、俺も今来たところ。: Sorry to keep you waiting! → No, I just got here too.
- [thing]飲もう。: Let's drink [thing].
- [name]って呼んでもいい？: Can I call you [name]?
- 週末、何してる？ → 特に予定はないよ。: What are you doing this weekend? → No plans in particular.
- [thing]見に行かない？: Want to go see [thing]?
- 何の[thing]？: What [thing]?
- [thing]、ちょっと難しいかもしれないけど。: [thing] might be a bit hard, though.
- ありがとう。優しいね。: Thanks. That's sweet of you.

### Grammar Note

- **敬語やめない？**: The moment you switch to casual. After this, drop さん and です/ます.
- **～って呼んでもいい？**: "Can I call you ~?". ～って is casual と.
- **～かもしれない**: "might ~". 難しいかもしれない = it might be hard.
- **～けど at the end**: Softens the sentence, like "..., though".
- **～の (explaining)**: 見たい映画があるの = there's a movie I want to see (that's why). Common in women's casual speech, and fine for anyone.
