# Dinner date: asking her out

*At an izakaya. You ask あや to be your girlfriend.*

## The Conversation

### Part 1: Ordering

**私:** 何食べたい？
*What do you want to eat?*

**あや:** 焼き鳥！あと、唐揚げも。
*Yakitori! And karaage too.*

**私:** いいね。飲み物は？
*Nice. Drinks?*

**あや:** レモンサワーにしようかな。
*I think I'll have a lemon sour.*

**私:** 俺はビール。すみません、注文お願いします！
*Beer for me. Excuse me, we'd like to order!*

### Part 2: Talking

**あや:** フェルディと話してると、いつも楽しい。
*It's always fun talking with you.*

**私:** 俺も。あやといると、すごく落ち着く。
*Me too. I feel really relaxed when I'm with you.*

**あや:** ほんと？嬉しい。
*Really? That makes me happy.*

### Part 3: Asking Her Out

**私:** あの…あや、ちょっと話があるんだけど。
*Um... Aya, there's something I want to talk about.*

**あや:** え、何？
*Huh, what?*

**私:** 俺、あやのことが好きです。付き合ってください。
*I like you, Aya. Please go out with me.*

**あや:** …私も、フェルディのこと好き。よろしくお願いします。
*...I like you too, Ferdi. Yes, please.*

**私:** 本当に？よかった…！
*Really? Thank goodness...!*

**あや:** 緊張してたでしょ？
*You were nervous, weren't you?*

**私:** うん、すごく緊張した。
*Yeah, really nervous.*

## To Learn

### Template Phrases

- 何食べたい？: What do you want to eat?
- 飲み物は？: Drinks?
- [drink]にしようかな。: I think I'll have [drink].
- すみません、注文お願いします！: Excuse me, we'd like to order!
- [name]と話してると、いつも楽しい。: It's always fun talking with [name].
- [name]といると、すごく落ち着く。: I feel really relaxed with [name].
- ちょっと話があるんだけど。: There's something I want to talk about.
- [name]のことが好きです。付き合ってください。: I like you, [name]. Please go out with me.

### Grammar Note

- **告白 (confession)**: In Japan, dating usually starts with a clear "付き合ってください". Before that, you're just going out as friends.
- **Switching back to です/ます**: 好きです and 付き合ってください are polite on purpose. It shows you're serious.
- **～のこと**: あやのことが好き = I like Aya (as a person). More natural than あやが好き here.
- **～と、～**: "when ~, ~". 話してると、楽しい = when I talk with you, it's fun.
- **～ようかな**: "I think I'll ~". しようかな.
- **～でしょ？**: "~, right?". 緊張してたでしょ？
