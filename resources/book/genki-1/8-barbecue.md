# Lesson 8 — バーベキュー Barbecue

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 186–207)
Story context: Michiko, Takeshi, Sue, and Robert (Ken can't make it) throw a barbecue, with Robert cooking and Mary arriving late — the lesson's real subject is register: shifting from the polite ます／です forms used so far to plain/casual speech.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `8-barbecue/grammar/`
- [[short-forms]] — the plain-form conjugation of verbs, adjectives, and です across present/negative (the paradigm underlying nearly all later grammar).
- [[informal-speech]] — when/how short forms replace polite forms in casual conversation, and the seniority dynamics involved.
- [[と思います-と言っていました]] — stating an opinion vs. reporting someone else's statement, both built on short-form quoting.
- [[ないでください]] — negative request ("please don't..."), a softer alternative to 〜てはいけません.
- [[verb-のが好きです]] — nominalizing a verb with の to make it the object of 好き／上手／下手 (an activity, not just a thing).
- [[が]] — presenting a subject as new information, and answering だれが／何が questions.
- [[何か-何も]] — "something" vs. "not... anything" (何＋か／も pattern, generalizable to どこか／だれか later).

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `8-barbecue/phrases/`: [[ざんねんですね]], [[かんぱい]], [[うん-ううん]], [[する-nominalization]] (irregular verbs hiding a free-standing noun).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `8-barbecue/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[な-adjectives]], [[u-verbs]], [[ru-verbs]], [[irregular-verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]].
