# Ru-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 起（お）きる | to get up |
| 食（た）べる | to eat（〜を） |
| 寝（ね）る | to sleep; to go to sleep |
| 見（み）る | to see; to look at; to watch（〜を） |

All four follow the regular ru-verb pattern in [[../grammar/verb-conjugation]] — just drop る and add ます／ません.
