# Places

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 家（いえ） | home; house |
| うち | home; house; my place |
| 学校（がっこう） | school |

うち and 家（いえ）overlap heavily in meaning; うち additionally carries a sense of "my (own) place/household" that いえ doesn't.
