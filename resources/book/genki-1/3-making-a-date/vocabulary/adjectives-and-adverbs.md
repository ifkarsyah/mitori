# Adjectives & Adverbs

## Adjectives
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| いい | good |
| 早（はや）い | early |

## Adverbs
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| あまり＋negative | not much |
| 全然（ぜんぜん）＋negative | not at all |
| たいてい | usually |
| ちょっと | a little |
| 時々（ときどき） | sometimes |
| よく | often; much |

See [[../grammar/frequency-adverbs]] for how these adverbs combine with affirmative/negative verb endings — あまり and ぜんぜん specifically require a negative ending.
