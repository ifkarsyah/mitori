# U-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 行（い）く | to go（destination に／へ） |
| 帰（かえ）る | to go back; to return（destination に／へ） |
| 聞（き）く | to listen; to hear（〜を） |
| 飲（の）む | to drink（〜を） |
| 話（はな）す | to speak; to talk（language を／で） |
| 読（よ）む | to read（〜を） |

帰る is the exceptional u-verb flagged in [[../grammar/verb-conjugation]] — it ends in る with え before it, but conjugates as an u-verb (帰ります, not ×帰えます).
