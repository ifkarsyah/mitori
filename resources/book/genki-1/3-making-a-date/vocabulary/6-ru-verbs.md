# Ru-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>起<rt>お</rt></ruby>きる | to get up |
| <ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べる | to eat（〜を） |
| <ruby>寝<rt>ね</rt></ruby>る | to sleep; to go to sleep |
| <ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>る | to see; to look at; to watch（〜を） |

All four follow the regular ru-verb pattern in [[../grammar/1-verb-conjugation]] — just drop る and add ます／ません.
