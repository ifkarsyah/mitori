# Adjectives & Adverbs

## Adjectives
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| いい | good |
| <ruby>早<rt>はや</rt></ruby>い | early |

## Adverbs
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| あまり＋negative | not much |
| <ruby>全然<rt>ぜんぜん</rt></ruby>＋negative | not at all |
| たいてい | usually |
| ちょっと | a little |
| <ruby>時々<rt>ときどき</rt></ruby> | sometimes |
| よく | often; much |

See [[../grammar/7-frequency-adverbs]] for how these adverbs combine with affirmative/negative verb endings — あまり and ぜんぜん specifically require a negative ending.
