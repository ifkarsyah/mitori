# そうですね

Opens Mary's answer to Takeshi's question about her weekend — functions as a thinking pause ("let me see...") as much as an agreement marker:

たけし：週末（しゅうまつ）はたいてい何（なに）をしますか。
メアリー：そうですね。たいていうちで勉強（べんきょう）します。

## Notes
Distinct from [[../../1-new-friends/phrases/そうですか|そうですか]] (Lesson 1, "I see"/"is that so") — そうですね is used both to agree with something just said and, as here, to buy a moment to think before answering.
