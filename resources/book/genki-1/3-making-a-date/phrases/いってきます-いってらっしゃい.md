# いってきます／いってらっしゃい

The fixed exchange when someone leaves home, from the host-family dialogue:

お母（かあ）さん：そうですか。じゃあ、いってらっしゃい。
Host mother: I see. Well, have a nice day (lit. "go and come back").

メアリー：いってきます。
Mary: Good-bye (lit. "I'll go and come back").

## Notes
Both phrases derive from 行（い）く／来（く）る — the person leaving says いってきます ("I'm going and I'll come back"), and the person staying replies いってらっしゃい ("go and come back safely"). Neither is used literally as a standalone verb form here; they function as a fixed departure ritual.
