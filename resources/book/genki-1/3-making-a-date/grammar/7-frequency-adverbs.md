# Frequency Adverbs

Add a frequency adverb to describe how often something happens: <ruby>毎日<rt>まいにち</rt></ruby>（every day）、よく（often）、ときどき（sometimes）, plus two that describe *in*frequency: ぜんぜん（never; not at all）、あまり（not often; not very much）.

私はときどき<ruby>喫茶店<rt>きっさてん</rt></ruby>に行きます。
I sometimes go to a coffee shop.

## ぜんぜん and あまり anticipate a negative ending
These two adverbs require the sentence to end in ません — they can't appear in an affirmative sentence.

私はぜんぜんテレビを<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>ません。
I do not watch TV at all.

たけしさんはあまり<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>しません。
Takeshi does not study much.
