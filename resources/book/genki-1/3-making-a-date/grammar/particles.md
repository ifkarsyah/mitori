# Particles（を・で・に・へ）

Four particles marking a noun's relationship to the verb.

## を — direct object
コーヒーを飲（の）みます。
I drink coffee.

音楽（おんがく）を聞（き）きます。
I listen to music.

## で — where the action happens
図書館（としょかん）で本（ほん）を読（よ）みます。
I will read books in the library.

## に — goal of movement, or point in time
**Goal of movement:**
私は今日学校（がっこう）に行きません。
I will not go to school today.

**Point in time:**
日曜日（にちようび）に京都（きょうと）に行きます。
I will go to Kyoto on Sunday.

十一時（じゅういちじ）に寝（ね）ます。
I will go to bed at eleven.

Approximate time: substitute ごろ or ごろに for に — 十一時ごろ（に）寝ます ("around eleven").

## へ — goal of movement (alternative to this use of に)
私は今日学校へ行きません。
(Same meaning as with に above — へ can only replace the goal-of-movement に, not に's time-marking use.)

## Notes
- In spoken language particles are often dropped entirely — covered later, in Lesson 15 (Genki II).
- で here only marks *location of an action*; later lessons introduce verbs that pair with different location particles.
