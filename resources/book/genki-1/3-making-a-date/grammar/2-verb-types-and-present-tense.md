# Verb Types and the "Present Tense"

The ます／ません forms from [[1-verb-conjugation]] double as both present and future meaning, depending on context.

## Habitual actions
<ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>はよくテレビを<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>ます。
I often watch TV.

メアリーさんはときどき<ruby>朝<rt>あさ</rt></ruby>ご<ruby>飯<rt>はん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べません。
Mary sometimes doesn't eat breakfast.

## Future actions
私は<ruby>明日<rt>あした</rt></ruby><ruby>京都<rt>きょうと</rt></ruby>に<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>きます。
I will go to Kyoto tomorrow.

スーさんは<ruby>今日<rt>きょう</rt></ruby>うちに<ruby>帰<rt>かえ</rt></ruby>りません。
Sue will not return home today.

## Notes
- There is no separate future-tense conjugation in Japanese for this class of verbs — the same ます／ません form covers both "does regularly" and "will do," disambiguated only by time words or context.
