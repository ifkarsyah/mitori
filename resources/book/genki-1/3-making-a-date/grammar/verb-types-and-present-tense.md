# Verb Types and the "Present Tense"

The ます／ません forms from [[verb-conjugation]] double as both present and future meaning, depending on context.

## Habitual actions
私（わたし）はよくテレビを見（み）ます。
I often watch TV.

メアリーさんはときどき朝（あさ）ご飯（はん）を食（た）べません。
Mary sometimes doesn't eat breakfast.

## Future actions
私はあした京都（きょうと）に行（い）きます。
I will go to Kyoto tomorrow.

スーさんは今日（きょう）うちに帰（かえ）りません。
Sue will not return home today.

## Notes
- There is no separate future-tense conjugation in Japanese for this class of verbs — the same ます／ません form covers both "does regularly" and "will do," disambiguated only by time words or context.
