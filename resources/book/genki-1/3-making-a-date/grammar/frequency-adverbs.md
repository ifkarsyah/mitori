# Frequency Adverbs

Add a frequency adverb to describe how often something happens: 毎日（まいにち, every day）、よく（often）、ときどき（sometimes）, plus two that describe *in*frequency: ぜんぜん（never; not at all）、あまり（not often; not very much）.

私はときどき喫茶店（きっさてん）に行きます。
I sometimes go to a coffee shop.

## ぜんぜん and あまり anticipate a negative ending
These two adverbs require the sentence to end in ません — they can't appear in an affirmative sentence.

私はぜんぜんテレビを見（み）ません。
I do not watch TV at all.

たけしさんはあまり勉強（べんきょう）しません。
Takeshi does not study much.
