# 〜ませんか

The present-tense negative question form doubles as a polite invitation ("won't you...?"). Its affirmative counterpart 〜ますか cannot be used this way — 昼（ひる）ご飯（はん）を食（た）べますか can only be a plain question, never an invitation.

昼ご飯を食べませんか。
What do you say to having lunch with me?

いいですね。
Sounds great.

テニスをしませんか。
Will you play tennis with me?

うーん、ちょっと。
Um, it's slightly (inconvenient for me at this moment). — see [[../phrases/ちょっと]]
