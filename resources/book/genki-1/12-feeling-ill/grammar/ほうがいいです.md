# 〜ほうがいいです

"It is better (for you) to do..." — a sentence-final expression for giving advice. Suggesting an activity with ほうがいいです is a specific piece of advice: it's advisable to follow it, and there's a danger/problem if you don't.

## Different tense depending on polarity
**Affirmative advice** → past-tense short form of the verb:
もっと野菜（やさい）を食（た）べたほうがいいですよ。
You'd better eat more vegetables.

**Negative advice** → present-tense short form of the verb:
授業（じゅぎょう）を休（やす）まないほうがいいですよ。
It is better not to skip classes.

## Notes
This asymmetric tense behavior is a quirk specific to this construction — contrast with [[../../9-kabuki/grammar/past-tense-short-forms]] and [[../../10-winter-vacation-plans/grammar/つもりだ]], where the present/past choice tracks real time reference rather than affirmative/negative polarity.
