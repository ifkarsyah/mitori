# 〜すぎる

すぎる ("too much," "to excess") attaches to a verb stem, conjugating as a regular ru-verb.

早（はや）く起（お）きすぎました。
I got up too early.

食（た）べすぎてはいけません。
You must not eat too much.

## With adjectives — drop the final い／な
すぎる can also follow the "base" of an い- or な-adjective (the part that doesn't change across conjugations):

（高（たか）い）　この本（ほん）は高すぎます。
This book is too expensive.

（静（しず）かな）　この町（まち）は静かすぎます。
This town is too quiet.

## Notes
すぎる signals that something is beyond normal/proper — implying the speaker does *not* welcome it. 親切（しんせつ）すぎます ("too kind") is therefore not a straightforward compliment, unlike とても親切です／すごく親切です — use とても／すごく instead if you just mean "very," with no negative implication.
