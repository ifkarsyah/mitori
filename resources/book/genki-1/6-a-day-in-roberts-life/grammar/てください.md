# 〜てください

Te-form + ください makes a polite request — "please do... for me."

教科書（きょうかしょ）を読（よ）んでください。
Please read the textbook.

すみません。ちょっと教（おし）えてください。
Excuse me. Please teach me a little. (= Tell me, I need your advice.)

## Notes
Among very close friends or family, a bare te-form (no ください) can itself function as a request:
窓（まど）を開（あ）けて。
Open the window, will you?
