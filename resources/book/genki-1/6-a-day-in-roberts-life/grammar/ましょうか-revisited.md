# 〜ましょうか (revisited)

[[../../5-a-trip-to-okinawa/grammar/ましょう-ましょうか]] covered ましょうか as "shall we...?" This lesson adds a second sense: **offering to do something for someone** ("let me do...").

（私（わたし）が）やりましょうか。
I'll do it. (offering help with something someone is struggling with)

荷物（にもつ）を持（も）ちましょうか。
Shall I carry your bag?

## Notes
Both senses use the identical construction — context (an invitation to do something together vs. an offer of help) is what distinguishes them.
