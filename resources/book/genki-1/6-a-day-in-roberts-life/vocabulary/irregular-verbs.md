# Irregular Verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 連（つ）れてくる | to bring (a person)（〜を） |
| 持（も）ってくる | to bring (a thing)（〜を） |

Both are compounds built on 来（く）る ("to come") — 連れる／持つ + てくる, and both conjugate irregularly the same way 来る does.
