# お〜 (politeness prefix)

Many words beginning with お can also be used without it — お adds smoothness and a nuance of social refinement, without changing the word's core meaning.

お酒（さけ）、お金（かね）、お風呂（ふろ）、お祭（まつ）り (festival)

## Notes
This isn't a rule for attaching お to any noun freely — it's a fixed set of words where the お-prefixed form is simply the more polished/common variant. Compare with おみやげ ([[../../4-the-first-date/vocabulary/people-and-things]]) and おなまえ ([[../../2-shopping/phrases/どうぞ]]), which work the same way.
