# <ruby>遅<rt>おそ</rt></ruby>くvs <ruby>遅<rt>おそ</rt></ruby>い

Both mean "late," but 遅い is an **adjective** (modifies nouns, or stands as a predicate) while 遅く is an **adverb** (modifies verbs).

A：<ruby>昨日<rt>きのう</rt></ruby><ruby>一時<rt>いちじ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>寝<rt>ね</rt></ruby>ました。
I went to bed at one o'clock yesterday.
B：遅いですね。
That's late.

<ruby>週末<rt>しゅうまつ</rt></ruby>には、<ruby>十時<rt>じゅうじ</rt></ruby>ごろ<ruby>起<rt>お</rt></ruby>きて、遅い<ruby>朝<rt>あさ</rt></ruby>ご飯を<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べます。
On weekends, I get up around 10:00 and eat a late breakfast. (遅い modifies the noun 朝ご飯)

<ruby>昨日<rt>きのう</rt></ruby>、遅く寝ました。
I went to bed late yesterday. (遅く modifies the verb 寝ました)

## Notes
The same い-adjective／adverb pattern applies to <ruby>早<rt>はや</rt></ruby>く／早い (early) — see [[../../1-new-friends/vocabulary/3-school-and-affiliation|早い]] from Lesson 1's vocabulary.
