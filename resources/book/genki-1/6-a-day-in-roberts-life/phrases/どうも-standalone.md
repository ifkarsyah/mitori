# どうも (as a standalone word)

Builds on [[../../2-shopping/phrases/どうも]] (Lesson 2's "casual thanks"). Expression Notes 7 explains どうも more fully:

どうも normally pairs with ありがとう (どうもありがとう, "thank you very much") or with すみません (どうもすみません, "I am very sorry" / "thank you very much"). Used **alone**, it's simply an abbreviation of one of those — a short way to show gratitude or regret without a full sentence. Some speakers also use どうも on its own as an informal "hello" or "good-bye."
