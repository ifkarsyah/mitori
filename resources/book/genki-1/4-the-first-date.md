# Lesson 4 — 初めてのデート The First Date

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 102–127)
Story context: Mary asks a stranger for directions downtown, then goes to her host family's alone after Takeshi fails to show — it turns out he waited an hour at the wrong burger place.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/grammar/`
- [[1-xがあります-います]] — existence verbs, inanimate (ある) vs. animate (いる).
- [[2-describing-where-things-are]] — location nouns (上/下/中/前/後ろ etc.) combined with の.
- [[3-past-tense-of-です]] — でした／じゃなかったです.
- [[4-past-tense-of-verbs]] — polite past ました／ませんでした.
- [[5-も-revisited]] — extended to actions, two things, two occasions.
- [[6-一時間]] — duration-of-time expressions (no particle needed).
- [[7-たくさん]] — quantity word, flexible placement before or after を.
- [[8-と]] — connecting nouns ("and"), and the "with (someone)" companion particle.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/phrases/`: [[1-ただいま-おかえりなさい]], [[2-どうして-だから]], [[3-えっ-あっ]], [[4-ごめんなさい]].

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/vocabulary/`: [[1-activities]], [[2-people-and-things]], [[3-places]], [[4-time-and-calendar]], [[5-verbs]], [[6-adverbs-and-expressions]], [[7-location-words]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の祝日 — Japanese National Holidays (p. 114).

**Useful Expressions:** 日・週・月・年 — Days/Weeks/Months/Years (p. 127) — folded into [[4-time-and-calendar]] above.
