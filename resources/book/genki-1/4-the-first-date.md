# Lesson 4 — 初めてのデート The First Date

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 102–127)
Story context: Mary asks a stranger for directions downtown, then goes to her host family's alone after Takeshi fails to show — it turns out he waited an hour at the wrong burger place.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/grammar/`
- [[xがあります-います]] — existence verbs, inanimate (ある) vs. animate (いる).
- [[describing-where-things-are]] — location nouns (上/下/中/前/後ろ etc.) combined with の.
- [[past-tense-of-です]] — でした／じゃなかったです.
- [[past-tense-of-verbs]] — polite past ました／ませんでした.
- [[も-revisited]] — extended to actions, two things, two occasions.
- [[一時間]] — duration-of-time expressions (no particle needed).
- [[たくさん]] — quantity word, flexible placement before or after を.
- [[と]] — connecting nouns ("and"), and the "with (someone)" companion particle.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/phrases/`: [[ただいま-おかえりなさい]], [[どうして-だから]], [[えっ-あっ]], [[ごめんなさい]].

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `4-the-first-date/vocabulary/`: [[activities]], [[people-and-things]], [[places]], [[time-and-calendar]], [[verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[location-words]].

**Useful Expressions:** 日・週・月・年 — Days/Weeks/Months/Years (p. 127) — folded into [[time-and-calendar]] above.
