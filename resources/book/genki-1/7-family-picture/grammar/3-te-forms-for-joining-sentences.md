# Te-forms for Joining Sentences

[[../../6-a-day-in-roberts-life/grammar/1-te-form]] covered verbal te-forms joining sentences. い-adjectives, な-adjectives, and noun+です also have their own te-forms for combining two elements into one longer sentence.

## Formation
| Type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| い-adjective | swap final い for くて | <ruby>安<rt>やす</rt></ruby>い　→　安くて |
| いい (irregular) | | いい　→　よくて |
| な-adjective | add で to the base | <ruby>元気<rt>げんき</rt></ruby>（な）　→　元気で |
| noun ＋ です | add で to the noun | <ruby>日本人<rt>にほんじん</rt></ruby>です　→　日本人で |

あの<ruby>店<rt>みせ</rt></ruby>の<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べ<ruby>物<rt>もの</rt></ruby>は安くて、おいしいです。
The food at that restaurant is inexpensive and delicious.

ホテルはきれいで、よかったです。
The hotel was clean, and we were happy.

<ruby>山下先生<rt>やましたせんせい</rt></ruby>は日本人で、<ruby>五十歳<rt>ごじっさい</rt></ruby>ぐらいです。
Professor Yamashita is a Japanese and he is about fifty years old.

## Notes
Functionally parallel to the verbal te-form from Lesson 6 — same "and" role, different conjugation rule depending on part of speech.
