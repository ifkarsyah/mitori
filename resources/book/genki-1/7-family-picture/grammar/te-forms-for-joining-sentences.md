# Te-forms for Joining Sentences

[[../../6-a-day-in-roberts-life/grammar/te-form]] covered verbal te-forms joining sentences. い-adjectives, な-adjectives, and noun+です also have their own te-forms for combining two elements into one longer sentence.

## Formation
| Type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| い-adjective | swap final い for くて | 安（やす）い　→　安くて |
| いい (irregular) | | いい　→　よくて |
| な-adjective | add で to the base | 元気（げんき）（な）　→　元気で |
| noun ＋ です | add で to the noun | 日本人（にほんじん）です　→　日本人で |

あの店（みせ）の食（た）べ物（もの）は安くて、おいしいです。
The food at that restaurant is inexpensive and delicious.

ホテルはきれいで、よかったです。
The hotel was clean, and we were happy.

山下先生（やましたせんせい）は日本人で、五十歳（ごじっさい）ぐらいです。
Professor Yamashita is a Japanese and he is about fifty years old.

## Notes
Functionally parallel to the verbal te-form from Lesson 6 — same "and" role, different conjugation rule depending on part of speech.
