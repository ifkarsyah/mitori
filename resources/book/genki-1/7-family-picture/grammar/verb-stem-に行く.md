# verb stem + に行く

Describes movement to another place **in order to** do something.

destination（に／へ） ＋ purpose-of-movement-phrase（stem form）に ＋ 行く／来る／帰る

The purpose phrase (a verb + its object, etc.) must be in **stem** form — the part left after removing ます from the present-tense long form (see [[../../3-making-a-date/grammar/verb-conjugation]]).

デパートに　かばんを買（か）い　に行きました。
I went to a department store to buy a bag.

メアリーさんは日本（にほん）に　日本語を勉強（べんきょう）し　に来（き）ました。
Mary has come to Japan to study Japanese.

## Notes
A bare noun like 買（か）い物（もの） ("shopping") can also fill the purpose slot: デパートに買い物に行きました。("I went to a department store for shopping.")
