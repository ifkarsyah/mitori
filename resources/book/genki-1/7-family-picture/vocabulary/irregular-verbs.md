# Irregular Verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 結婚（けっこん）する | to get married（〜と） |

The textbook example verb for [[../grammar/ている]]'s "result of a change" reading — 結婚しています means "is married," not "is getting married."
