# Family & People

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 姉（あね） | (my) older sister |
| 妹（いもうと） | younger sister |
| おじいさん | grandfather; old man |

| 男（おとこ）の人（ひと） | man |
| お兄（にい）さん | older brother |
| お姉（ねえ）さん | older sister |
| おばあさん | grandmother; old woman |
| 女（おんな）の人（ひと） | woman |
| 家族（かぞく） | family |
| 兄弟（きょうだい） | brothers and sisters |

| 母（はは） | (my) mother |

## Notes
This lesson's family vocabulary splits into "referring to my own family" (姉／弟／父／母, etc.) vs. "referring to someone else's family or addressing your own" (お姉さん／お父さん, etc.) — the full three-way system (referring to others' families / referring to your own formally vs. casually / addressing your own family directly) is this lesson's Culture Note, 家族の呼び方 (Kinship Terms, p. 184).
