# Lesson 11 — 休みのあと After the Vacation

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 250–265)
Story context: Michiko and Mary catch up after the break, then Mary introduces Michiko to her friend John, a new arrival from Australia — grammar focuses on wants and listing example activities.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `11-after-the-vacation/grammar/`
- [[たい]] — expressing desire ("want to do X"), conjugating like an い-adjective, plus たがっている for observing that someone *else* wants something.
- [[たりたりする]] — listing a few representative actions out of a larger set ("do things like X and Y"), vs. plain て-joining which implies an exhaustive, ordered list.
- [[ことがある]] — expressing past experience ("have done X before").
- [[noun-a-や-noun-b]] — the non-exhaustive "and" for nouns (contrast with と from Lesson 4, which lists everything).

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `11-after-the-vacation/phrases/`: [[ひさしぶり-まあまあ]], [[こちら-紹介します]] (introducing someone), [[は-だけ-に-in-negatives]] (は in negative sentences, だけ, occasion/role に), [[ドライブ-夢-には]] (ドライブ, 夢, には vs に).
