# （〜を）おねがいします／（〜を）ください

Two ways to request an item, both attaching to 〜を + noun.

じゃあ、そのとけいを　ください。
Then, I'll take that watch.

じゃあ、これを　おねがいします。
Then, I'll have this.

## Notes (Expression Notes 3, p. 67)
- （〜を）ください — "Please give me X." Used for requesting concrete items in general.
- （〜を）おねがいします — also a request for X, but sounds slightly more upscale when asking for a concrete object; commonly heard when ordering food at a restaurant ("I will have..."). It can also be used to request "abstract objects," like repairs, explanations, or understanding — a range くださいdoesn't cover.
