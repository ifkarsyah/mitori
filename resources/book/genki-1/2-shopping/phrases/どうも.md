# どうも

A shorter, more casual "thanks" than [[../../1-new-friends/phrases/ありがとうございます|ありがとうございます]] — used here as Mary's reply after being handed the menu.

メアリー：どうも。これは　なんですか。
Mary: Thanks. What is this?
