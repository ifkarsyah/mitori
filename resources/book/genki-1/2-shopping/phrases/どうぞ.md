# どうぞ

Used when offering item X to someone.

いらっしゃいませ。メニューを　どうぞ。
Welcome. Here's the menu.

## Notes (Expression Notes 3, p. 67)
Also used when waiting for someone to come forward with item X — e.g. a phone operator asking for your name would say おなまえを　どうぞ (お is a politeness marker; おなまえ is "your honorable name").
