# どうも

A shorter, more casual "thanks" than [[../../1-new-friends/phrases/2-ありがとうございます|ありがとうございます]] — used here as Mary's reply after being handed the menu.

メアリー：どうも。これは　<ruby>何<rt>なん</rt></ruby>ですか。
Mary: Thanks. What is this?
