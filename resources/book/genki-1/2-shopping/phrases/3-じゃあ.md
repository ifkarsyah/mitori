# じゃあ

Transition word bridging from what was just established to a resulting decision or next statement.

たかいですね。じゃあ、あの<ruby>時計<rt>とけい</rt></ruby>は　いくらですか。
It's expensive. Well then, how much is that watch over there?

じゃあ、その<ruby>時計<rt>とけい</rt></ruby>を　ください。
Then, I'll take that watch.
