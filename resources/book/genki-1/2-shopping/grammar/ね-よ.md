# 〜ね／〜よ

Sentence-final tags that signal how the speaker views the interaction with the listener.

## ね — seeking agreement/confirmation
Added when the speaker wants the listener's confirmation ("right?"):

これは　にくじゃないですね。
This is not meat, is it?

## よ — asserting new information
Added when the speaker wants to assure the listener of something, especially information the listener might not already know. With よ, a statement reads as more emphatic/authoritative:

とんかつは　さかなじゃないですよ。
Let me assure you — tonkatsu is not fish.

すみすさんは　イギリスじんですよ。
(In case you're wondering,) Mr. Smith is British.

## Notes
- Both appear in this lesson's dialogue: たかいですね (mild agreement-seeking about price) and せんはっぴゃくえんですよ (the vendor asserting the actual price).
