# noun も

"Item A is this, and item B is this, too." も is a particle indicating that another item shares the property just stated about the first.

たけしさんは　にほんじんです。
Takeshi is a Japanese person.

みちこさんも　にほんじんです。
Michiko is Japanese, too.

## The pattern
| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | は | X | です。→ A is X. |
| B | も | X | です。→ B too is X. |

も **replaces** は — the two sentences above are otherwise identical in shape, but the second swaps は for も rather than adding "too" anywhere else in the sentence.

## Notes
- Placement matters: unlike English "too" (which can trail the whole sentence), も must directly follow the noun it's attached to — here, みちこさん.
- も cannot describe "in addition to being X, also Y" for the *same* item (e.g. "Pat is Japanese, but also American") — that needs a different construction (でも あります), not covered in this lesson.
- Two dialogue lines that end in です but can't be negated this way: あれも　たかいですね and おいしいですよ — たかい／おいしい are **adjectives**, not nouns, so [[noun-じゃないです]] doesn't apply to them. Adjective conjugation is Lesson 5.
