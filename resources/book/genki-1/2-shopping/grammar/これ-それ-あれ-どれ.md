# これ／それ／あれ／どれ

The demonstrative pronouns for things whose name you don't know — "this thing," "that thing," etc.

これは　いくらですか。
How much is this?

それは　さんぜんえんです。
That is 3,000 yen.

## The three-way distinction
- これ — close to the **speaker** ("this thing here")
- それ — close to the **listener** ("that thing near you")
- あれ — far from **both** speaker and listener ("that thing over there")

あれは　わたしの　ペンです。
That (over there) is my pen.

## どれ — "which one"
どれですか。
Which one is it (that you are talking to)?

## Notes
- どれ is a question word, and question words like どれ／なに cannot be followed by は — they take が instead: どれが　あなたの　ペンですか。("Which one is your pen?")
- This lesson doesn't explore どれ's full range because of that が complication; it's introduced just for basic use.
- The noun-modifying counterparts (この／その／あの／どの) are the next grammar point: [[この-その-あの-どの-noun]].
