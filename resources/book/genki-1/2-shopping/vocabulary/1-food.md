# Food

| <ruby>魚<rt>さかな</rt></ruby> | fish |

## Notes
おいしい appears in the dialogue as おいしいですよ ("It's delicious!") — an adjective, not a noun, so it can't be negated with [[../grammar/6-noun-じゃないです]] (see the note there).
