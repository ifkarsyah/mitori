# Lesson 10 — 冬休みの予定 Winter Vacation Plans

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 228–249)
Story context: Mary and Takeshi discuss winter break plans (Korea vs. Taiwan), then Mary books a flight to Seoul at a travel agency — grammar centers on comparison and stating intentions.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `10-winter-vacation-plans/grammar/`
- [[comparison-two-items]] — 〜のほうが〜です and どちらのほうが〜ですか.
- [[comparison-three-or-more]] — 〜の中で〜がいちばん〜です superlative pattern.
- [[adjective-noun-の]] — replacing a repeated noun with の, for both adjective+noun and noun₁の noun₂.
- [[つもりだ]] — stating an intention/plan.
- [[adjective-なる]] — "become [adjective]," change-of-state.
- [[どこかに-どこにも]] — "somewhere" vs. "nowhere/not anywhere," extending Lesson 8's 何か／何も pattern to だれか／どこか and beyond.
- [[で-means]] — で marking means of transportation or instrument ("by bus," "with chopsticks").

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `10-winter-vacation-plans/phrases/`: [[が-けど-trailing]] (leaving a sentence open for the listener to respond).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `10-winter-vacation-plans/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjectives]], [[な-adjective]], [[u-verbs]], [[ru-verb]], [[irregular-verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[station]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の交通機関 — Public Transportation in Japan (p. 247).

**Useful Expressions:** 駅で — At the Station (p. 248) — folded into [[station]] above.
