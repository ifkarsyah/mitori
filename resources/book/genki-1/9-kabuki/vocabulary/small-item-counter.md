# Numbers — counter for small items（つ）

A general-purpose native-Japanese counter for small items (used for the box lunches/tea/coffee ordered in this lesson's dialogue), distinct from the Sino-Japanese counters like 枚 ([[../../5-a-trip-to-okinawa/grammar/counting]]) or 人 ([[../../7-family-picture/grammar/counting-people]]).

| # | Japanese |
|---|---|
| 1 | ひとつ（一つ） |
| 2 | ふたつ（二つ） |
| 3 | みっつ（三つ） |
| 4 | よっつ（四つ） |
| 5 | いつつ（五つ） |
| 6 | むっつ（六つ） |
| 7 | ななつ（七つ） |
| 8 | やっつ（八つ） |
| 9 | ここのつ（九つ） |
| 10 | とお（十） |

お弁当（べんとう）を二（ふた）つください。それから、お茶を一（ひと）つとコーヒーを一つ。
Two box lunches, please. And then, one tea and one coffee.

## Notes
Unlike 枚／人, this series has no kanji-numeral + counter-suffix structure — each word is a single fused native-Japanese number word, and it stops being usable past 10 (larger quantities switch to the Sino-Japanese number system with a different, item-specific counter).
