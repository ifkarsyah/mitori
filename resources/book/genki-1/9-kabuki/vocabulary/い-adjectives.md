# い-adjectives

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 青（あお）い | blue |
| 赤（あか）い | red |
| 黒（くろ）い | black |
| 寂（さび）しい | lonely |
| 白（しろ）い | white |
| 若（わか）い | young |

The four basic color adjectives here (黒／赤／白／青) belong to Group 1 in [[../vocabulary/colors]]'s two-group color-word system.
