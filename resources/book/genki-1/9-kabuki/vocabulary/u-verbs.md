# U-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 踊（おど）る | to dance |
| 終（お）わる | (something) ends（〜が） |
| 人気（にんき）がある | to be popular |
| 始（はじ）まる | (something) begins（〜が） |
| 弾（ひ）く | to play (a string instrument or piano)（〜を） |
| もらう | to get (from somebody)（person に thing を） |

始まる／終わる are the intransitive pair behind this lesson's Kabuki showtime (十二時（じゅうにじ）から四時（よじ）まで) — "the show begins/ends," not "someone begins/ends the show."
