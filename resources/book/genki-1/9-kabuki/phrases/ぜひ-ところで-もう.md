# ぜひ／ところで／もう

Three small but frequent discourse words from this lesson's dialogue.

## ぜひ — by all means
たけし：かぶきの切符（きっぷ）を二枚（にまい）もらったから、見（み）に行きませんか。
メアリー：ええ、ぜひ。
Mary: Sure, I'd love to (by all means).

## ところで — by the way
たけし：ところで、もう昼（ひる）ご飯を食（た）べましたか。
Takeshi: By the way, did you already eat lunch?
Signals a topic change, often to something only loosely related.

## もう — already
The signal word for the "present-relevant past" reading discussed in [[../grammar/まだていません]] — もう昼ご飯を食べましたか asks "have you eaten lunch (already, as of now)," setting up まだ食（た）べていません as the natural negative answer.
