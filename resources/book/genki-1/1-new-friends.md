# Lesson 1 — あたらしいともだち New Friends

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 38–57)
Story context: Mary, an international student, meets new people on campus and introduces herself.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `1-new-friends/grammar/`
- [[x-は-y-です]] — the basic "X is Y" copula sentence, and its topic marker は.
- [[question-sentences]] — forming yes/no and content questions with か, question-word placement.
- [[noun-no-noun]] — the possessive/associative linking particle の ("noun₁'s noun₂", e.g. affiliation: 〜大学の学生).

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `1-new-friends/phrases/`: [[すみません]], [[ありがとうございます]] (+いいえ), [[あの]], [[ええ-はい|ええ／はい]], [[そうですか]], [[pronunciation-of-は]], [[self-introduction]].

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `1-new-friends/vocabulary/`: [[time-and-numbers]], [[identity-and-personal-info]], [[school-and-affiliation]], [[nationality-and-language]], [[countries]], [[majors]], [[occupations]], [[family]].

**CultureNote:** にほんじんのなまえ — Japanese Names (p. 45). Family-name-first order, and honorific suffixes (さん etc.).

**Useful Expressions:** じかん・とし — Time / Age (p. 57). Telling time and stating age as standalone reference vocab, ahead of the grammar for numbers/counters that comes later.
