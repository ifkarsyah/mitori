# Lesson 5 — 沖縄旅行 A Trip to Okinawa

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 128–145)
Story context: Robert and Ken vacation in Okinawa (swimming, surfing plans), Robert buys stamps at the post office, then back at school he and Takeshi compare notes on the trip — Takeshi's own date goes conspicuously unmentioned.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `5-a-trip-to-okinawa/grammar/`
- [[adjectives]] — い-adjective and な-adjective conjugation (present/past, affirmative/negative), plus attributive vs. predicate use.
- [[好き-きらい]] — "like/dislike" as relational な-adjectives, and the が that marks their object.
- [[ましょう-ましょうか]] — "let's..." and "shall we...?".
- [[counting]] — number-word placement after the counted item, and the 枚 counter for flat objects.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `5-a-trip-to-okinawa/phrases/`: [[だいじょうぶ]], [[忙しい-にぎやか]] (忙しい for people/days vs. にぎやか for places).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `5-a-trip-to-okinawa/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjectives]], [[な-adjectives]], [[verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[post-office]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の祭り — Japanese Festivals (p. 144).

**Useful Expressions:** 郵便局で — At the Post Office (p. 145) — folded into [[post-office]] above.
