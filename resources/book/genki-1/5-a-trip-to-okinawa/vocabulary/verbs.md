# Verbs

## U-verbs
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 泳（およ）ぐ | to swim |
| 聞（き）く | to ask（person に） |
| 乗（の）る | to ride; to board（〜に） |
| やる | to do; to perform（〜を） |

## Ru-verb
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 出（で）かける | to go out |

Note 聞く has a second meaning here ("to ask a person") beyond "to listen/hear" from [[../../3-making-a-date/vocabulary/u-verbs]] — the particle distinguishes them (person に聞く = ask; 〜を聞く = listen to).
