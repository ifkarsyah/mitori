# Verbs

## U-verbs
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>泳<rt>およ</rt></ruby>ぐ | to swim |
| <ruby>聞<rt>き</rt></ruby>く | to ask（person に） |
| <ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>る | to ride; to board（〜に） |
| やる | to do; to perform（〜を） |

## Ru-verb
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>出<rt>で</rt></ruby>かける | to go out |

Note 聞く has a second meaning here ("to ask a person") beyond "to listen/hear" from [[../../3-making-a-date/vocabulary/5-u-verbs]] — the particle distinguishes them (person に聞く = ask; 〜を聞く = listen to).
