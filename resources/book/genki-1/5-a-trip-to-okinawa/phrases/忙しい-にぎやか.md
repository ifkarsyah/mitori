# 忙しい（いそがしい）vs にぎやか（な）

Both can translate as "busy" in English, but they aren't interchangeable in Japanese.

忙しい describes **people** (or days) being busy:
たけしさんは忙しいです。
Takeshi is busy.

にぎやか（な）describes **places** being busy/lively:
東京（とうきょう）はにぎやかです。
Tokyo is busy / lively.

## Notes
日曜日（にちようび）は忙しいです。("I'm busy on Sunday") is fine even though it looks like it's describing a day — the subject "I" is simply omitted, and it's understood as 日曜日は（私は）忙しいです。
