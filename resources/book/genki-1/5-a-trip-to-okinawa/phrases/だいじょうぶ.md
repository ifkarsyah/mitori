# 大丈夫（だいじょうぶ）

Robert's reassurance when Ken worries surfing might be too hard:

けん：でも、難（むずか）しくないですか。
Ken: But isn't it difficult?

ロバート：大丈夫ですよ。
Robert: It's okay (don't worry about it).

Functions broadly as "it's fine / no problem / everything's under control," beyond just this exchange.
