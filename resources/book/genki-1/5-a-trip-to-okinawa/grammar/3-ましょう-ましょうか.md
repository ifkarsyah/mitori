# 〜ましょう／〜ましょうか

Replace the ます ending of a verb's long form with ましょう or ましょうか to get "let's..." — suggesting a plan of action.

<ruby>一緒<rt>いっしょ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>図書館<rt>としょかん</rt></ruby>で<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>しましょう。
Let's study in the library together.

<ruby>喫茶店<rt>きっさてん</rt></ruby>でコーヒーを飲みましょうか。
Shall we drink coffee at a coffee shop?

## Notes
- ましょうか is also used to make a follow-up suggestion after someone remarks on a situation: <ruby>寒<rt>さむ</rt></ruby>いですね。→ お<ruby>茶<rt>ちゃ</rt></ruby>を飲みましょうか。("It's cold, isn't it." → "Shall we drink some tea?")
- Compare with [[../../3-making-a-date/grammar/5-ませんか]] (〜ませんか) — both invite, but ましょう frames it as "let's (do this together)," while ませんか frames it as "won't you (do this with me)?"
