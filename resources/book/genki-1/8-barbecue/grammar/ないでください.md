# 〜ないでください

To request that someone refrain from doing something: negative verbal **short form** + でください.

ここで写真（しゃしん）を撮（と）らないでください。
Please don't take pictures here.

negative short form ＋ でください　＝　"Please don't..."

## Notes
This is the softer alternative to [[../../6-a-day-in-roberts-life/grammar/てはいけません]] (〜てはいけません) promised back in Lesson 6 — a polite request not to do something, rather than a blunt prohibition/rule statement.
