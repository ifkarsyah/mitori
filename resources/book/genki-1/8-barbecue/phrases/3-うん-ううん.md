# うん／ううん

The casual counterparts to はい／いいえ, used in informal speech (see [[../grammar/2-informal-speech]]).

Q：よく<ruby>魚<rt>さかな</rt></ruby>を<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べる？
A：うん、食べる。／ううん、食べない。
Do you often eat fish? — Yeah, I do. / Nah, I don't.

うん = yes (casual); ううん = no (casual) — note they're distinguished only by length/vowel doubling, easy to mix up when written.
