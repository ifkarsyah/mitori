# U-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| かかる | to take (amount of time/money)（no particle） |
| 泊（と）まる | to stay (at a hotel, etc.)（〜に） |
| なる | to become — see [[../grammar/adjective-なる]] |
| 払（はら）う | to pay（〜を） |

かかる is the key verb for this lesson's travel-time/cost questions: ソウルまでどのぐらいかかりますか。("How long does it take to Seoul?")
