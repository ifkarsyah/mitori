# い-adjectives

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 暖（あたた）かい | warm |
| 遅（おそ）い | slow; late |
| 涼（すず）しい | cool (weather — not used for things) |
| 冷（つめ）たい | cold (things/people) |
| 眠（ねむ）い | sleepy |

This lesson's running comparison examples (中国のほうが日本より大きい, etc. — [[../grammar/comparison-two-items]]) draw heavily on this list plus earlier size/temperature adjectives.
