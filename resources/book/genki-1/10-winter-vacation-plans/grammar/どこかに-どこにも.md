# どこかに／どこにも

[[../../8-barbecue/grammar/何か-何も]] introduced 何か／何も, built from the question word 何 + か／も. The same pattern generalizes to other question words:

| | something/someone/somewhere | not anything/anyone/anywhere |
|---|---|---|
| things | 何（なに）か | 何も＋negative |
| people | だれか | だれも＋negative |
| places | どこか | どこも＋negative |

## Interaction with other particles
何か／何も stand in for は／が／を (used without any particle attached). But other particles — に／へ／で — still appear, attached directly to these words:

どこかへ行きましたか。　　いいえ、どこへも行きませんでした。
Did you go anywhere? — No, I didn't go anywhere.

だれかに会いましたか。　　いいえ、だれにも会いませんでした。
Did you see anybody? — No, I didn't see anybody.

何かしましたか。　　いいえ、何もしませんでした。
Did you do anything? — No, I didn't do anything. (no particle needed — direct object slot)

## Notes
The underlying particle determines whether か／も attaches with nothing extra (何か／何も alone, for は／が／を slots) or stacks after another particle (どこかへ／だれにも, for に／へ／で slots).
