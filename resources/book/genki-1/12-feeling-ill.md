# Lesson 12 — 病気 Feeling Ill

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 266–287)
Story context: Mary comes down with a stomachache after dinner with a friend, then sees a doctor about a sore throat and fever — the final lesson of Genki I, rounding out explanation/obligation/conjecture grammar.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `12-feeling-ill/grammar/`
- [[んです]] — the explanatory の(ん)です, framing a statement as background/explanation rather than a bare fact.
- [[すぎる]] — "too much" (excessive degree), attaching to verb/adjective stems/bases.
- [[ほうがいいです]] — giving advice ("it's better to..."), with a quirky tense-by-polarity rule.
- [[ので]] — a softer, slightly more formal reason clause than から.
- [[なければいけません]] — obligation ("must do X"), polite なければ vs. colloquial なきゃ.
- [[でしょう]] — conjecture/probability ("probably..."), plus its casual confirmation-checking use.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `12-feeling-ill/phrases/`: [[おだいじに]], [[心配しないでください]].

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `12-feeling-ill/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjectives]], [[な-adjective]], [[u-verbs]], [[ru-verbs]], [[irregular-verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[health-and-illness]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の気候 — The Japanese Climate (p. 286).

**Useful Expressions:** 健康と病気 — Health and Illness (p. 287) — folded into [[health-and-illness]] above.
