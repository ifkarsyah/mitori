# Lesson 2 — かいもの Shopping

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 58–83)
Story context: Mary haggles over prices at a flea market, a stranger returns her dropped wallet, then she orders at a restaurant.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `2-shopping/grammar/`
- [[これ-それ-あれ-どれ]] — demonstrative pronouns ("this/that/that over there/which") for objects.
- [[この-その-あの-どの-noun]] — demonstrative adjectives modifying a noun directly.
- [[ここ-そこ-あそこ-どこ]] — demonstrative pronouns for places.
- [[だれの-noun]] — asking/stating whose something is.
- [[noun-も]] — the inclusive/"also" particle.
- [[noun-じゃないです]] — noun-sentence negation (colloquial, 2nd-edition form replacing 〜じゃありません).
- [[ね-よ]] — sentence-final particles for seeking agreement (ね) vs. asserting new information (よ).
