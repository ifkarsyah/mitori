# Nationality & Language

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| にほん | Japan |
| えいご | English (language) |
| 〜ご | ...language — にほんご "Japanese language" |
| 〜じん | ...people — にほんじん "Japanese people" |

## Notes
- 〜ご and 〜じん are productive suffixes: attach them to a country name (see [[countries]]) to get "the language of X" / "a person from X" — アメリカじん (American), かんこくご (Korean language), etc.
- にほんご is also this lesson's running example for [[../grammar/x-は-y-です]] (せんこうはにほんごです — "my major is Japanese").
