# School & Affiliation

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| がくせい | student |
| だいがく | college; university |
| こうこう | high school |
| せんせい | teacher; Professor... |
| せんこう | major |
| ともだち | friend |
| りゅうがくせい | international student |
| 〜ねんせい | ...year student — いちねんせい "first-year student" |

## Notes
- Combine with の ([[../grammar/noun-no-noun]]) for affiliation: アリゾナだいがくの　がくせい ("a student at the University of Arizona").
- せんせい is reserved for describing *someone else's* occupation — わたしはせんせいです sounds arrogant since せんせい literally means "honorable master." A teacher referring to themselves modestly would use きょうし instead.
