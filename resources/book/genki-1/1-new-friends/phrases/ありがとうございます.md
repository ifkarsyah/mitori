# ありがとうございます／いいえ

The formal "thank you" and its standard reply, straight from the dialogue:

メアリー：ありがとう　ございます。
Mary: Thank you.

たけし：いいえ。
Takeshi: You're welcome.

## Notes
- いいえ literally means "no," but as a reply to thanks (or to a compliment/apology) it functions as "not at all" / "you're welcome" — a deflection, not a literal negation of anything.
- ありがとうございます is the polite/formal register; casual conversation shortens this to ありがとう or どうも (どうも also appears later in this chapter's dialogue as a standalone "thanks").
