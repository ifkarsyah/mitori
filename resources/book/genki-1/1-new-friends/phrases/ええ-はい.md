# ええ／はい

Two ways to say "yes" in response to a yes/no question, used in this lesson's dialogue:

たけし：あの、りゅうがくせいですか。
メアリー：ええ、アリゾナだいがくの　がくせいです。

## Notes (Expression Notes 2, p. 46)
- Both はい and ええ mean "yes" in response to yes/no questions. ええ is more conversational/relaxed than はい; in informal situations, うん is used instead.
- はい has an extra use ええ doesn't share: responding to a knock at the door or to your name being called, meaning "Here" —
  Teacher: スミスさん？ "Mr. Smith?"
  Student: はい。"Here."
