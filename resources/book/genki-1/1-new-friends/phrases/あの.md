# あの

A hesitation filler opening Takeshi's question in the dialogue — signals he has some reservation about what he's about to say (here, asking a personal question of a stranger):

たけし：あの、りゅうがくせいですか。
Takeshi: Um... are you an international student?

## Notes
From Expression Notes 2 (p. 46): あの indicates reservations about the coming statement — worry about interrupting, or about sounding rude/impolite for asking something personal. Distinct from あの+noun (the demonstrative adjective covered in Lesson 2) — this is the standalone discourse filler, unrelated in function despite the identical spelling.
