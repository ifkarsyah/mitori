# Self-introduction template

The lesson's review exercise (p. 55) models a full self-introduction, bookended by two fixed expressions not yet explained anywhere else in the lesson:

はじめまして。メアリー・ハートです。
How do you do. I am Mary Hart.

アリゾナだいがくの　がくせいです。
I am a student at the University of Arizona. — uses [[../grammar/noun-no-noun]]

いま　にねんせいです。せんこうは　にほんごです。
I am a sophomore now. My major is Japanese. — uses [[../grammar/x-は-y-です]]

じゅうきゅうさいです。
I am nineteen years old.

よろしく　おねがいします。
Please treat me well / Nice to meet you.

## Notes
- はじめまして ("how do you do") is used only the very first time you meet someone — never again after that.
- よろしくおねがいします closes a self-introduction as a set, fixed phrase; it doesn't translate literally into English and is used far more broadly elsewhere in Japanese (requests, favors, closing correspondence).
- The middle of the template is just this lesson's grammar points chained together — see [[../grammar/x-は-y-です]] and [[../grammar/noun-no-noun]].
