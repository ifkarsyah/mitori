# noun₁ の noun₂

の is a particle that connects two nouns into one noun phrase, noun₁ の noun₂. The second noun (noun₂) is the **main idea**; the first noun (noun₁) restricts/specifies it further. Where the two nouns land depends on which relationship の is expressing.

## As possession ("X's Y") — same order as English
たけしさんの　でんわばんごう
Takeshi's phone number

だいがくの　せんせい
a college professor

## As "a Y of/in X" — reversed order from English
にほんごの　がくせい
a student **of** the Japanese language

にほんの　だいがく
a college **in** Japan

## The whole phrase acts like one noun
Because noun₁ の noun₂ behaves as a single noun, it can be dropped into any of the patterns from this lesson, including chained with another の or used as the X or Y of [[x-は-y-です]]:

たけしさんの　おかあさんは　こうこうの　せんせいです。
Takeshi's mother is a high school teacher.

## Notes
- の is not limited to possession — treat it as "the following noun belongs to/is restricted by the preceding noun," and let context supply the exact relationship (possession, source, category, material, etc.).
- This is the pattern used throughout the lesson's self-introduction drills, e.g. アリゾナだいがくの　がくせいです ("[I am] a student of Arizona University") in the [[../phrases/self-introduction|self-introduction phrase]].
