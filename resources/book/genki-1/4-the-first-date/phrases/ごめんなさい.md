# ごめんなさい

Takeshi's apology after realizing he waited at the wrong burger place:

たけし：マクドナルド……ごめんなさい！
Takeshi: McDonald's... I'm sorry!

A direct, personal apology — distinct in register from すみません ([[../../1-new-friends/phrases/すみません]]), which is lighter and doubles as "excuse me."
