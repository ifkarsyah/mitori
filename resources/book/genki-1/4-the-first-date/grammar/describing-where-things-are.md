# Describing Where Things Are

Lesson 2 covered pointing and saying あそこ／そこ／ここ です ([[../../2-shopping/grammar/ここ-そこ-あそこ-どこ]]). This lesson adds describing one item's location *relative to another*: "X is in front of Y."

（マクドナルドは）あのデパートの前（まえ）です。
It's in front of that department store.

## Location words
X は Y の ＿＿ です。
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| みぎ | to the right of |
| ひだり | to the left of |
| まえ | in front of |
| うしろ | behind |
| なか | inside |
| うえ | on/above |
| した | under/beneath |
| ちかく | near |
| となり | next to |

X は Y と Z のあいだです。("X is between Y and Z.")

銀行（ぎんこう）は図書館（としょかん）のとなりです。
The bank is next to the library.

レストランはデパートと病院（びょういん）の間（あいだ）です。
The restaurant is between the department store and the hospital.

## With verbs, not just です
Combine location words with a verb using で for the location-of-action:

私（わたし）はモスバーガーの前でメアリーさんを待（ま）ちました。
I waited for Mary in front of the Mos Burger place.

## Notes
- X の前 can also mean "across from X" — an alternative word for that is X のむかい. If something is out of sight behind X, both X の後ろ and X のうら are used.
- となり requires X and Y to be the same *kind* of thing (two people, two buildings); よこ ("beside") doesn't have that restriction — かばんはつくえのよこです is fine even though a bag and a desk aren't the same category, but ×かばんはつくえのとなりです sounds odd.
