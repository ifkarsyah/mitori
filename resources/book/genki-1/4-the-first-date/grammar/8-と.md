# と

Two distinct uses.

## Connecting two nouns — "A and B"
<ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby>と<ruby>英語<rt>えいご</rt></ruby>を<ruby>話<rt>はな</rt></ruby>します。
I speak Japanese and English.

<ruby>京都<rt>きょうと</rt></ruby>と<ruby>大阪<rt>おおさか</rt></ruby>に行きました。
I went to Kyoto and Osaka.

## "Together with" — who you do something with
メアリーさんはスーさんと<ruby>韓国<rt>かんこく</rt></ruby>に行きます。
Mary will go to Korea with Sue.

## Notes
- と for connecting only works between **nouns** — connecting verbs or whole sentences is covered later, in Lesson 6.
- "With" in the instrumental sense ("with chopsticks") needs a different particle entirely — covered in Lesson 10.
