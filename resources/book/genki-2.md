# GENKI II: An Integrated Course in Elementary Japanese (2nd Edition)

初級日本語げんき II / Second Edition, The Japan Times, 2011.
Authors: Eri Banno, Yoko Ikeda, Yutaka Ohno, Chikako Shinagawa, Kyoko Tokashiki.

The second and final volume of the Genki series — Lessons 13–23, completing the 23-lesson elementary course begun in [[genki-1|Genki I]]. Grammar and vocabulary pick up exactly where Genki I left off; there is no new "front matter" (no writing-system intro, no greetings section) — Genki II starts straight into Lesson 13.

## Structure of the book

Same three-part structure as Genki I:

### 1. 会話・文法編 (Conversation & Grammar)
Lessons 13–23, each with Dialogue, Vocabulary, Grammar, Practice, CultureNote, and (for most lessons) Useful Expressions — same section layout as every Genki I lesson.

### 2. 読み書き編 (Reading & Writing)
Continues the same lesson numbering from Genki I (which ended at Reading & Writing Lesson 12) — Lessons 13–23 here introduce further kanji (~15 per lesson) and progressively longer, more varied reading passages (letters, essays, manga excerpts, advertisements), no longer paired 1:1 in topic with the conversation lesson of the same number.

### 3. 巻末 (Appendix)
Indices, map of Japan, numbers, and conjugation chart (pp. 350–405) — structured identically to Genki I's appendix.

## What's new in this volume

Genki II is where the grammar shifts from "elementary sentence patterns" to the connective tissue of fluent Japanese: potential form, volitional form, giving/receiving verbs (あげる／くれる／もらう and their て-form counterparts), passive and causative sentences, honorific and humble speech (敬語), and a long tail of sentence-connecting particles (て form nuances, ～ながら, ～たら, ～ば, ～のに, etc.). By Lesson 23, the course has covered plain-style speech, keigo, and every major verb voice — the complete elementary-Japanese grammar inventory the Genki series promises.
