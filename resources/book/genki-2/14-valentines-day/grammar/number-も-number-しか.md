# number ＋も／number ＋しか＋negative

Recall the basic number-expressing structure: noun（が／を）＋number.

私（わたし）のうちには猫（ねこ）が三匹（さんびき）います。
There are three cats in our house.

かさを三本（さんぼん）買（か）いました。
We bought three umbrellas.

## ＋も — "as many as"
私の母（はは）は猫を三匹も飼（か）っています。
My mother owns three, count them, three cats.

きのうのパーティーには学生（がくせい）が二十人（にじゅうにん）も来（き）ました。
As many as twenty students showed up at the party yesterday.

## ＋しか＋negative — "as few as; only"
私は日本語（にほんご）の辞書（じしょ）を一冊（いっさつ）しか持（も）っていません。
I have only one Japanese dictionary.

この会社（かいしゃ）にはパソコンが二台（にだい）しかありません。
There are only two computers in this company.

## Notes
Both attach to the number word itself — も and しか signal the speaker's *evaluation* of the quantity (surprisingly many / disappointingly few), not just the bare count.
