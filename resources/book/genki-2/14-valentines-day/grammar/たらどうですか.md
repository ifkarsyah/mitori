# 〜たらどうですか

After a verb, たらどうですか conveys advice or recommendation. The initial た matches the past-tense short-form affirmative ending. Casual speech shortens it to たらどう or just たら.

もっと勉強（べんきょう）したらどうですか。
Why don't you study harder?

薬（くすり）を飲（の）んだらどうですか。
How about taking some medicine?

## Notes
- Can sound critical, implying the listener should have already done the thing — safest to use only when you've actually been asked for advice/consultation.
- **Not** for extending invitations — うちに来（こ）ませんか (won't you come to my place?), never ×うちに来たらどうですか.
