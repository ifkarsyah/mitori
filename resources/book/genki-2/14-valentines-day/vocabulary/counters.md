## Notes
- Numbers 1, 6, 8, and 10 shift their own pronunciation before こ／さつ／ひき／ほん, except 6 before さつ (ろくさつ, not ろっさつ).
- The counters ひき／ほん shift their own initial sound to びき／ぼん after 1, 6, 8, 10, and to ぴき／ぽん after 3 and なん.
