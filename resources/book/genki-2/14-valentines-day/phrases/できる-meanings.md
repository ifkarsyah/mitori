# できる — several meanings

## "can do / be good at / do well"
日本語（にほんご）ができます。
I am capable in Japanese.

彼（かれ）はスキーができません。
He can't ski.

テストであまりできませんでした。
I didn't do well on the exam.

## "be completed / finished"
晩（ばん）ご飯ができましたよ。
Dinner is ready.

宿題（しゅくだい）はできましたか。
Is your homework done yet?

## "appear / come into existence / be made"
新（あたら）しい店（みせ）ができました。
A new store has opened.

友（とも）だちがたくさんできました。
I have made many friends.

## Notes
This is the same できる that's the potential form of する ([[../../13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/potential-verbs|see Lesson 13]]) — its "can do" sense is the direct potential meaning; the other two senses are idiomatic extensions worth memorizing separately.
