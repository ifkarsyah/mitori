# Passive Sentences

When you're inconvenienced by something someone else has done, express your dissatisfaction with a passive sentence. Compare (a) the objective description with (b) the passive, which makes clear how you feel about it:

(a) <ruby>友<rt>とも</rt></ruby>だちが<ruby>車<rt>くるま</rt></ruby>を<ruby>使<rt>つか</rt></ruby>いました。
A friend of mine used my car.

(b) <ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>は友だちに車を使われました。
I had my car used by a friend of mine (and I am mad/sad about it).

## Basic makeup
私は　友だちに　車を使われました。
（victim）は　（villain）に　（evil act, passive verb）

The "victim" is affected by an event, marked with は or が. The "villain" performs the action causing the suffering, marked with に. The "evil act" is described with the verb's passive form.

## Forming the passive
| Type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ru-verbs | drop -る, add -られる | <ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べる → 食べられる |
| u-verbs | drop -u, add -areる | <ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く → 行かれる、<ruby>話<rt>はな</rt></ruby>す → 話される、<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ → 待たれる、<ruby>死<rt>し</rt></ruby>ぬ → 死なれる、<ruby>読<rt>よ</rt></ruby>む → 読まれる、<ruby>取<rt>と</rt></ruby>る → 取られる、<ruby>泳<rt>およ</rt></ruby>ぐ → 泳がれる、<ruby>遊<rt>あそ</rt></ruby>ぶ → 遊ばれる、<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>う → 買われる |
| irregular | | くる → こられる、する → される |

Ru-verb passives and the irregular こられる look identical to the potential forms from [[../../13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/1-potential-verbs]] — but u-verb passives differ visibly from their potential counterparts: 読む's passive is 読まれる, while its potential is 読める.

Passive verbs conjugate as regular ru-verbs:
| | short, affirmative | short, negative | long, affirmative | long, negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| present | 読まれる | 読まれない | 読まれます | 読まれません |
| past | 読まれた | 読まれなかった | 読まれました | 読まれませんでした |
| te-form | 読まれて | | | |

## Unfavorable effect
Most passive sentences describe the "victim" being unfavorably affected — anger, embarrassment, sadness, hurt.

私は<ruby>隣<rt>となり</rt></ruby>の<ruby>人<rt>ひと</rt></ruby>にたばこを<ruby>吸<rt>す</rt></ruby>われました。
I was annoyed with the person sitting next to me for smoking.

たけしさんはメアリーさんによく<ruby>笑<rt>わら</rt></ruby>われます。
Takeshi is often laughed at by Mary.

<ruby>山下先生<rt>やましたせんせい</rt></ruby>はだれかにパスワードを<ruby>盗<rt>ぬす</rt></ruby>まれたそうです。
I hear that Professor Yamashita had his password stolen by someone.

Compare with the *intended/favorable* focus of a てもらう sentence ([[../../16-lost-and-found/grammar/1-てあげる-くれる-もらう]]):
私は友だちに<ruby>日記<rt>にっき</rt></ruby>を読まれました。（unwanted — annoyed）
I was annoyed with a friend of mine for reading my diary.

私は友だちに<ruby>手紙<rt>てがみ</rt></ruby>を読んでもらいました。（wanted — grateful）
I had a friend of mine read the letter for me.

## Notes
- Since passive only applies to verbs, you cannot use it to express suffering from an *adjectival* situation — 私は<ruby>雨<rt>あめ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>降<rt>ふ</rt></ruby>られました ("I was annoyed by the fact that it rained/I was rained on") works because 降る is a verb, but there's no way to passivize <ruby>悪<rt>わる</rt></ruby>い (bad) to express "I was annoyed that the weather was bad." You also can't passivize an already-negated verb, so "Professor Yamashita was annoyed because students did not come to class" isn't expressible this way.
- Not every passive sentence is unfavorable — a handful come out neutral or even positive:
私はその<ruby>人<rt>ひと</rt></ruby>にデートに<ruby>誘<rt>さそ</rt></ruby>われました。("I was asked out by that person for a date.")
私は<ruby>兄<rt>あに</rt></ruby>に友だちに<ruby>紹介<rt>しょうかい</rt></ruby>されました。("I was introduced by my big brother to a friend of his.")
私は友だちにパーティーに<ruby>呼<rt>よ</rt></ruby>ばれました。("I was invited by a friend to a party.")
その人はみんなに<ruby>尊敬<rt>そんけい</rt></ruby>されています。("That person is looked up to by most everyone.")
- A second, rarer type — "direct passive" — has an inanimate/non-sentient subject and carries no implication of inconvenience; it's more common in writing than speech, and the agent takes によって instead of に:
ここに<ruby>公園<rt>こうえん</rt></ruby>が<ruby>作<rt>つく</rt></ruby>られます。("A park will be built here.")
この<ruby>絵<rt>え</rt></ruby>はピカソによってかかれました。("The picture was drawn by Picasso.")
<ruby>南<rt>みなみ</rt></ruby>アフリカでワールドカップが<ruby>開<rt>ひら</rt></ruby>かれました。("The World Cup was held in South Africa.")
