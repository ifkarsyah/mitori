# 〜てみる

Te-form ＋ みる expresses "doing something tentatively" or "trying something" — you're not sure of the outcome, but you do it to see what effect it has.

漢字（かんじ）がわからなかったので、日本人（にほんじん）の友（とも）だちに聞（き）いてみました。
I did not know the kanji, so I tried asking a Japanese friend of mine.

友だちがあの店（みせ）のケーキはおいしいと言（い）っていましたから、今度（こんど）食（た）べてみます。
My friends say that the cake at the shop is good. I will have a piece one of these days (and see if it really lives up to its reputation).

## Notes
みる comes from 見（み）る and conjugates as a regular ru-verb, but 〜てみる is always written in hiragana (never with the 見 kanji) — unlike the main verb 見る itself.
