# Playing musical instruments — different verbs per instrument type

## Stringed and keyboard instruments — 弾（ひ）く
ギターを弾く — to play the guitar
ピアノを弾く — to play the piano

## Wind instruments — 吹（ふ）く
サックスを吹く — to play the saxophone

## Percussion instruments — たたく
ドラムをたたく — to play the drum

## Referring to instruments in general — やる／できる
When not naming a specific instrument, use やる or できる (for potential, see [[../grammar/potential-verbs]]):

何（なに）か楽器（がっき）ができますか。
Can you play any instruments?

何か楽器をやりますか。
Do you play any instruments?
