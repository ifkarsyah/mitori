# 〜ないで

To mention an action that was **skipped** while doing something else, use the short-form present negative ＋ で. The present tense ない is used regardless of whether the missed action was present or past.

verb ＋ ないで　＝　"without doing X"

きのうの夜（よる）は、寝（ね）ないで、勉強（べんきょう）しました。
Last night, I studied without getting any sleep.

辞書（じしょ）を使（つか）わないで、新聞（しんぶん）を読（よ）みます。
I read a newspaper without using dictionaries.

## Notes
Distinct from [[../../17-grumble-and-gossip/grammar/なくてもいいです]]'s なくて — なくて gives a *reason* or apologizes for an omission ("sorry for not..."), while ないで describes a *manner* — how one action was carried out relative to a skipped one.
