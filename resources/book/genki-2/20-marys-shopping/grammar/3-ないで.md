# 〜ないで

To mention an action that was **skipped** while doing something else, use the short-form present negative ＋ で. The present tense ない is used regardless of whether the missed action was present or past.

verb ＋ ないで　＝　"without doing X"

<ruby>昨日<rt>きのう</rt></ruby>の<ruby>夜<rt>よる</rt></ruby>は、<ruby>寝<rt>ね</rt></ruby>ないで、<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>しました。
Last night, I studied without getting any sleep.

<ruby>辞書<rt>じしょ</rt></ruby>を<ruby>使<rt>つか</rt></ruby>わないで、<ruby>新聞<rt>しんぶん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>読<rt>よ</rt></ruby>みます。
I read a newspaper without using dictionaries.

## Notes
Distinct from [[../../17-grumble-and-gossip/grammar/4-なくてもいいです]]'s なくて — なくて gives a *reason* or apologizes for an omission ("sorry for not..."), while ないで describes a *manner* — how one action was carried out relative to a skipped one.
