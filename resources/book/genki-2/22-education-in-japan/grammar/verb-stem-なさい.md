# verb stem ＋ なさい

A direct command. なさい carries a strong implication of "talking down" to somebody, or that the speaker is more mature, knows better, and should be obeyed — appropriate for a parent to a child, or a teacher to a student. Also common in exam instructions.

うちに毎日（まいにち）電話（でんわ）しなさい。
Call home every day.

かっこの中（なか）に単語（たんご）を入（い）れなさい。
Fill in the blanks with a word.

## Negative — verb ＋ のをやめなさい
文句（もんく）を言（い）うのをやめなさい。
Stop complaining.

("Don't do..." is expressed as verb ＋ のを ＋ やめなさい, from やめる.)
