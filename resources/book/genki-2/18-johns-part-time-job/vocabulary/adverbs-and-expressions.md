# Adverbs & Expressions

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 今（いま）すぐ | right away |
| おかげで | thanks to...（〜の） — see [[../phrases/おかげで-おかげさまで]] |
| お先（さき）に失礼（しつれい）します | See you. (lit. "I'm leaving ahead of you.") |
| お疲（つか）れさま（でした） | You must be tired after working so hard. (ritualistic expression) |
| 〜（ん）だろう | short form of 〜（ん）でしょう |
| どうしよう | What should I/we do? |
| 本当（ほんとう）に | really |
| まず | first of all |
| 〜までに | by (time/date) |
