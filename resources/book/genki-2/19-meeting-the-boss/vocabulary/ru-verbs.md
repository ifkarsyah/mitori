# Ru-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 遅（おく）れる | to become late（〜に） |
| かける | to sit down（seat に） |
| 晴（は）れる | to become sunny |
| もてる | to be popular (in terms of romantic interest)（people に） |

All the honorific verbs from this lesson — including ご覧（らん）になる／お休（やす）みになる, which end in なる — are u-verbs; see [[u-verbs]] and [[../grammar/honorific-verbs]].
