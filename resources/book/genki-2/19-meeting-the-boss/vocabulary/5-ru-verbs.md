# Ru-verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>遅<rt>おく</rt></ruby>れる | to become late（〜に） |
| かける | to sit down（seat に） |
| <ruby>晴<rt>は</rt></ruby>れる | to become sunny |
| もてる | to be popular (in terms of romantic interest)（people に） |

All the honorific verbs from this lesson — including ご<ruby>覧<rt>らん</rt></ruby>になる／お<ruby>休<rt>やす</rt></ruby>みになる, which end in なる — are u-verbs; see [[4-u-verbs]] and [[../grammar/1-honorific-verbs]].
