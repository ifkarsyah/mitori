# Irregular Verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 遠慮（えんりょ）する | to hold back for the time being; to refrain from |
| ごちそうする | to treat/invite (someone) to a meal（person に meal を） |
| 招待（しょうたい）する | to invite someone (to an event/a place)（person を event/place に） |
| 注意（ちゅうい）する | to watch out; to give warning |
| 話（はなし）をする | to have a talk |
