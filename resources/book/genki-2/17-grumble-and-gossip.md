# Lesson 17 — ぐちとうわさ話 Grumble and Gossip

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 118–139)
Story context: Sue runs into Takeshi, now working at a travel agency and grumbling about overtime and low pay; she later reports the encounter to Ken, who worries about Takeshi and Mary's relationship given how little free time he has.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `17-grumble-and-gossip/grammar/`
- [[1-そうです-hearsay]] — a second, unrelated そうです: reporting hearsay ("I hear that...") rather than a visual impression.
- [[2-って]] — the casual quotation particle, standing in for と／そうです in informal speech.
- [[3-たら]] — conditional "if/when A, B," ranging from likely conditions to purely hypothetical, contrary-to-fact ones.
- [[4-なくてもいいです]] — "does not need to...," built on the negative te-form.
- [[5-みたいです]] — "resembles..." for nouns, or "appears to be the case" for verbs — a less formal cousin of そうです.
- [[6-前に-てから]] — "before doing A" (present short form ＋ 前に) vs. "after doing A" (te-form ＋ から).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `17-grumble-and-gossip/vocabulary/`: [[1-nouns]], [[2-い-adjectives]], [[3-u-verbs]], [[4-ru-verbs]], [[5-irregular-verbs]], [[6-adverbs-and-expressions]], [[7-barber-and-salon]].

**CultureNote:** 日本人のジェスチャー — Japanese Gestures (p. 128). Nonverbal gestures for "come here," "sorry," "I/me," "no good," "not me / I don't understand," "I don't know."

**Useful Expressions:** 床屋／美容院で — At the Barber/Beauty Salon (p. 139) — folded into [[7-barber-and-salon]] above.
