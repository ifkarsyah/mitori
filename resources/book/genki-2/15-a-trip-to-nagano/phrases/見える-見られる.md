# 見える vs 見られる

見（み）える is different from 見られる, the regular potential form of 見る ([[../../13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/potential-verbs|potential verbs]]). 見える means something/someone is **spontaneously visible**; 見られる means the subject can see something/someone **actively**, rather than passively.

部屋（へや）の窓（まど）から海（うみ）が見えます。
I can see the ocean from the window of the room. (spontaneous — the ocean is just there to be seen)

どこであの映画（えいが）が見られますか。
Where can I see that movie? (active — seeking it out)

## 聞こえる vs 聞ける — the same distinction
聞（き）こえる means something is **spontaneously audible**; 聞（き）ける, the potential form of 聞く, means the subject can actively hear/listen to the sound.

けさ、鳥（とり）の声（こえ）が聞こえました。
This morning, I heard the voices of the birds. (spontaneous)

インターネットで日本（にほん）のラジオ番組（ばんぐみ）が聞けます。
We can listen to Japanese radio programs on the Internet. (active)
