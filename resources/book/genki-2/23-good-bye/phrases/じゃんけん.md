# じゃんけん

A children's game used throughout Japan to decide who goes first, who is "it," or how to split into teams — this lesson's causative/causative-passive practice activities are built around it.

Players call out "じゃん、けん、ぽん" (jan, ken, pon) and each make one of three hand shapes: stone (rock), scissors, or paper. Stone breaks scissors; scissors cut paper; paper covers stone.

Practical vocabulary from a round: ほい！(the call as hands are shown), あいこで（しょ）("tied, so [again]" — said when there's a tie and another round is needed), しょ (part of the tie-breaker call), かった！("[I] won!").
