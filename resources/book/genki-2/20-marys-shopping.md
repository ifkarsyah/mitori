# Lesson 20 — メアリーさんの買い物 Mary's Shopping

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 184–207)
Story context: Mary returns a broken electronic dictionary to a store (handled by an extra-modestly-speaking clerk), then asks an old man on the street for directions to a fan shop and ends up carrying his bag for him in the rain.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `20-marys-shopping/grammar/`
- [[extra-modest-expressions]] — おります／参ります／申します, etc. — a *third* register, for talking modestly about your own actions on very formal occasions, distinct from both honorific and humble speech.
- [[humble-expressions]] — お＋stem＋する, for describing your own action performed in deference to someone else (visiting, lending, borrowing).
- [[ないで]] — "without doing X," using the negative short form ＋ で.
- [[questions-within-larger-sentences]] — embedding a question inside a sentence ("I don't know when..."), with か or かどうか depending on whether a question word is present.
- [[という-item]] — introducing an item/person by a name the listener may not know: "X という Y."
- [[やすい-にくい]] — "easy-to-do" / "hard-to-do," adjective-forming suffixes on a verb stem.
