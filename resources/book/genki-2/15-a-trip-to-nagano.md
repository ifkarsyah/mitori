# Lesson 15 — 長野旅行 A Trip to Nagano

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 74–95)
Story context: Mary invites Takeshi along to visit Michiko's family in Nagano; they arrive early and sightsee at Zenkoji Temple, then stop at the travel information office for a map and museum discount tickets.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `15-a-trip-to-nagano/grammar/`
- [[volitional-form]] — the casual "let's..." form, and its use for offers with か.
- [[volitional-form-と思っています]] — stating a determination/intention, vs. と思います for a decision made on the spot.
- [[ておく]] — doing something in advance, in preparation for later.
- [[using-sentences-to-qualify-nouns]] — full clauses (not just single adjectives) modifying a noun, Japanese's version of a relative clause.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `15-a-trip-to-nagano/phrases/`: [[casual-contractions-and-けど]] (てる／てた contractions, だ retention before よ, background-setting けど／が), [[予定です]] (scheduled to...), [[見える-見られる]] (spontaneous visibility/audibility vs. active potential), [[目-ordinal-suffix]] (turning a number into "1st/2nd/3rd...").

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `15-a-trip-to-nagano/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjective]], [[u-verbs]], [[ru-verbs]], [[irregular-verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[hotel]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の宿 — Japanese Accommodations (p. 81). ホテル (Western-style hotels, including budget ビジネスホテル), 旅館 (Japanese-style inns with 畳 floors, 浴衣, 布団, often with communal 温泉 baths, meals included), and the cheaper 民宿／ペンション (family-run guesthouses).

**Useful Expressions:** ホテルで — At the Hotel (p. 95) — folded into [[hotel]] above.
