# 〜そうです (I hear...)

[[../../13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/そうです-looks-like]] covered そうです meaning "seemingly." This is a **second, distinct** そうです — a hearsay report ("I hear that..."). The two differ in both meaning and in what forms of predicate they attach to.

Add hearsay そうです to a **short-form** sentence.

If you heard someone say... | You can report it as...
---|---
「日本語（にほんご）の授業（じゅぎょう）は楽（たの）しいです。」("Our Japanese class is fun.") | 日本語の授業は楽しいそうです。(I have heard that their Japanese class is fun.)
「先生（せんせい）はとても親切（しんせつ）です。」("Our professor is very kind.") | 先生はとても親切だそうです。(I have heard that their professor is very kind.)
「今日（きょう）は授業がありませんでした。」("We did not have a class today.") | その日は授業がなかったそうです。(I've heard that they didn't have a class that day.)

The reported speech keeps the original's tense and polarity — you simply convert the predicate to its short form (です after a な-adjective or noun becomes だ; です after an い-adjective is dropped).

| | I hear that... | (compare) It looks like... |
|---|---|---|
| verbs | 話（はな）すそうです | — |
| い-adjectives | さびしいそうです | さびしそうです |
| な-adjectives | 好（す）きだそうです | 好きそうです |
| noun ＋ です | 学生（がくせい）だそうです | — |

## Notes
- Hearsay そうです is robustly invariant — the only forms in common use are そうです and its casual counterpart そうだ. There's no negative そうじゃないです and no past-tense そうでした; negate/tense-shift the *quoted* predicate instead.
- Citing a source: 〜によると ("according to...") — トムさんによると (according to Tom), 新聞（しんぶん）によると (according to the newspaper report), 天気予報（てんきよほう）によると (according to the weather forecast):
天気予報によると、台風（たいふう）が来（く）るそうです。
According to the weather forecast, a typhoon is approaching.
