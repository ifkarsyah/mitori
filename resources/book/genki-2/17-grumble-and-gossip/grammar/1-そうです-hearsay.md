# 〜そうです (I hear...)

[[../../13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/3-そうです-looks-like]] covered そうです meaning "seemingly." This is a **second, distinct** そうです — a hearsay report ("I hear that..."). The two differ in both meaning and in what forms of predicate they attach to.

Add hearsay そうです to a **short-form** sentence.

If you heard someone say... | You can report it as...
---|---
「<ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby>の<ruby>授業<rt>じゅぎょう</rt></ruby>は<ruby>楽<rt>たの</rt></ruby>しいです。」("Our Japanese class is fun.") | 日本語の授業は楽しいそうです。(I have heard that their Japanese class is fun.)
「<ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby>はとても<ruby>親切<rt>しんせつ</rt></ruby>です。」("Our professor is very kind.") | 先生はとても親切だそうです。(I have heard that their professor is very kind.)
「<ruby>今日<rt>きょう</rt></ruby>は授業がありませんでした。」("We did not have a class today.") | その日は授業がなかったそうです。(I've heard that they didn't have a class that day.)

The reported speech keeps the original's tense and polarity — you simply convert the predicate to its short form (です after a な-adjective or noun becomes だ; です after an い-adjective is dropped).

| | I hear that... | (compare) It looks like... |
|---|---|---|
| verbs | <ruby>話<rt>はな</rt></ruby>すそうです | — |
| い-adjectives | さびしいそうです | さびしそうです |
| な-adjectives | <ruby>好<rt>す</rt></ruby>きだそうです | 好きそうです |
| noun ＋ です | <ruby>学生<rt>がくせい</rt></ruby>だそうです | — |

## Notes
- Hearsay そうです is robustly invariant — the only forms in common use are そうです and its casual counterpart そうだ. There's no negative そうじゃないです and no past-tense そうでした; negate/tense-shift the *quoted* predicate instead.
- Citing a source: 〜によると ("according to...") — トムさんによると (according to Tom), <ruby>新聞<rt>しんぶん</rt></ruby>によると (according to the newspaper report), <ruby>天気予報<rt>てんきよほう</rt></ruby>によると (according to the weather forecast):
天気予報によると、<ruby>台風<rt>たいふう</rt></ruby>が<ruby>来<rt>く</rt></ruby>るそうです。
According to the weather forecast, a typhoon is approaching.
