# Lesson 13 — アルバイト探し Looking for a Part-time Job

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 26–49)
Story context: John calls a restaurant about a job ad, interviews, gets hired, and starts working — Professor Yamashita later shows up as a customer.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/grammar/`
- [[potential-verbs]] — the "can do" verb form, ru-verb -られる vs. u-verb -える, and its casual ra-less variant.
- [[し]] — stacking multiple reasons, extending から to list more than one cause.
- [[そうです-looks-like]] — guessing an impression from appearance ("looks like...").
- [[てみる]] — trying something tentatively, to see what happens.
- [[なら]] — contrast and limitation ("if we're talking about X specifically...").
- [[一週間に三回]] — the (period)に(frequency) framework for describing how often something happens.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/phrases/`: [[楽器を弾く-吹く-たたく]] (instrument-specific verbs), [[上手に-adverbial]] (adjective→adverb conversion).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `13-looking-for-a-part-time-job/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjectives]], [[な-adjectives]], [[u-verbs]], [[irregular-verb]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[day-counters]], [[bank]].

**CultureNote:** 年号と干支 — Names of Years (p. 48). Japan's imperial-era calendar (年号: 明治／大正／昭和／平成) alongside the Gregorian year, and the 12-animal zodiac (干支) used to describe birth years.

**Useful Expressions:** 銀行で — At the Bank (p. 49) — folded into [[bank]] above.
