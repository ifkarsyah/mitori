# Lesson 14 — バレンタインデー Valentine's Day

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 50–73)
Story context: Mary knits Takeshi a sweater for Valentine's Day; the next day John compares notes on who got what (Robert did suspiciously well) and learns about White Day, the March 14th "return the favor" custom for men.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `14-valentines-day/grammar/`
- [[ほしい]] — "(I) want (something)," a private predicate restricted to the speaker, with がる for describing others.
- [[かもしれません]] — "might/may be," a softer, less committal cousin of でしょう.
- [[あげる-くれる-もらう]] — the giving/receiving verb triad, chosen by direction relative to the speaker.
- [[たらどうですか]] — giving advice/suggestions ("why don't you...?").
- [[number-も-number-しか]] — number＋も ("as many as") vs. number＋しか＋negative ("as few as; only").

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `14-valentines-day/phrases/`: [[casual-short-forms]] (question-particle dropping, んだ／の, exclamatory わあ／へえ／なあ), [[できる-meanings]] (できる's several senses).

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `14-valentines-day/vocabulary/`: [[nouns]], [[い-adjective]], [[な-adjective]], [[u-verbs]], [[ru-verbs]], [[irregular-verbs]], [[adverbs-and-expressions]], [[counters]].

**CultureNote:** 日本の年中行事 — Annual Events in Japan (p. 72). 節分 (bean-throwing, Feb 3), ひな祭り (Doll Festival/Girls' Day, Mar 3), こどもの日 (Children's Day, May 5), 七夕 (Tanabata, Jul 7), お盆 (Obon, mid-August).
