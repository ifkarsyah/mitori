# <ruby>結構<rt>けっこう</rt></ruby>です

The old woman on the bus politely declines Robert's offer of a seat:

ロバート：どうぞ<ruby>座<rt>すわ</rt></ruby>ってください。
Robert: Take this seat, please.

おばあさん：いいえ、結構です。すぐ<ruby>降<rt>お</rt></ruby>りますから。
Old woman: No, thank you. I'll get off soon.

Functions as a polite "that would be fine" / "that won't be necessary" — a soft decline, not a rude refusal.
