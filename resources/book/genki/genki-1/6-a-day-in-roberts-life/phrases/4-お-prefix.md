# お〜 (politeness prefix)

Many words beginning with お can also be used without it — お adds smoothness and a nuance of social refinement, without changing the word's core meaning.

お<ruby>酒<rt>さけ</rt></ruby>、お<ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>、お<ruby>風呂<rt>ふろ</rt></ruby>、お<ruby>祭<rt>まつ</rt></ruby>り (festival)

## Notes
This isn't a rule for attaching お to any noun freely — it's a fixed set of words where the お-prefixed form is simply the more polished/common variant. Compare with お<ruby>土産<rt>みやげ</rt></ruby> ([[2-people-and-things]]) and お<ruby>名前<rt>なまえ</rt></ruby> ([[5-どうぞ]]), which work the same way.
