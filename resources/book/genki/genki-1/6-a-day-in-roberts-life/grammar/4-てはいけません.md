# 〜てはいけません

Te-form + はいけません means "you must not do..." — a strong prohibition, as in rules and regulations.

ここで<ruby>写真<rt>しゃしん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>撮<rt>と</rt></ruby>ってはいけません。
You must not take pictures here.

## Notes
- If someone asks permission and you want to deny it, てはいけません works, but it can sound too harsh unless you're speaking from a position of authority (a teacher, a rule-setter). A softer way to say "please don't" comes in Lesson 8.
- Unlike てもいいです's optional も, the は here **cannot** be dropped.
