# <ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>る vs わかる

If you don't know the answer to a question but *should* have thought about it, use わかりません, not <ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>りません. 知りません in that context sounds rude — it implies your ignorance of the matter is none of the asker's business.

Compare with [[2-どうして-だから]]'s dialogue: Mary's father asks why Takeshi didn't come, and Mary answers わかりません — not 知りません — because she's expected to have some idea.

## Notes
This is a register/politeness distinction layered on top of the plain meaning difference: 知っています／知りません describe knowledge of a specific fact (see [[1-ている]]'s 知る example), while わかる is about understanding/being able to account for something.
