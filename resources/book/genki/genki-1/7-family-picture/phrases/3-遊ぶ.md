# <ruby>遊<rt>あそ</rt></ruby>ぶ

Means "to play," "to spend time pleasantly," or "to pay a social call" — broader than English "play."

<ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>の<ruby>時<rt>とき</rt></ruby>、よく<ruby>友<rt>とも</rt></ruby>だちと遊びました。
When I was a child, I often played with friends.

<ruby>先週<rt>せんしゅう</rt></ruby>の<ruby>週末<rt>しゅうまつ</rt></ruby>は<ruby>東京<rt>とうきょう</rt></ruby>に遊びに行きました。
I went to Tokyo to have fun last weekend.

私のうちに遊びに<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>てください。
Please come and see us.

## Notes — "to play" needs different words depending on what's being played
- Sports: テニスをする (to play tennis)
- Games: ゲームをする (to play games), トランプをする (to play cards)
- Musical instruments: ギターを<ruby>弾<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>く (to play the guitar)

遊ぶ itself is never used for any of these — it's reserved for unstructured play/socializing.
