# Irregular Verbs

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>結婚<rt>けっこん</rt></ruby>する | to get married（〜と） |

The textbook example verb for [[1-ている]]'s "result of a change" reading — 結婚しています means "is married," not "is getting married."
