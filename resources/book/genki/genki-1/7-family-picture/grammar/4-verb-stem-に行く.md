# verb stem + に行く

Describes movement to another place **in order to** do something.

destination（に／へ） ＋ purpose-of-movement-phrase（stem form）に ＋ 行く／来る／帰る

The purpose phrase (a verb + its object, etc.) must be in **stem** form — the part left after removing ます from the present-tense long form (see [[1-verb-conjugation]]).

デパートに　かばんを<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>い　に行きました。
I went to a department store to buy a bag.

メアリーさんは<ruby>日本<rt>にほん</rt></ruby>に　日本語を<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>し　に<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>ました。
Mary has come to Japan to study Japanese.

## Notes
A bare noun like <ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>い<ruby>物<rt>もの</rt></ruby> ("shopping") can also fill the purpose slot: デパートに買い物に行きました。("I went to a department store for shopping.")
