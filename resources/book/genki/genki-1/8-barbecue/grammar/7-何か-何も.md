# 何か and 何も

"Something" is <ruby>何<rt>なに</rt></ruby>か, and "anything" in negative sentences is 何も.

| | word |
|---|---|
| positive statements | 何か — something |
| questions | 何か — anything? |
| negative statements | 何も＋negative — not... anything |

<ruby>猫<rt>ねこ</rt></ruby>が何か<ruby>持<rt>も</rt></ruby>ってきました。
The cat has brought something.

猫は何か<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べましたか。
Did the cat eat anything?

いいえ、猫は何も食べませんでした。
No, the cat did not eat anything.

## Notes
何か／何も are used exactly where は／が／を would otherwise go — they stand alone, without a particle, in those slots. (What to do when a *different* particle is expected — 何かに, 何かで, etc. — is covered in Lesson 10.)
