# <ruby>乾杯<rt>かんぱい</rt></ruby>！

The toast said before everyone starts drinking/eating together — closes the barbecue dialogue once Mary finally arrives:

メアリー：<ruby>遅<rt>おそ</rt></ruby>くなってすみません。
Mary: I'm sorry for being late.

みんな：じゃあ、乾杯！
Everyone: Well then... Cheers!
