# ちょっと (as a polite refusal)

Literally "a little"/"a bit," as in ちょっとください (please give me a little) or ちょっと<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>ってください (please wait a moment). This lesson highlights a second, very common use: a **polite refusal**.

A：<ruby>土曜日<rt>どようび</rt></ruby>に<ruby>映画<rt>えいが</rt></ruby>を<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>ませんか。
Will you see a movie on Saturday?

B：土曜日は、ちょっと。
Saturday is not convenient. (lit. "Saturday is a little bit...")

## Notes
Japanese speakers don't normally reject requests, suggestions, or invitations with a flat いいえ ("no") — it sounds too direct. Trailing off after ちょっと is the softer, standard way to decline.
