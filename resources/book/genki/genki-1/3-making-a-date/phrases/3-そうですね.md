# そうですね

Opens Mary's answer to Takeshi's question about her weekend — functions as a thinking pause ("let me see...") as much as an agreement marker:

たけし：<ruby>週末<rt>しゅうまつ</rt></ruby>はたいてい<ruby>何<rt>なに</rt></ruby>をしますか。
メアリー：そうですね。たいていうちで<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>します。

## Notes
Distinct from [[5-そうですか|そうですか]] (Lesson 1, "I see"/"is that so") — そうですね is used both to agree with something just said and, as here, to buy a moment to think before answering.
