# Places

| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>家<rt>いえ</rt></ruby> | home; house |
| うち | home; house; my place |
| <ruby>学校<rt>がっこう</rt></ruby> | school |

うち and <ruby>家<rt>いえ</rt></ruby>overlap heavily in meaning; うち additionally carries a sense of "my (own) place/household" that いえ doesn't.
