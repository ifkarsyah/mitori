# Time Words

## Days
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>今日<rt>きょう</rt></ruby> | today |
| <ruby>明日<rt>あした</rt></ruby> | tomorrow |

## Days of the week
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>土曜日<rt>どようび</rt></ruby> | Saturday |
| <ruby>日曜日<rt>にちようび</rt></ruby> | Sunday |
| <ruby>週末<rt>しゅうまつ</rt></ruby> | weekend |

## Times of day
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>朝<rt>あさ</rt></ruby> | morning |
| <ruby>今晩<rt>こんばん</rt></ruby> | tonight |

## Every ~
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>毎日<rt>まいにち</rt></ruby> | every day |
| <ruby>毎晩<rt>まいばん</rt></ruby> | every night |

## Asking when
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| いつ | when |
| 〜ごろ | at about... |

See [[4-time-reference]] for exactly which of these take に and which don't — it's not a uniform rule across this list.
