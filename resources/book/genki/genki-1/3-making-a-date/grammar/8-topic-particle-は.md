# The Topic Particle は (revisited)

は marks the topic of an utterance — "as for item X, it is such that..." A topic phrase is often the sentence's subject (メアリーさんは<ruby>二年生<rt>にねんせい</rt></ruby>です, 私の<ruby>専攻<rt>せんこう</rt></ruby>は日本語です — see [[1-x-は-y-です]]), but this lesson shows it doesn't have to be.

## Non-subject topics
メアリーさん、<ruby>週末<rt>しゅうまつ</rt></ruby>はたいてい<ruby>何<rt>なに</rt></ruby>をしますか。
Mary, what do you usually do on the weekend?
→ は promotes 週末 (a time expression, not a subject) to topic: "Let's talk about weekends; what do you do on weekends?"

<ruby>今日<rt>きょう</rt></ruby>は<ruby>京都<rt>きょうと</rt></ruby>に行きます。
I'm going to Kyoto today.
→ は promotes 今日: "Let me say what I will do today; I will go to Kyoto."

<ruby>晩<rt>ばん</rt></ruby>ご<ruby>飯<rt>はん</rt></ruby>は？　―　<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べません。
How about dinner? — I will not eat.
→ 晩ご飯 is the broached topic, but it's the **direct object** of 食べます, not the subject. は here directs the listener's attention and invites a completion of the thought.

## Notes
は can promote essentially any phrase — subject, object, time expression — to topic status; what unifies its uses is "let's talk about this," not any one grammatical role.
