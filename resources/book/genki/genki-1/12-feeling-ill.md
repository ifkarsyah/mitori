# Lesson 12 — 病気 Feeling Ill

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 266–287)
Story context: Mary comes down with a stomachache after dinner with a friend, then sees a doctor about a sore throat and fever — the final lesson of Genki I, rounding out explanation/obligation/conjecture grammar.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `12-feeling-ill/grammar/`
- [[resources/book/genki/genki-1/12-feeling-ill/grammar/1-んです]] — the explanatory の(ん)です, framing a statement as background/explanation rather than a bare fact.
- [[2-すぎる]] — "too much" (excessive degree), attaching to verb/adjective stems/bases.
- [[3-ほうがいいです]] — giving advice ("it's better to..."), with a quirky tense-by-polarity rule.
- [[4-ので]] — a softer, slightly more formal reason clause than から.
- [[5-なければいけません]] — obligation ("must do X"), polite なければ vs. colloquial なきゃ.
- [[6-でしょう]] — conjecture/probability ("probably..."), plus its casual confirmation-checking use.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `12-feeling-ill/phrases/`: [[1-おだいじに]], [[2-心配しないでください]].
