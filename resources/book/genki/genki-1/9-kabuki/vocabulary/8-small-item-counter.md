# Numbers — counter for small items（つ）

A general-purpose native-Japanese counter for small items (used for the box lunches/tea/coffee ordered in this lesson's dialogue), distinct from the Sino-Japanese counters like 枚 ([[4-counting]]) or 人 ([[5-counting-people]]).

| # | Japanese |
|---|---|
| 1 | <ruby>一<rt>ひと</rt></ruby>つ |
| 2 | <ruby>二<rt>ふた</rt></ruby>つ |
| 3 | <ruby>三<rt>みっ</rt></ruby>つ |
| 4 | <ruby>四<rt>よっ</rt></ruby>つ |
| 5 | <ruby>五<rt>いつ</rt></ruby>つ |
| 6 | <ruby>六<rt>むっ</rt></ruby>つ |
| 7 | <ruby>七<rt>なな</rt></ruby>つ |
| 8 | <ruby>八<rt>やっ</rt></ruby>つ |
| 9 | <ruby>九<rt>ここの</rt></ruby>つ |
| 10 | <ruby>十<rt>とお</rt></ruby> |

お<ruby>弁当<rt>べんとう</rt></ruby>を<ruby>二<rt>ふた</rt></ruby>つください。それから、お茶を<ruby>一<rt>ひと</rt></ruby>つとコーヒーを一つ。
Two box lunches, please. And then, one tea and one coffee.

## Notes
Unlike 枚／人, this series has no kanji-numeral + counter-suffix structure — each word is a single fused native-Japanese number word, and it stops being usable past 10 (larger quantities switch to the Sino-Japanese number system with a different, item-specific counter).
