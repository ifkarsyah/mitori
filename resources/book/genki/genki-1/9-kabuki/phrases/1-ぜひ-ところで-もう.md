# ぜひ／ところで／もう

Three small but frequent discourse words from this lesson's dialogue.

## ぜひ — by all means
たけし：かぶきの<ruby>切符<rt>きっぷ</rt></ruby>を<ruby>二枚<rt>にまい</rt></ruby>もらったから、<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>に行きませんか。
メアリー：ええ、ぜひ。
Mary: Sure, I'd love to (by all means).

## ところで — by the way
たけし：ところで、もう<ruby>昼<rt>ひる</rt></ruby>ご飯を<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べましたか。
Takeshi: By the way, did you already eat lunch?
Signals a topic change, often to something only loosely related.

## もう — already
The signal word for the "present-relevant past" reading discussed in [[3-まだていません]] — もう昼ご飯を食べましたか asks "have you eaten lunch (already, as of now)," setting up まだ<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べていません as the natural negative answer.
