# Describing Where Things Are

Lesson 2 covered pointing and saying あそこ／そこ／ここ です ([[3-ここ-そこ-あそこ-どこ]]). This lesson adds describing one item's location *relative to another*: "X is in front of Y."

（マクドナルドは）あのデパートの<ruby>前<rt>まえ</rt></ruby>です。
It's in front of that department store.

## Location words
X は Y の ＿＿ です。
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>右<rt>みぎ</rt></ruby> | to the right of |
| <ruby>左<rt>ひだり</rt></ruby> | to the left of |
| <ruby>前<rt>まえ</rt></ruby> | in front of |
| <ruby>後<rt>うし</rt></ruby>ろ | behind |
| <ruby>中<rt>なか</rt></ruby> | inside |
| <ruby>上<rt>うえ</rt></ruby> | on/above |
| <ruby>下<rt>した</rt></ruby> | under/beneath |
| <ruby>近<rt>ちか</rt></ruby>く | near |
| <ruby>隣<rt>となり</rt></ruby> | next to |

X は Y と Z のあいだです。("X is between Y and Z.")

<ruby>銀行<rt>ぎんこう</rt></ruby>は<ruby>図書館<rt>としょかん</rt></ruby>の<ruby>隣<rt>となり</rt></ruby>です。
The bank is next to the library.

レストランはデパートと<ruby>病院<rt>びょういん</rt></ruby>の<ruby>間<rt>あいだ</rt></ruby>です。
The restaurant is between the department store and the hospital.

## With verbs, not just です
Combine location words with a verb using で for the location-of-action:

<ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>はモスバーガーの前でメアリーさんを<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>ちました。
I waited for Mary in front of the Mos Burger place.

## Notes
- X の前 can also mean "across from X" — an alternative word for that is X のむかい. If something is out of sight behind X, both X の後ろ and X のうら are used.
- <ruby>隣<rt>となり</rt></ruby> requires X and Y to be the same *kind* of thing (two people, two buildings); <ruby>横<rt>よこ</rt></ruby> ("beside") doesn't have that restriction — かばんは<ruby>机<rt>つくえ</rt></ruby>の<ruby>横<rt>よこ</rt></ruby>です is fine even though a bag and a desk aren't the same category, but ×かばんは<ruby>机<rt>つくえ</rt></ruby>の<ruby>隣<rt>となり</rt></ruby>です sounds odd.
