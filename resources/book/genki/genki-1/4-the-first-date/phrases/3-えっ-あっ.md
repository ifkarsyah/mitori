# えっ／あっ

Two short interjections from the dialogue, both spelled with a small っ to show they're pronounced very short/clipped.

## えっ — incredulous "what?"
Used when you've heard something hard to believe:
お<ruby>父<rt>とう</rt></ruby>さん：えっ、どうしてですか。
Father: What? Why?

## あっ — sudden notice/realization
Used when you've just noticed or remembered something:
メアリー：あっ、たけしさん。<ruby>昨日<rt>きのう</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>ませんでしたね。
Mary: Oh, Takeshi. You didn't come yesterday, did you?
