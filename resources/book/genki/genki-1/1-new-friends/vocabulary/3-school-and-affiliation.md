# School & Affiliation

## People
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>友達<rt>ともだち</rt></ruby> | friend |
| <ruby>学生<rt>がくせい</rt></ruby> | student |
| <ruby>留学生<rt>りゅうがくせい</rt></ruby> | international student |
| <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby> | teacher; Professor... |

## Schools
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>大学<rt>だいがく</rt></ruby> | university |
| <ruby>高校<rt>こうこう</rt></ruby> | high school |

## Study
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>専攻<rt>せんこう</rt></ruby> | major |
| 〜<ruby>年生<rt>ねんせい</rt></ruby> | ...year student — <ruby>一年生<rt>いちねんせい</rt></ruby> "first-year student" |

## Notes
- Combine with の ([[3-noun-no-noun]]) for affiliation: アリゾナ<ruby>大学<rt>だいがく</rt></ruby>の　<ruby>学生<rt>がくせい</rt></ruby> ("a student at the University of Arizona").
- <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby> is reserved for describing *someone else's* occupation — <ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>は<ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby>です sounds arrogant since <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby> literally means "honorable master." A teacher referring to themselves modestly would use <ruby>教師<rt>きょうし</rt></ruby> instead.
