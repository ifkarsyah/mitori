# Nationality & Language

## Country & language
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <ruby>日本<rt>にほん</rt></ruby> | Japan |
| <ruby>英語<rt>えいご</rt></ruby> | English (language) |

## Suffixes
| Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 〜<ruby>語<rt>ご</rt></ruby> | ...language — <ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby> "Japanese language" |
| 〜<ruby>人<rt>じん</rt></ruby> | ...people — <ruby>日本人<rt>にほんじん</rt></ruby> "Japanese people" |

## Notes
- 〜<ruby>語<rt>ご</rt></ruby> and 〜<ruby>人<rt>じん</rt></ruby> are productive suffixes: attach them to a country name (see [[5-countries]]) to get "the language of X" / "a person from X" — アメリカ<ruby>人<rt>じん</rt></ruby> (American), <ruby>韓国語<rt>かんこくご</rt></ruby> (Korean language), etc.
- <ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby> is also this lesson's running example for [[1-x-は-y-です]] (<ruby>専攻<rt>せんこう</rt></ruby>は<ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby>です — "my major is Japanese").
