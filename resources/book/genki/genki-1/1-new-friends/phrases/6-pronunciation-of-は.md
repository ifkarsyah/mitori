# Pronunciation of は

The topic particle は (covered in [[1-x-は-y-です]]) is pronounced the same as わ, not the same as its own kana value — but it's still written は, not わ.

<ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>の　<ruby>電話番号<rt>でんわばんごう</rt></ruby>は　37-8667です。
My telephone number is 37-8667.

## Notes
- All other instances of the わ sound are written わ. は is the one exception, and only when functioning as the topic particle.
- A few greeting words are written with は for historical reasons despite being fixed compounds rather than "topic + rest of sentence": こんにちは (good afternoon) and こんばんは (good evening).
