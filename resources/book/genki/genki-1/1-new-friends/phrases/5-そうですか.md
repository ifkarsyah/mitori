# そうですか／そうです

たけし：そうですか。<ruby>専攻<rt>せんこう</rt></ruby>は　<ruby>何<rt>なん</rt></ruby>ですか。
Takeshi: I see. What is your major?

## Notes
- そうですか acknowledges what was just said — "Is that so?" / "I see." (The か here doesn't make it a real question needing an answer; it's a reaction particle.)
- そうです (without か) is the plain acknowledgment "That's right" — confirming a yes/no question, as in the telephone-number pair drill: A: 283-9547ですね。 B: はい、そうです。("Yes, that's right.")
