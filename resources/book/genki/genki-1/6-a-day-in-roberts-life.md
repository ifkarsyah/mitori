# Lesson 6 — ロバートさんの一日 A Day in Robert's Life

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 146–165)
Story context: Robert dozes off in Professor Yamashita's class and forgets his textbook, then borrows Sue's notes after a rough day, then offers his seat (and later his help carrying bags) to an old woman on the bus — the て-form, the single biggest hub of Japanese verb grammar, ties it all together.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `6-a-day-in-roberts-life/grammar/`
- [[1-te-form]] — formation rules across all verb groups (the mechanical core of this lesson).
- [[2-てください]] — polite request ("please do...").
- [[3-てもいいです]] — asking/granting permission.
- [[4-てはいけません]] — prohibition.
- [[5-describing-two-activities]] — joining clauses with the te-form ("do X and then/while Y").
- [[6-から]] — reason/cause clause ("because...").
- [[7-ましょうか-revisited]] — offering to do something for someone.

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `6-a-day-in-roberts-life/phrases/`: [[1-けっこうです]], [[2-ほんとうですか]], [[3-どうも-standalone]], [[4-お-prefix]], [[5-遅く-遅い]].
