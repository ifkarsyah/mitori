# Lesson 1 — あたらしいともだち New Friends

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 38–57)
Story context: Mary, an international student, meets new people on campus and introduces herself.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `1-new-friends/grammar/`
- [[1-x-は-y-です]] — the basic "X is Y" copula sentence, and its topic marker は.
- [[2-question-sentences]] — forming yes/no and content questions with か, question-word placement.
- [[3-noun-no-noun]] — the possessive/associative linking particle の ("noun₁'s noun₂", e.g. affiliation: 〜大学の学生).

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `1-new-friends/phrases/`: [[1-すみません]], [[2-ありがとうございます]] (+いいえ), [[3-あの]], [[4-ええ-はい|ええ／はい]], [[5-そうですか]], [[6-pronunciation-of-は]], [[7-self-introduction]].

**Vocabulary themes** — full notes in `1-new-friends/vocabulary/`: [[1-time-and-numbers]], [[2-identity-and-personal-info]], [[3-school-and-affiliation]], [[4-nationality-and-language]], [[5-countries]], [[6-majors]], [[7-occupations]], [[8-family]].

**CultureNote:** <ruby>日本人<rt>にほんじん</rt></ruby>の<ruby>名前<rt>なまえ</rt></ruby> — Japanese Names (p. 45). Family-name-first order, and honorific suffixes (さん etc.).

**Useful Expressions:** <ruby>時間<rt>じかん</rt></ruby>・<ruby>年<rt>とし</rt></ruby> — Time / Age (p. 57). Telling time and stating age as standalone reference vocab, ahead of the grammar for numbers/counters that comes later.
