# 〜すぎる

すぎる ("too much," "to excess") attaches to a verb stem, conjugating as a regular ru-verb.

<ruby>早<rt>はや</rt></ruby>く<ruby>起<rt>お</rt></ruby>きすぎました。
I got up too early.

<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べすぎてはいけません。
You must not eat too much.

## With adjectives — drop the final い／な
すぎる can also follow the "base" of an い- or な-adjective (the part that doesn't change across conjugations):

（<ruby>高<rt>たか</rt></ruby>い）　この<ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt></ruby>は高すぎます。
This book is too expensive.

（<ruby>静<rt>しず</rt></ruby>かな）　この<ruby>町<rt>まち</rt></ruby>は静かすぎます。
This town is too quiet.

## Notes
すぎる signals that something is beyond normal/proper — implying the speaker does *not* welcome it. <ruby>親切<rt>しんせつ</rt></ruby>すぎます ("too kind") is therefore not a straightforward compliment, unlike とても親切です／すごく親切です — use とても／すごく instead if you just mean "very," with no negative implication.
