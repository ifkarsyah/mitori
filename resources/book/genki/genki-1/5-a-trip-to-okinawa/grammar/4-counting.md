# Counting

Two key facts about counting items in Japanese: (1) different kinds of items use different number words ("counters") — people are counted differently from books, which are counted differently from flat objects; (2) the number word usually comes **after** the item counted, not before.

リーさんは<ruby>切手<rt>きって</rt></ruby>を<ruby>三枚<rt>さんまい</rt></ruby><ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>いました。
Lee bought three stamps.
　item　number

三枚 = the numeral 三 (three) + the counter <ruby>枚<rt>まい</rt></ruby>, used for sheets of paper and other flat objects (stamps, tickets, etc.).

## Notes
Other counters — for people, for books, for stick-like objects — are introduced in later lessons; this lesson only covers 枚.
