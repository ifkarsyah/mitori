# <ruby>忙<rt>いそが</rt></ruby>しいvs にぎやか（な）

Both can translate as "busy" in English, but they aren't interchangeable in Japanese.

忙しい describes **people** (or days) being busy:
たけしさんは忙しいです。
Takeshi is busy.

にぎやか（な）describes **places** being busy/lively:
<ruby>東京<rt>とうきょう</rt></ruby>はにぎやかです。
Tokyo is busy / lively.

## Notes
<ruby>日曜日<rt>にちようび</rt></ruby>は忙しいです。("I'm busy on Sunday") is fine even though it looks like it's describing a day — the subject "I" is simply omitted, and it's understood as 日曜日は（私は）忙しいです。
