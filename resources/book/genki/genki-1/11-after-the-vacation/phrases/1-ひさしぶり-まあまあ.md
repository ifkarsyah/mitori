# <ruby>久<rt>ひさ</rt></ruby>しぶり／まあまあ

## 久しぶり — it's been a long time
みちこ：メアリーさん、久しぶりですね。<ruby>休<rt>やす</rt></ruby>みはどうでしたか。
Michiko: Mary, I haven't seen you for a long time. How was your vacation?

Said on reuniting with someone you haven't seen in a while — "long time no see."

## まあまあ — okay, so-so
みちこ：まあまあでした。<ruby>一日<rt>いちにち</rt></ruby>だけドライブに行きましたが、<ruby>毎日<rt>まいにち</rt></ruby>アルバイトをしていました。
Michiko: It was okay. I went for a drive just for one day, but I was working part-time every day.

A middling, unenthusiastic evaluation — better than bad, not quite good.
