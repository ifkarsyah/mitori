# noun A や noun B

や connects two nouns, like と ([[8-と]]) does — but や signals that the listed items are just **examples**, not an exhaustive list.

A や B　＝　"A and B, for example"

<ruby>京都<rt>きょうと</rt></ruby>や<ruby>奈良<rt>なら</rt></ruby>に行きました。
I went to Kyoto and Nara (for example, and may have visited other places as well).

## Notes
Contrast directly with と: 京都と奈良に行きました implies those were the *only* two places visited. Swapping in や signals there could have been more — the same "example, not exhaustive list" spirit as [[6-どこかに-どこにも|か／も]] and [[2-たりたりする|たり〜たりする]] elsewhere in this unit of the book.
