# だれの noun

Asking who something belongs to. だれ is the question word for "who"; add the particle の (from Lesson 1's noun₁の noun₂) to get "whose."

これは　だれの　かばんですか。
Whose bag is this?

それは　スーさんの　かばんです。
That is Sue's bag.

## Notes
- Directly reuses the possessive use of の introduced in Lesson 1 (Takeshi's phone number, Mary's mother) — だれの is just that pattern with the question word だれ standing in for the possessor.
- Answering with じゃないです (negation) combines naturally: いいえ、メアリーさんの　<ruby>財布<rt>さいふ</rt></ruby>じゃないです。("No, it's not Mary's wallet.") — see [[6-noun-じゃないです]].
