# ここ／そこ／あそこ／どこ

The third こそあど set — words for **places** — following the same speaker/listener/far/unknown pattern as [[1-これ-それ-あれ-どれ]] and [[2-この-その-あの-どの-noun]].

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ここ | here, near me |
| そこ | there, near you |
| あそこ | over there |
| どこ | where |

## Asking and giving directions
すみません。<ruby>郵便局<rt>ゆうびんきょく</rt></ruby>は　どこですか。
Excuse me. Where is the post office?

（<ruby>郵便局<rt>ゆうびんきょく</rt></ruby>は）あそこです。
(The post office is) right over there.

## Notes
- Full, specific directions (turn left, go straight, etc.) are covered later, in Lesson 6.
