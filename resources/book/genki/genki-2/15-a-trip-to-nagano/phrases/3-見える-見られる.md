# 見える vs 見られる

<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>える is different from 見られる, the regular potential form of 見る ([[1-potential-verbs|potential verbs]]). 見える means something/someone is **spontaneously visible**; 見られる means the subject can see something/someone **actively**, rather than passively.

<ruby>部屋<rt>へや</rt></ruby>の<ruby>窓<rt>まど</rt></ruby>から<ruby>海<rt>うみ</rt></ruby>が見えます。
I can see the ocean from the window of the room. (spontaneous — the ocean is just there to be seen)

どこであの<ruby>映画<rt>えいが</rt></ruby>が見られますか。
Where can I see that movie? (active — seeking it out)

## 聞こえる vs 聞ける — the same distinction
<ruby>聞<rt>き</rt></ruby>こえる means something is **spontaneously audible**; <ruby>聞<rt>き</rt></ruby>ける, the potential form of 聞く, means the subject can actively hear/listen to the sound.

<ruby>今朝<rt>けさ</rt></ruby>、<ruby>鳥<rt>とり</rt></ruby>の<ruby>声<rt>こえ</rt></ruby>が聞こえました。
This morning, I heard the voices of the birds. (spontaneous)

インターネットで<ruby>日本<rt>にほん</rt></ruby>のラジオ<ruby>番組<rt>ばんぐみ</rt></ruby>が聞けます。
We can listen to Japanese radio programs on the Internet. (active)
