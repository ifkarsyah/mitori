# Transitivity Pairs

**Transitive** verbs describe a person acting on a thing (I open the door, you turn on the TV). **Intransitive** verbs describe changes things/people undergo on their own (the door opens, the TV goes on). Most verbs are "loners" with no counterpart of the opposite transitivity, but several important verbs come in matched pairs.

| Transitive | | Intransitive | |
|---|---|---|---|
| <ruby>開<rt>あ</rt></ruby>ける | open something | <ruby>開<rt>あ</rt></ruby>く | something opens |
| <ruby>閉<rt>し</rt></ruby>める | close something | <ruby>閉<rt>し</rt></ruby>まる | something closes |
| <ruby>入<rt>い</rt></ruby>れる | put something in | <ruby>入<rt>はい</rt></ruby>る | something goes inside |
| <ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す | take something out | <ruby>出<rt>で</rt></ruby>る | something goes out |
| つける | turn something on | つく | something goes on |
| <ruby>消<rt>け</rt></ruby>す | turn something off; extinguish something | <ruby>消<rt>き</rt></ruby>える | something goes off |
| <ruby>壊<rt>こわ</rt></ruby>す | break something | <ruby>壊<rt>こわ</rt></ruby>れる | something breaks |
| <ruby>汚<rt>よご</rt></ruby>す | make something dirty | <ruby>汚<rt>よご</rt></ruby>れる | something becomes dirty |
| <ruby>落<rt>お</rt></ruby>とす | drop something | <ruby>落<rt>お</rt></ruby>ちる | something drops |
| <ruby>沸<rt>わ</rt></ruby>かす | boil water | <ruby>沸<rt>わ</rt></ruby>く | water boils |

## Argument structure
Transitive verbs need both a subject (agent) and an object (the thing acted on). Intransitive verbs need only a subject (the thing/person that changes).

たけしさんが<ruby>電気<rt>でんき</rt></ruby>をつけました。（transitive）
Takeshi turned the light on.

電気がつきました。（intransitive）
The light went on.

たけしさんがお<ruby>湯<rt>ゆ</rt></ruby>を沸かしました。
Takeshi boiled the water.

お湯が沸きました。
The water boiled.

## With ている — action in progress vs. resulting state
Recall from [[1-ている]]: activity verbs ＋ ている describe an action in progress; change verbs ＋ ている describe the state resulting from a change. Transitivity pairs follow that same split — transitive members are activity verbs; intransitive members are change verbs.

ロバートさんは<ruby>窓<rt>まど</rt></ruby>を開けています。
Robert is opening the windows. (action in progress)

ドアが開いています。
Doors are open. / There's an open door. (resulting state)

ともこさんは電気を消しています。
Tomoko is turning the light off. (action in progress)

テレビは消えています。
The TV set is off. (resulting state)

ゴジラが<ruby>町<rt>まち</rt></ruby>を壊しています。
There goes Godzilla, destroying the city. (action in progress)

このパソコンは壊れています。
This computer is broken. (resulting state)
