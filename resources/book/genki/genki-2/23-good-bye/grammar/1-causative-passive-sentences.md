# Causative-passive Sentences

The passive of the causative — use when you were **made to do**, or harassed/talked into doing, something you didn't want to do.

（<ruby>下手<rt>へた</rt></ruby>だから<ruby>歌<rt>うた</rt></ruby>いたくなかったのに）歌を歌わされました。
(I didn't want to sing because I'm not a good singer, but) I was forced to sing.

（きらいだから<ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べたくないんですが、いつも）<ruby>肉<rt>にく</rt></ruby>を食べさせられます。
(I don't want to eat it because I don't like meat, but) I am (always) made to eat meat.

## Formation
1. **ru-verbs**: drop -る, add -saseraれる — <ruby>食<rt>た</rt></ruby>べる → 食べさせられる
2. **u-verbs ending in す**: drop -u, add -aseraれる — <ruby>話<rt>はな</rt></ruby>す → 話させられる
3. **all other u-verbs**: drop -u, add -asaれる (shorter than the sum of causative + passive) — <ruby>書<rt>か</rt></ruby>く → 書かされる、<ruby>読<rt>よ</rt></ruby>む → 読まされる、<ruby>泳<rt>およ</rt></ruby>ぐ → 泳がされる、<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>う → 買わされる、<ruby>立<rt>た</rt></ruby>つ → 立たされる、<ruby>取<rt>と</rt></ruby>る → 取らされる、<ruby>遊<rt>あそ</rt></ruby>ぶ → 遊ばされる
4. **irregular**: する → させられる、くる → こさせられる

(The more transparently combinative -aserareる form, like 書かせられる, is grammatical but far less common than the -aされる form, 書かされる.)

## Basic structure
<ruby>私<rt>わたし</rt></ruby>は　<ruby>彼女<rt>かのじょ</rt></ruby>に　<ruby>車<rt>くるま</rt></ruby>を<ruby>洗<rt>あら</rt></ruby>わされました。
（puppet）は　（puppet master）に　（action, causative-passive verb）
I was tricked by my girlfriend into washing her car.

The "puppet" is forced into the action — marked は／が. The "puppet master" wields power over/manipulates the puppet — marked に. The "action" forced upon the puppet is a causative-passive verb.

## Actors switch, relative to the causative
| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Causative-passive | 私は | <ruby>友<rt>とも</rt></ruby>だちに | <ruby>宿題<rt>しゅくだい</rt></ruby>を<ruby>手伝<rt>てつだ</rt></ruby>わされました。("I was forced by my friend into helping him with his homework.") |
| Causative | 友だちは | 私に | 宿題を手伝わせました。("My friend made me help him with his homework.") |

## Compare with a plain sentence
Add the "puppet master" role to a plain, non-causative sentence and lengthen the verb, and you get a causative-passive sentence — same subject throughout.

Causative-passive: ゆみは　お<ruby>母<rt>かあ</rt></ruby>さんに　<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>させられました。("Yumi was ordered by her mother to study.")
Plain: ゆみは　　　　勉強しました。("Yumi studied.")
