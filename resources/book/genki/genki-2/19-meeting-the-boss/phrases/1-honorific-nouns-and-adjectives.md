# Honorific forms of nouns and adjectives

Some nouns and adjectives become honorific by adding the prefix お or ご — お for native Japanese words, ご for words borrowed from Chinese.

お〜：お<ruby>名前<rt>なまえ</rt></ruby>、お<ruby>仕事<rt>しごと</rt></ruby>、お<ruby>好<rt>す</rt></ruby>き、お<ruby>元気<rt>げんき</rt></ruby>、おたばこ、お<ruby>忙<rt>いそが</rt></ruby>しい
ご〜：ご<ruby>両親<rt>りょうしん</rt></ruby>、ご<ruby>兄弟<rt>きょうだい</rt></ruby>、ご<ruby>親切<rt>しんせつ</rt></ruby>、ご<ruby>主人<rt>しゅじん</rt></ruby>、ご<ruby>病気<rt>びょうき</rt></ruby>

## Some words are replaced entirely
| Plain | Honorific |
|---|---|
| <ruby>家<rt>いえ</rt></ruby> | お<ruby>宅<rt>たく</rt></ruby> |
| <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby> | お<ruby>子<rt>こ</rt></ruby>さん |
| だれ | どなた／どちら<ruby>様<rt>さま</rt></ruby> |
| どこ | どちら |
| どうですか | いかがですか |

## Never for yourself, your family, or your own group
These words/expressions cannot describe yourself, your family, or the group you belong to.

A：ご両親はお元気ですか。
How are your parents?
B：はい。おかげさまで元気です。
Yes, thanks to you, they are fine. (×お元気です would be wrong — never applied to your own family)
