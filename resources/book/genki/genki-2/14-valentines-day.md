# Lesson 14 — バレンタインデー Valentine's Day

## Conversation & Grammar (pp. 50–73)
Story context: Mary knits Takeshi a sweater for Valentine's Day; the next day John compares notes on who got what (Robert did suspiciously well) and learns about White Day, the March 14th "return the favor" custom for men.

**Grammar points** — full notes in `14-valentines-day/grammar/`
- [[1-ほしい]] — "(I) want (something)," a private predicate restricted to the speaker, with がる for describing others.
- [[2-かもしれません]] — "might/may be," a softer, less committal cousin of でしょう.
- [[3-あげる-くれる-もらう]] — the giving/receiving verb triad, chosen by direction relative to the speaker.
- [[4-たらどうですか]] — giving advice/suggestions ("why don't you...?").
- [[5-number-も-number-しか]] — number＋も ("as many as") vs. number＋しか＋negative ("as few as; only").

**Notable phrases** — full notes in `14-valentines-day/phrases/`: [[1-casual-short-forms]] (question-particle dropping, んだ／の, exclamatory わあ／へえ／なあ), [[2-できる-meanings]] (できる's several senses).
