## Extracted

### Verb
- 拭く (ふく) — to wipe
- こぼす (こぼす) — to spill
- 怒られる (おこられる) — to get scolded
- おごる (おごる) — to treat (someone), pay for someone
- 追加する (ついかする) — to add, order an extra (item)
- 出す (だす) — to serve, put out; also "to pay/cover" (お金を出す)

### Noun
- おしぼり — wet hand towel (given at restaurants)
- お通し (おとおし) — small starter dish automatically served at izakayas
- 串 (くし) — skewer
- 生ビール／生中 (なまビール／なまちゅう) — draft beer / a medium-size draft beer
- 塩コショウ (しおコショウ) — salt and pepper
- タレ — sauce (savory, e.g. for yakitori)
- 砂肝 (すなぎも) — chicken gizzard
- 豚足 (とんそく) — pig's feet

### Adjective
- おいしい — delicious
- なみなみ (な) — filled to the brim
- っぽい — "-like" / "typical of" (suffix, e.g. 居酒屋っぽい "izakaya-like")

### Conjunction
- とりあえず — "for now" / "first of all"
- やっぱり — "after all" / "as expected"

### Phrase
- 乾杯！ (かんぱい) — "Cheers!"
- おつかれさまです — "Thanks for your hard work" (casual greeting among friends/colleagues)
- ごちそうさまです — "Thank you for the meal"
- マナーが悪い — "(that's) bad manners"
- こぼさないと、怒られちゃう — "If you don't spill some, you'll get scolded" (sake-pouring custom — filling to overflowing is a sign of generosity)
