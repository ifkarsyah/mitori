### Other Ways to Ask About Allergies

卵アレルギー以外のアレルギーがある人は、例えば:
For allergies other than egg, for example:

すみません、私甲殻類アレルギーがあるんですけど、甲殻類アレルギーの人でも食べられるものはありますか?
"Excuse me, I have a shellfish allergy — is there anything people with a shellfish allergy can eat?"

すみません、私、小麦アレルギーがあるんですけど、小麦アレルギーの人でも食べられるものはありますか?
"Excuse me, I have a wheat allergy — is there anything people with a wheat allergy can eat?"

*(Narration in English): But many Japanese people are not so familiar with gluten-free food and celiac disease, so you might need to explain more, like:*

小麦が入っている醤油とかも、だめなんです。
"...things like soy sauce, which contains wheat, are also no good for me."

*(Narration in English): Another way to ask about allergies is:*

すみません、私、ナッツアレルギーがあるんですけど、これはナッツが入っていますか?
"Excuse me, I have a nut allergy — does this have nuts in it?"
