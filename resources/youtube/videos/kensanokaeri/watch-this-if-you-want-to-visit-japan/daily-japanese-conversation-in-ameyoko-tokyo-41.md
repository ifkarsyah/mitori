### Verb
- 履く (はく) — to wear (on the feet/legs — shoes, pants)
- 売る (うる) — to sell

- 投稿する (とうこうする) — to post (a video, online content)
- 付き合う (つきあう) — to accompany, keep someone company

### Noun
- 商店街 (しょうてんがい) — shopping street
- 下町 (したまち) — old downtown, traditional working-class neighborhood
- 活気 (かっき) — liveliness, energy (of a place)
- 海鮮丼 (かいせんどん) — seafood rice bowl
- 緑茶 (りょくちゃ) — green tea
- センス — sense, taste (e.g. fashion sense)

### Adjective
- 新鮮 (しんせん) — fresh
- 珍しい (めずらしい) — unusual, rare
- 濃い (こい) — strong, thick (flavor, color)
- おしゃれ — stylish, fashionable

### Conjunction
- っていうか — "well, I mean" / "or rather" (casual, correcting or rephrasing)
- なんか — "kind of" / "somehow" (casual filler)

### Phrase
- テンションが上がる — "to get pumped up / excited"
- せっかくだから — "since we're here / since we have the chance"
- 生き返る — "I feel alive again" (lit. "to come back to life")
- お楽しみに！ — "Look forward to it!"
