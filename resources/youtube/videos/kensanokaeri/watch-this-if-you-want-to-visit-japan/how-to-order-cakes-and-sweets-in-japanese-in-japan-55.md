## Extracted

### Verb
- 添える (そえる) — to add, accompany (e.g. a message alongside a gift)
- 挟む (はさむ) — to sandwich, insert between
- 予約する (よやくする) — to reserve, book in advance
- 変わる (かわる) — to change
- 販売する (はんばいする) — to sell, put on sale

### Noun
- 季節限定 (きせつげんてい) — seasonal-only, limited to the season
- 人気 (にんき) — popularity
- 材料 (ざいりょう) — ingredients, materials
- ろうそく — candle
- 手提げ袋 (てさげぶくろ) — carrying bag, tote bag
- レジ袋 (レジぶくろ) — plastic shopping bag
- お会計 (おかいけい) — the bill, total
- 有料 (ゆうりょう) — paid, chargeable (not free)

### Adjective
- 人気がある (にんきがある) — popular
- きれい — pretty, clean
- かわいい — cute

### Conjunction
- やっぱり — after all, as expected (used when reverting to a choice)
- そしたら — "so then" / "in that case" (casual)

### Phrase
- お決まりになったら、お声かけくださいね — "Please let us know when you've decided" (said by cake shop staff)
- どれになさいますか？ — "Which one would you like?" (polite)
- 気をつけた方がいい — "(something) you should be careful about"
- お預かりします — "I'll take that / receive that from you" (used by staff for payment or items)
