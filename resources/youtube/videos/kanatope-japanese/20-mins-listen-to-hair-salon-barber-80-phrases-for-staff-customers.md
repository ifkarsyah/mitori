## Extracted

### Verb
- 預かる (あずかる) — to keep/hold something for someone
- 整える (ととのえる) — to trim, to tidy up
- 梳く (すく) — to thin out (hair)
- 結ぶ (むすぶ) — to tie up
- 倒す (たおす) — to recline, to tilt back
- しみる — to sting
- 流す (ながす) — to rinse
- つける — to apply (e.g. oil, wax)
- 巻く (まく) — to curl (with an iron)
- 刈り上げる (かりあげる) — to buzz/shave close
- 残す (のこす) — to leave (some length)

### Noun
- 毛先 (けさき) — hair ends
- 前髪 (まえがみ) — bangs
- 後れ毛 (おくれげ) — loose side strands, side fringe
- 分け目 (わけめ) — hair part
- 襟足 (えりあし) — nape hairline
- 力加減 (ちからかげん) — pressure/strength level
- ツーブロック — "two-block" hairstyle (undercut)
- 刈り上げ (かりあげ) — buzzed/shaved-up section
- ひげそり — shave
- ロッカー — locker
- 貴重品 (きちょうひん) — valuables

### Adjective
- 明るめ (あかるめ) — on the brighter side
- 落ち着いた (おちついた) — calm, subtle
- 強め (つよめ) — on the firmer/stronger side
- 弱め (よわめ) — on the softer/weaker side
- かゆい — itchy
- ぬるい — lukewarm

### Conjunction
- じゃあ — well then, so
- それとも — or, alternatively
- で — and (then) (casual connector)

### Phrase
- どうされますか? — how would you like [it]? (polite)
- いつも通りで — the usual, same as always
- そのままでお願いします — please keep it as is
- 〜くらいで — about that much, around ~
- 大丈夫ですか? — is that okay?
- 〜ぐらいになると思うんですけど — I think it'll be about ~
- どうしますか? — what would you like to do?
- こんな感じでいかがですか? — how about something like this?
- お待ちいただくことになりそうですけど、大丈夫ですか? — it looks like you'll have to wait, is that okay?
