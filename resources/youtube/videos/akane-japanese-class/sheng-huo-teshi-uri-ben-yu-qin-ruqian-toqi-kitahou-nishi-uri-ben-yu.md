## Extracted

### Verb
- 下げる (さげる) — to lower (e.g. blinds)
- 外す (はずす) — to take off, remove (contacts, glasses, earrings, necklace)
- 拭く (ふく) — to wipe
- 結ぶ (むすぶ) — to tie (up hair)
- ゆすぐ — to rinse
- 落とす (おとす) — to remove (here: makeup)
- 塗る (ぬる) — to apply, spread on
- 溜める (ためる) — to fill up, collect (water in a tub)
- 乾かす (かわかす) — to dry
- 畳む (たたむ) — to fold
- とかす — to comb (hair)
- つける — to attach, put on (dictionary form; contrasted with ます形 in the video)

### Noun
- 洗面台 (せんめんだい) — washbasin, sink
- コンタクトレンズ — contact lenses
- 歯磨き粉 (はみがきこ) — toothpaste
- クレンジングオイル — cleansing oil
- すっぴん — a bare face, no makeup
- 浴槽 (よくそう) — bathtub
- 栓 (せん) — plug, stopper (お風呂の栓 "bath plug")
- 化粧水 (けしょうすい) — toner, lotion
- まぶた — eyelid
- 耳栓 (みみせん) — earplugs
- 下地 (したじ) — makeup primer/base

### Adjective
- 邪魔 (じゃま) — in the way, a hindrance
- 適当 (てきとう) — rough, sloppy, done carelessly (here: 適当につける "put on roughly")

### Conjunction
- それでは / では — well then, so
- そして — and, then
- でも — but, however
- なんか — like, kind of (casual filler)

### Phrase
- おやすみなさい — "Good night" (said before sleeping)
- おはようございます — "Good morning"
- 〜とも言います — "It's also called ~"
- 予想してみてください — "Try to guess/predict"
- しょうがないですね — "It can't be helped" / "Oh well"
- 辞書形は〜です — "The dictionary form is ~" (grammar aside)
