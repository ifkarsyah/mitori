# Kochi

Kochi offers the scenic Katsurahama beach, seared bonito (katsuo) cuisine, sites tied to Ryoma Sakamoto's role in ending the shogunate, and the clear waters of the Shimanto River.

## Cities

- **Aki** — Known as the training ground of Hanshin Tigers baseball and citrus farming.
- **Kochi** — The prefectural capital, known for Katsurahama beach and seared bonito cuisine.
- **Nankoku** — Home to Kochi's airport and rural farming plains.
- **Sukumo** — A small fishing port on Shikoku's southwestern tip.
