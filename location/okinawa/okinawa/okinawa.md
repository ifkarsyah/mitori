# Okinawa

Okinawa Prefecture covers the main island and outlying chains, drawing visitors with the Churaumi Aquarium, Shuri Castle's restored Ryukyu-era architecture, and some of Japan's best diving and snorkeling. Local specialties include Okinawa soba, goya champuru, and awamori liquor.

## Cities

- **Itoman** — Known for its fishing port and as the site of the final WWII battle of Okinawa.
- **Nago** — Home to the Churaumi Aquarium and northern Okinawa's beach resorts.
- **Naha** — Okinawa's capital, home to Shuri Castle and the lively Kokusai-dori shopping street.
- **Okinawa** — A city built up around US military bases, distinct from Naha despite the shared prefecture name.
- **Uruma** — Known for its offshore islands connected by scenic sea bridges.
