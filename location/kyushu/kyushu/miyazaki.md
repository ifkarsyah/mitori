# Miyazaki

Miyazaki offers the mythology-steeped Takachiho Gorge, laid-back surfing beaches, a warm subtropical climate, and some of Japan's best mangoes.

## Cities

- **Miyakonojo** — Known for shochu distilling and Japan's largest wooden torii gate nearby.
- **Miyazaki** — The prefectural capital, known for its warm subtropical climate and surfing beaches.
- **Nichinan** — Home to the coastal Aoshima Island and Cape Toi's wild horses.
- **Nobeoka** — A chemical industry city on Kyushu's east coast.
