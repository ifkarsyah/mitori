# Fukuoka

Fukuoka is Kyushu's gateway city, famous for Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen, open-air yatai food stalls, and Dazaifu Tenmangu, a major shrine dedicated to the god of learning.

## Cities

- **Dazaifu** — Home to Dazaifu Tenmangu, a major shrine dedicated to the god of learning.
- **Fukuoka** — Kyushu's largest city, famous for Hakata tonkotsu ramen and open-air yatai food stalls.
- **Kitakyushu** — A historic industrial city formed from five merged cities, once central to Japan's steel industry.
- **Kurume** — Known as the birthplace of tonkotsu ramen and for kurume kasuri textile weaving.
- **Yanagawa** — Known for its canal boat rides and eel cuisine.
