# Nagasaki

A historic trading port shaped by centuries of Dutch and Chinese contact, Nagasaki is home to the Peace Park, the Huis Ten Bosch theme park, champon noodles, and the atmospheric ruins of Gunkanjima island.

## Cities

- **Hirado** — An early foreign trading port with a mix of Dutch, Portuguese, and Christian heritage.
- **Nagasaki** — The prefectural capital, shaped by centuries of Dutch and Chinese trade, home to the Peace Park.
- **Sasebo** — A major naval port city and home to the Huis Ten Bosch Dutch-themed park.
- **Shimabara** — Known for its castle and the volcanic history of nearby Mount Unzen.
- **Unzen** — A hot spring resort town on the slopes of an active volcano.
