# Tokushima

Tokushima hosts the exuberant Awa Odori dance festival, the tidal whirlpools of Naruto, a centuries-old indigo dyeing tradition, and the remote vine bridges of the Iya Valley.

## Cities

- **Anan** — A coastal city known for surfing beaches and its LED industry.
- **Miyoshi** — Home to the remote vine bridges and gorge scenery of the Iya Valley.
- **Naruto** — Known for the powerful tidal whirlpools in the strait between Shikoku and Awaji.
- **Tokushima** — The prefectural capital, famous for the Awa Odori dance festival each August.
