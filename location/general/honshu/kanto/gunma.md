# Gunma

Gunma is a hot spring destination centered on Kusatsu Onsen and Ikaho Onsen, with Mount Haruna offering scenic hiking and views. The prefecture is also a leading producer of konnyaku (konjac).

## Cities

- **Isesaki** — A textile-industry city in the Kanto plain.
- **Maebashi** — The prefectural capital, historically tied to Japan's silk industry.
- **Numata** — A highland farming city and gateway to Gunma's ski and onsen areas including Kusatsu.
- **Ota** — An automotive manufacturing hub, long home to Subaru production.
- **Takasaki** — Known for the giant Byakue Kannon statue and daruma doll craft.
