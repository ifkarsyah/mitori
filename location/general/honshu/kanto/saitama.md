# Saitama

Just north of Tokyo, Saitama is known for Kawagoe's well-preserved 'Little Edo' old town and Chichibu's shibazakura moss phlox fields and lively night festival, alongside its role as a major commuter hub for the capital.

## Cities

- **Chichibu** — Known for its shibazakura moss phlox fields and lively winter night festival.
- **Kawagoe** — Known as 'Little Edo' for its preserved merchant-era streetscape.
- **Kawaguchi** — A manufacturing city historically known for cast-iron casting, just north of Tokyo.
- **Saitama** — The prefectural capital, formed by merging Urawa and Omiya, and home to Saitama Super Arena.
- **Tokorozawa** — Home to Japan's first airfield and the sprawling Sayama hills.
