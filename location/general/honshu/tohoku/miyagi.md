# Miyagi

Centered on the city of Sendai, Miyagi is famous for grilled gyutan beef tongue, Matsushima Bay's pine-covered islets (one of Japan's three great scenic views), and the Zao ski resort with its frost-covered 'snow monster' trees.

## Cities

- **Ishinomaki** — A port city and manga hometown of Shotaro Ishinomori, rebuilt after the 2011 tsunami.
- **Kesennuma** — A Sanriku fishing port renowned for shark and saury catches.
- **Sendai** — Tohoku's largest city, known for grilled gyutan beef tongue and its tree-lined streets.
- **Shiogama** — A small port city famous for its fresh tuna and views over Matsushima Bay.
- **Tome** — A rice-farming city in Miyagi's interior, known for wetlands and migratory swans.
