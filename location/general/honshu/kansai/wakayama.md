# Wakayama

Wakayama offers the mountaintop Buddhist temple town of Koyasan with its temple lodgings, the Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trails, and the beaches and hot springs of Shirahama.

## Cities

- **Hashimoto** — A gateway town to the sacred mountain temple complex of Koyasan.
- **Shingu** — Home to Kumano Hayatama Taisha, one of the three grand Kumano shrines.
- **Tanabe** — Gateway to the Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trails and Shirahama's beaches and hot springs.
- **Wakayama** — The prefectural capital, known for Wakayama Castle and fresh citrus.
