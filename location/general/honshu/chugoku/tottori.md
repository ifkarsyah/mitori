# Tottori

Tottori is known for Japan's largest sand dunes, sweet pears, views of Mount Daisen, and its ties to manga creators, celebrated with Conan and Kitaro-themed attractions.

## Cities

- **Kurayoshi** — Known for its preserved white-walled warehouse district.
- **Sakaiminato** — A fishing port famous for crab and its Kitaro manga-themed shopping street.
- **Tottori** — The prefectural capital, famous for Japan's largest sand dunes.
- **Yonago** — Gateway to Mount Daisen, western Japan's tallest peak.
