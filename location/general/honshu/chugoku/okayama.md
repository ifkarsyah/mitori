# Okayama

Okayama features Korakuen, one of Japan's three great gardens, the canal-lined merchant district of Kurashiki, the folklore of Momotaro, and a growing reputation for denim production.

## Cities

- **Ibara** — A quieter city known for its townscape of preserved Edo-era buildings.
- **Kurashiki** — Famous for its canal-lined Bikan historical quarter and denim production.
- **Okayama** — The prefectural capital, home to Korakuen, one of Japan's three great gardens.
- **Tsuyama** — Known for its castle ruins park, one of Japan's top cherry blossom spots.
