# Yamaguchi

Yamaguchi is famous for fugu pufferfish cuisine, the arched Kintai Bridge, the vast Akiyoshido limestone cave, and the striking red torii gates of Motonosumi Inari Shrine along the coast.

## Cities

- **Hagi** — A preserved castle town known as the birthplace of key Meiji Restoration figures.
- **Iwakuni** — Home to the distinctive five-arched Kintai Bridge.
- **Shimonoseki** — Famous for fugu pufferfish cuisine and the Kanmon Straits facing Kyushu.
- **Yamaguchi** — The prefectural capital, known for its Zen temples and Christian mission history.
