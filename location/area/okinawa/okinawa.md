# Okinawa

Okinawa region matches the prefecture and island, centered on beach resorts, diving spots, and the historic Ryukyu Kingdom sites around Shuri Castle. Local culture blends distinct cuisine, music, and a slower pace of life shaped by centuries of trade with China and Southeast Asia.

## Prefectures

- **Okinawa** — Okinawa Prefecture covers the main island and outlying chains, drawing visitors with the Churaumi Aquarium, Shuri Castle's restored Ryukyu-era architecture, and some of Japan's best diving and snorkeling.
