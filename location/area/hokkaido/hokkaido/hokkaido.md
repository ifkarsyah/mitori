# Hokkaido

Hokkaido Prefecture covers the entire island, centered on the capital Sapporo with its ramen alleys, beer museums, and winter Snow Festival. Popular draws include Otaru's canal townscape, Niseko's powder skiing, Furano's lavender fields, and abundant fresh seafood like crab, uni, and salmon.

## Cities

- **Asahikawa** — Hokkaido's second-largest city, home to Asahiyama Zoo and a gateway to nearby ski resorts and hot springs.
- **Furano** — A farming town famous for its rolling lavender fields, ski slopes, and scenic patchwork hills.
- **Hakodate** — A historic port city famed for its night view from Mount Hakodate, the star-shaped Goryokaku fort, and a morning seafood market.
- **Kushiro** — A fishing port on Hokkaido's eastern coast known for the Kushiro Wetlands, red-crowned cranes, and grilled seafood.
- **Otaru** — A charming canal town near Sapporo known for glassware, sushi, and its preserved Meiji-era warehouse district.
- **Sapporo** — Hokkaido's capital and Japan's fifth-largest city, known for the winter Snow Festival, Sapporo Beer, and miso ramen.
