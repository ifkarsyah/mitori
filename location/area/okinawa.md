# Okinawa

A subtropical chain of islands with a distinct Ryukyuan culture and history separate from mainland Japan. Known for turquoise beaches, coral reefs, Shuri Castle, unique cuisine like goya champuru, and one of the highest life expectancies in the world.

## Regions

- **Okinawa** — Okinawa region matches the prefecture and island, centered on beach resorts, diving spots, and the historic Ryukyu Kingdom sites around Shuri Castle.
