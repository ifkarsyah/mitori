# Aichi

Centered on Nagoya, Aichi is a manufacturing powerhouse and home to Toyota, alongside a distinctive food culture built around miso katsu and hitsumabushi grilled eel, plus a rebuilt Nagoya Castle.

## Cities

- **Ichinomiya** — A textile manufacturing city and home to Owari Province's leading shrine.
- **Inuyama** — Home to one of Japan's oldest original castles, overlooking the Kiso River.
- **Nagoya** — Aichi's capital and a major manufacturing hub, known for miso katsu and its rebuilt castle.
- **Okazaki** — Birthplace of shogun Tokugawa Ieyasu, with a reconstructed castle and hatcho miso brewing.
- **Toyota** — Named after and home to Toyota Motor Corporation's headquarters and factories.
