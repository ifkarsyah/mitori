# Niigata

Niigata is celebrated for Koshihikari rice and sake, the ski slopes of Echigo-Yuzawa, and Sado Island's history of gold mining and folk culture. Heavy snowfall defines much of the prefecture's winter landscape.

## Cities

- **Joetsu** — A historic castle town tied to the samurai lord Uesugi Kenshin, with heavy snowfall.
- **Nagaoka** — Famous for one of Japan's largest fireworks festivals held every August.
- **Niigata** — The prefectural capital, a rice- and sake-producing port city on the Sea of Japan.
- **Sado** — An island city known for its former gold mine and traditional taiko drumming.
