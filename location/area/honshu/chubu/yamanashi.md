# Yamanashi

Yamanashi offers some of the best views of Mount Fuji from the Fuji Five Lakes area, along with the Shosenkyo Gorge, and vineyards and orchards that make it a center for Japanese wine, grapes, and peaches.

## Cities

- **Chuo** — A small city in the Kofu basin known for farming.
- **Fuefuki** — Famous for its peach and grape orchards, especially around Isawa Onsen.
- **Fujiyoshida** — Gateway city to Mount Fuji's Yoshida Trail and the Fuji Five Lakes.
- **Hokuto** — Known for clear mountain air, sunshine hours, and views of the Yatsugatake range.
- **Kai** — A residential city near Kofu known for orchards.
- **Kofu** — The prefectural capital, a wine-producing city in a basin ringed by mountains including Mount Fuji.
- **Koshu** — Japan's oldest wine-producing area, known for Katsunuma's vineyards.
- **Minami-Alps** — Named for the Southern Japan Alps it borders, known for fruit farming.
- **Nirasaki** — Known for its rural scenery beneath the Southern Alps and Nanko shrine.
- **Otsuki** — A mountain gateway city known for Sasago Pass along the old Koshu Kaido road.
- **Tsuru** — Known for its textile industry and access to the Fuji Five Lakes area.
- **Uenohara** — A mountainous city known for silk and textile history.
- **Yamanashi** — Known for peach and grape orchards and traditional crystal carving.
