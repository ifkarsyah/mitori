# Gifu

Gifu is home to the UNESCO-listed thatched-roof village of Shirakawa-go, the well-preserved old town and morning markets of Takayama, and the relaxing hot spring town of Gero Onsen.

## Cities

- **Gifu** — The prefectural capital, known for traditional cormorant fishing on the Nagara River.
- **Ogaki** — Known as the endpoint of the historic Nakasendo and Basho's Oku no Hosomichi journey.
- **Seki** — Historically renowned for centuries of Japanese swordsmithing.
- **Takayama** — Famous for its beautifully preserved old town and twice-yearly Takayama Festival.
