# Toyama

Toyama is known for the dramatic Kurobe Gorge and the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route's towering spring snow walls, as well as fresh seafood like firefly squid and white shrimp, and the thatched-roof villages of Gokayama.

## Cities

- **Kurobe** — Gateway to the Kurobe Gorge and the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route's snow walls.
- **Nanto** — Home to the thatched-roof mountain village of Gokayama.
- **Takaoka** — Known for centuries-old bronze and copperware craftsmanship.
- **Toyama** — The prefectural capital, known for firefly squid, glassware, and views of the Tateyama mountains.
