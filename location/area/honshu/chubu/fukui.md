# Fukui

Fukui is home to a major dinosaur museum in Katsuyama, the dramatic Tojinbo cliffs, the historic Eiheiji Zen training temple, and prized echizen crab in winter.

## Cities

- **Fukui** — The prefectural capital, known for Echizen crab and Eiheiji Zen temple nearby.
- **Katsuyama** — Home to Japan's largest dinosaur museum and major fossil excavation sites.
- **Sabae** — Japan's center of eyeglass frame manufacturing.
- **Tsuruga** — A Sea of Japan port city with a history as a gateway for European trade.
