# Chubu

Central Honshu, dominated by the Japanese Alps and Mount Fuji. Known for ski resorts in Nagano, the historic Nakasendo post towns of Magome and Tsumago, Takayama's old town, Kanazawa's Kenrokuen garden, and Aichi's manufacturing hub Nagoya.

## Prefectures

- **Aichi** — Centered on Nagoya, Aichi is a manufacturing powerhouse and home to Toyota, alongside a distinctive food culture built around miso katsu and hitsumabushi grilled eel, plus a rebuilt Nagoya Castle.
- **Fukui** — Fukui is home to a major dinosaur museum in Katsuyama, the dramatic Tojinbo cliffs, the historic Eiheiji Zen training temple, and prized echizen crab in winter.
- **Gifu** — Gifu is home to the UNESCO-listed thatched-roof village of Shirakawa-go, the well-preserved old town and morning markets of Takayama, and the relaxing hot spring town of Gero Onsen.
- **Ishikawa** — Ishikawa's capital Kanazawa is famed for the Kenrokuen garden, gold leaf crafts, the geisha district of Higashi Chaya, and fresh seafood at Omicho Market, having largely escaped WWII bombing to preserve its Edo-era charm.
- **Nagano** — Set in the Japanese Alps, Nagano is known for ski resorts like Hakuba, Matsumoto Castle, the ancient temple Zenko-ji, snow monkeys bathing in hot springs at Jigokudani, and soba noodle culture.
- **Niigata** — Niigata is celebrated for Koshihikari rice and sake, the ski slopes of Echigo-Yuzawa, and Sado Island's history of gold mining and folk culture.
- **Shizuoka** — Shizuoka offers classic views of Mount Fuji, rolling green tea plantations, and the hot springs and coastline of the Izu Peninsula.
- **Toyama** — Toyama is known for the dramatic Kurobe Gorge and the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route's towering spring snow walls, as well as fresh seafood like firefly squid and white shrimp, and the thatched-roof villages of Gokayama.
- **Yamanashi** — Yamanashi offers some of the best views of Mount Fuji from the Fuji Five Lakes area, along with the Shosenkyo Gorge, and vineyards and orchards that make it a center for Japanese wine, grapes, and peaches.
