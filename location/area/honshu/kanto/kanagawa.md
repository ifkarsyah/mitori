# Kanagawa

Kanagawa features Yokohama's Chinatown and waterfront skyline, Kamakura's Great Buddha and temple-lined hiking trails, the hot spring resort town of Hakone with views of Mount Fuji, and the coastal island of Enoshima.

## Cities

- **Fujisawa** — Home to Enoshima island and popular surfing beaches along the Shonan coast.
- **Kamakura** — Japan's medieval capital, famous for the Great Buddha and dozens of Zen temples.
- **Kawasaki** — An industrial city between Tokyo and Yokohama, also known for its lively Halloween parade.
- **Odawara** — Known for its reconstructed castle and as the historic gateway to Hakone.
- **Yokohama** — Japan's second-largest city, known for its historic port, Chinatown, and waterfront skyline.
- **Yokosuka** — A naval port city with a long history as a US and Japanese naval base.
