# Ibaraki

Ibaraki is best known for the Kairakuen plum garden, one of Japan's three great gardens, and Hitachi Seaside Park's spring nemophila flower fields. It is also Japan's largest producer of natto fermented soybeans.

## Cities

- **Hitachi** — An industrial city built around Hitachi's manufacturing roots, with seaside cherry blossom cliffs.
- **Mito** — The prefectural capital, home to Kairakuen, one of Japan's three great gardens.
- **Tsuchiura** — Known for cycling routes around Lake Kasumigaura and its autumn fireworks competition.
- **Tsukuba** — A planned science city with Japan's largest concentration of research institutes and JAXA facilities.
