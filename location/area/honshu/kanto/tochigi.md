# Tochigi

Tochigi is home to Nikko's UNESCO World Heritage shrines and the Toshogu mausoleum, the powerful Kegon Falls, and Ashikaga Flower Park's famous wisteria tunnels. It is also a major strawberry-growing region.

## Cities

- **Ashikaga** — Known for the wisteria tunnels of Ashikaga Flower Park.
- **Nasushiobara** — A highland resort area near Nasu popular for hot springs and cool summers.
- **Nikko** — Home to the UNESCO-listed Toshogu shrine complex and the Kegon Falls.
- **Utsunomiya** — The prefectural capital, famous nationwide as Japan's gyoza dumpling city.
