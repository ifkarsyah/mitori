# Hiroshima

Hiroshima is defined by the Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome, alongside the floating torii gate of Miyajima and its own style of layered okonomiyaki.

## Cities

- **Fukuyama** — Known for its castle beside the shinkansen station and rose gardens.
- **Hatsukaichi** — Home to Miyajima island and its iconic floating torii gate.
- **Hiroshima** — The prefectural capital, defined by the Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome.
- **Kure** — A historic naval port city with a large maritime and battleship museum.
- **Onomichi** — A hillside port town famous for temple-hopping trails and as a cycling gateway to Shikoku.
