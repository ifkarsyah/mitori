# Shimane

Shimane is home to Izumo Taisha, one of Japan's oldest and most important Shinto shrines, the acclaimed gardens of the Adachi Museum of Art, and the well-preserved Matsue Castle.

## Cities

- **Hamada** — A Sea of Japan fishing port known for its aquarium and coastal scenery.
- **Izumo** — Home to Izumo Taisha, one of Japan's oldest and most important Shinto shrines.
- **Masuda** — Known for its Sesshu-designed Zen gardens.
- **Matsue** — The prefectural capital, known as the 'City of Water' for its castle moat and lakes.
