# Fukushima

Fukushima's highlights include Aizu-Wakamatsu's samurai history and Tsuruga Castle, the scenic Lake Inawashiro, sweet peaches, and numerous hot spring towns tucked into its mountains.

## Cities

- **Aizuwakamatsu** — A samurai stronghold city centered on the rebuilt Tsuruga Castle.
- **Fukushima** — The prefectural capital, known for peaches and the nearby Iizaka hot springs.
- **Iwaki** — A coastal city known for the Spa Resort Hawaiians hot spring theme park.
- **Koriyama** — A major transport and commercial hub in central Fukushima.
