# Iwate

Iwate is known for the UNESCO-listed temples of Hiraizumi, the dramatic Sanriku coastline, and Morioka's reimen cold noodles and wanko soba all-you-can-eat tradition. The Geto ski resort draws winter visitors to its deep powder snow.

## Cities

- **Ichinoseki** — Gateway city to the UNESCO-listed temples and gardens of nearby Hiraizumi.
- **Kitakami** — Known for the Tenshochi cherry blossom park along the Kitakami River.
- **Miyako** — A Sanriku coast fishing city known for dramatic cliffs and Jodogahama beach.
- **Morioka** — Iwate's capital, known for wanko soba, reimen cold noodles, and its castle ruins park.
