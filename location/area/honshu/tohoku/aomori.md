# Aomori

Known for the raucous Nebuta Festival, Aomori is Japan's top apple producer and home to Hirosaki Castle's cherry blossoms. Nature lovers visit the Oirase Gorge and Lake Towada for autumn colors and easy hiking trails.

## Cities

- **Aomori** — The prefectural capital, home of the Nebuta Festival and a major apple-shipping port.
- **Hachinohe** — A fishing port city famous for its morning market and squid catch.
- **Hirosaki** — Known for Hirosaki Castle's cherry blossoms and its role as a historic center of apple cultivation.
- **Towada** — Gateway to Lake Towada and the Oirase Gorge, popular for autumn foliage.
