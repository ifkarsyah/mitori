# Akita

Akita is known for the lantern-balancing Kanto Festival, the hot-spring hideaway of Nyuto Onsen, and its namesake Akita dog breed. Local specialties include kiritanpo rice-stick hot pot and highly regarded sake.

## Cities

- **Akita** — The prefectural capital, home to the Kanto lantern-pole festival and Senshu Park.
- **Odate** — Birthplace of the Akita dog breed, with a dedicated Akita dog museum.
- **Oga** — Home of the Namahage folklore demons and the scenic Oga Peninsula coastline.
- **Yokote** — Known for its winter kamakura snow-hut festival and yokote yakisoba.
