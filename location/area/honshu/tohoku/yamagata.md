# Yamagata

Yamagata is a fruit-growing prefecture famous for cherries, along with the Zao Onsen ski area's snow monsters, the cliffside mountain temple Yamadera, and the picturesque hot spring town of Ginzan Onsen.

## Cities

- **Sakata** — A historic Sea of Japan port town with preserved Edo-era rice warehouses.
- **Tsuruoka** — Gateway to the sacred Dewa Sanzan mountains and Shinto pilgrimage trails.
- **Yamagata** — The prefectural capital, known for cherry orchards and the Hanagasa dance festival.
- **Yonezawa** — Famous for Yonezawa beef and the legacy of samurai lord Uesugi Kenshin.
