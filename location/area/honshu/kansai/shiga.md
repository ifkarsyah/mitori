# Shiga

Shiga surrounds Lake Biwa, Japan's largest lake, offering lakeside recreation and views, alongside the historic Hikone Castle and the prized Omi wagyu beef.

## Cities

- **Hikone** — Home to Hikone Castle, one of Japan's few original feudal-era castles.
- **Nagahama** — A lakeside town known for Kurokabe Square's glass and craft shops.
- **Omihachiman** — Known for its preserved merchant canal district and Himure Hachimangu shrine.
- **Otsu** — The prefectural capital, on the shore of Lake Biwa, Japan's largest lake.
