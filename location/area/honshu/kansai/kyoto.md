# Kyoto

Japan's former imperial capital, Kyoto is packed with thousands of temples and shrines including Kiyomizu-dera, Fushimi Inari's torii tunnels, and Kinkaku-ji's golden pavilion, plus the geisha district of Gion and refined kaiseki cuisine.

## Cities

- **Ayabe** — A quiet farming city in the mountains of northern Kyoto.
- **Fukuchiyama** — A castle town in northern Kyoto, historically a key transport crossroads.
- **Joyo** — A commuter city between Kyoto and Osaka, known for bamboo groves.
- **Kameoka** — Known as the gateway for the scenic Hozugawa river boat ride into Kyoto.
- **Kizugawa** — Home to the ancient Kizugawa river and Kasagi's cherry blossoms.
- **Kyotanabe** — A university town known for tea cultivation.
- **Kyotango** — A coastal area in northern Kyoto known for silk weaving (Tango chirimen) and beaches.
- **Kyoto** — Japan's former imperial capital, packed with thousands of temples and shrines including Kiyomizu-dera and Fushimi Inari.
- **Maizuru** — A Sea of Japan naval port with a scenic red brick warehouse district.
- **Miyazu** — Home to Amanohashidate, one of Japan's three most scenic views.
- **Muko** — One of Kyoto's smallest cities, known for its Nagaoka-kyo era ruins.
- **Nagaokakyo** — Site of a short-lived 8th-century imperial capital, now known for bamboo shoots.
- **Nantan** — A rural city known for its rice fields and mountain scenery.
- **Uji** — Famous for Byodo-in temple's Phoenix Hall and centuries of prized matcha green tea cultivation.
- **Yawata** — Home to Iwashimizu Hachimangu shrine on a forested hilltop.
