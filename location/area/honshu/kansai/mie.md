# Mie

Mie is home to Ise Grand Shrine, Japan's most sacred Shinto site, as well as Toba's pearl-farming heritage and the Suzuka Circuit, a major venue for Japanese motorsports.

## Cities

- **Ise** — Home to Ise Grand Shrine, the most sacred site in Shinto.
- **Kuwana** — Famous for clams and its historic role along the old Tokaido road.
- **Toba** — Known for pearl cultivation, pioneered here by Mikimoto.
- **Tsu** — The prefectural capital, a quieter administrative and port city.
