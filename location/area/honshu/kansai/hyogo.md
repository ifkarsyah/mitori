# Hyogo

Hyogo is known for Kobe beef and the port city's harbor views, Himeji Castle, Japan's most celebrated original castle, and the relaxing hot springs of Arima Onsen.

## Cities

- **Amagasaki** — An industrial city bordering Osaka, with a revitalized waterfront and canal area.
- **Ashiya** — An upscale residential city between Kobe and Osaka, known for its coastal setting.
- **Himeji** — Home to Himeji Castle, Japan's most celebrated and best-preserved original castle.
- **Kobe** — The prefectural capital, famous for Kobe beef and its scenic port and mountainside views.
- **Nishinomiya** — Known for sake brewing and Koshien Stadium, home of Japan's high school baseball tournament.
