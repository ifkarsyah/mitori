# Kagawa

Japan's smallest prefecture, Kagawa is nicknamed 'Udon Prefecture' for its noodle culture, and is also home to Ritsurin Garden and the contemporary art islands of Naoshima and the Setouchi Triennale.

## Cities

- **Kanonji** — Famous for the giant Zenigata sand coin carved into a hillside beach.
- **Marugame** — Known for its towering castle and udon noodle production.
- **Sakaide** — An industrial port city connected to Honshu by the Great Seto Bridge.
- **Takamatsu** — The prefectural capital, home to Ritsurin Garden and the udon noodle culture Kagawa is famed for.
