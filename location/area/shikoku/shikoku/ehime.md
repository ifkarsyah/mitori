# Ehime

Ehime is known for Dogo Onsen, one of Japan's oldest hot springs, sweet mikan citrus grown on its hillsides, and the historic Matsuyama Castle overlooking the city.

## Cities

- **Imabari** — Known for towel manufacturing and the cycling route across the Shimanami Kaido bridges.
- **Matsuyama** — The prefectural capital, home to Dogo Onsen and Matsuyama Castle.
- **Niihama** — A historic copper-mining city that grew into a chemical industry hub.
- **Uwajima** — Famous for bullfighting tradition and fresh seafood, including pearl and fish farming.
