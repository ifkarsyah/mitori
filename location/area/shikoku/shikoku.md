# Shikoku

Shikoku region overlaps the island itself, centered on the Ohenro pilgrimage circuit and quiet mountain and coastal scenery. Popular stops include the Iya Valley's vine bridges, Naruto's whirlpools, and Kagawa's udon shops.

## Prefectures

- **Ehime** — Ehime is known for Dogo Onsen, one of Japan's oldest hot springs, sweet mikan citrus grown on its hillsides, and the historic Matsuyama Castle overlooking the city.
- **Kagawa** — Japan's smallest prefecture, Kagawa is nicknamed 'Udon Prefecture' for its noodle culture, and is also home to Ritsurin Garden and the contemporary art islands of Naoshima and the Setouchi Triennale.
- **Kochi** — Kochi offers the scenic Katsurahama beach, seared bonito (katsuo) cuisine, sites tied to Ryoma Sakamoto's role in ending the shogunate, and the clear waters of the Shimanto River.
- **Tokushima** — Tokushima hosts the exuberant Awa Odori dance festival, the tidal whirlpools of Naruto, a centuries-old indigo dyeing tradition, and the remote vine bridges of the Iya Valley.
