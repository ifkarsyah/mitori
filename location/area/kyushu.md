# Kyushu

Japan's third-largest island, shaped by active volcanoes like Mount Aso and Sakurajima and dotted with hot spring towns such as Beppu and Yufuin. It has a strong food culture built around tonkotsu ramen and shochu, plus a history as one of Japan's earliest gateways for foreign trade.

## Regions

- **Kyushu** — Kyushu region spans the same territory as the island, anchored by Fukuoka's food scene and a chain of volcanic hot spring towns.
