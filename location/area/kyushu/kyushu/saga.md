# Saga

Saga is known for the fine porcelain of Arita and Imari, the ancient Yoshinogari archaeological site, and its annual international hot air balloon festival.

## Cities

- **Imari** — Renowned for centuries of fine porcelain production.
- **Karatsu** — Famous for Karatsu ware pottery and a dramatic autumn kunchi festival.
- **Saga** — The prefectural capital, known for its annual international hot air balloon festival.
- **Takeo** — Known for the historic Takeo Onsen hot spring and a striking library.
