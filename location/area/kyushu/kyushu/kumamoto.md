# Kumamoto

Kumamoto features one of Japan's most impressive castles, the volcanic caldera of Mount Aso, the beloved black bear mascot Kumamon, and the mountain hot spring village of Kurokawa Onsen.

## Cities

- **Amakusa** — An island city known for dolphin watching and Christian martyr history.
- **Aso** — Centered on the vast volcanic caldera of Mount Aso, one of the world's largest.
- **Kumamoto** — The prefectural capital, home to one of Japan's most impressive castles and mascot Kumamon.
- **Yatsushiro** — Known for igusa rush-mat weaving and its autumn lantern festival.
