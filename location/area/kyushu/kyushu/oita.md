# Oita

Oita is Japan's onsen capital, led by Beppu's steaming 'hells' and the elegant ryokan town of Yufuin, with a local cuisine built around hot-spring steamed dishes.

## Cities

- **Beppu** — Japan's onsen capital, famous for its steaming 'hells' hot spring displays.
- **Nakatsu** — Known for its castle and deep-fried karaage chicken.
- **Oita** — The prefectural capital, an industrial port city on Kyushu's east coast.
- **Yufu** — Home to the elegant hot spring town of Yufuin, set beneath Mount Yufu.
