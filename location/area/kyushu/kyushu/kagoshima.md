# Kagoshima

Kagoshima sits in the shadow of the ever-active Sakurajima volcano and is known for kurobuta black pork, sweet potato shochu, and the sand bath hot springs of Ibusuki.

## Cities

- **Amami** — A subtropical island city known for coral reefs and Amami black rabbits.
- **Ibusuki** — Famous for naturally heated sand bath hot springs on the beach.
- **Kagoshima** — The prefectural capital, set in the shadow of the ever-active Sakurajima volcano.
- **Kirishima** — Known for its shrine, hot springs, and mythological ties to Japan's creation legends.
