# Honshu

Japan's largest and most populous island, home to Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, and Mount Fuji. It is the country's cultural and economic core, blending ultra-modern cities with ancient temples, shrines, and historic castles, and is famous for cherry blossoms and the shinkansen bullet train network.

## Regions

- **Chubu** — Central Honshu, dominated by the Japanese Alps and Mount Fuji.
- **Chugoku** — The westernmost region of Honshu, home to Hiroshima's Peace Memorial Park and Miyajima's floating torii gate.
- **Kansai** — Historic western Honshu centered on Kyoto and Osaka, Japan's former imperial capital and current food capital.
- **Kanto** — The most populous region of Japan, centered on Tokyo and its surrounding prefectures.
- **Tohoku** — The northeastern region of Honshu, known for rugged coastlines, rice paddies, and apple orchards.
