# Nature

Attractions centered on Japan's natural landscapes and outdoor scenery, from alpine peaks to volcanic hot springs. This covers mountains, coastlines, lakes, parks, and other spots where the appeal is scenery and the outdoors rather than history or shopping.

## Subtypes

- **Mountain** — Peaks, alpine trails, and volcanic summits popular for hiking, climbing, ropeways, and scenic viewpoints, from Mount Fuji to Kyushu's active volcanoes.
- **Beach** — Coastal spots for swimming, surfing, and sunbathing, ranging from Okinawa's coral-reef beaches to Shonan's surf breaks near Tokyo.
- **Lake** — Scenic lakes popular for boating, hiking loops, and seasonal views, including volcanic caldera lakes and the Fuji Five Lakes.
- **Onsen** — Natural hot spring baths and the resort towns built around them, from Beppu's steaming 'hells' to remote mountain ryokan.
- **Park** — Public gardens and nature parks known for seasonal scenery like cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, or flower fields.
- **Waterfall** — Notable waterfalls valued for scenic hiking and photography, such as Nikko's Kegon Falls.
- **Island** — Smaller islands and island chains visited for beaches, diving, or a slower pace of travel, from the Seto Inland Sea to the Okinawan archipelago.
