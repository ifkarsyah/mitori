# Food

Attractions centered on eating and drinking, from bustling food markets to entire districts built around a regional specialty. This covers markets, street food, restaurant districts, and breweries.

## Subtypes

- **Market** — Fresh food and seafood markets popular for browsing, tasting, and buying local specialties, like Kyoto's Nishiki Market.
- **Street Food** — Casual food-stall culture such as yatai stalls and festival food streets, centered on quick, affordable local dishes.
- **Restaurant District** — Dense clusters of restaurants and izakaya known for a city's signature food culture, like Osaka's Dotonbori.
- **Brewery** — Sake, beer, and shochu breweries and distilleries, many offering tastings and tours.
