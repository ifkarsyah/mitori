# Entertainment

Attractions built for leisure, nightlife, and events, including theme parks, shopping streets, and festivals. This covers theme parks, nightlife districts, shopping districts, and seasonal festivals.

## Subtypes

- **Theme Park** — Amusement parks and large entertainment complexes, from Tokyo Disney Resort to regional theme parks.
- **Nightlife** — Bar districts, izakaya alleys, and late-night entertainment areas such as Shinjuku's Kabukicho.
- **Shopping District** — Major shopping streets and districts, from luxury flagship stores to arcades and electronics towns like Akihabara.
- **Festival** — Seasonal matsuri and citywide celebrations, from summer fireworks to winter illuminations.
