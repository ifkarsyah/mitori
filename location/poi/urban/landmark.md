# Landmark

Recognizable structures and monuments that define a city's identity, from bridges to iconic buildings.

## Popular Spots

- **Tokyo Tower** — An instantly recognizable red-and-white lattice tower and symbol of postwar Tokyo.
- **Kaminarimon** — The giant red lantern gate marking the entrance to Asakusa's Senso-ji temple.
- **Shibuya Crossing** — The world's busiest pedestrian scramble crossing, a symbol of Tokyo's energy.
- **Osaka Castle** — A rebuilt feudal-era castle at the center of a large downtown park.
- **Kintai Bridge** — Yamaguchi's distinctive five-arched wooden bridge over the Nishiki River.
