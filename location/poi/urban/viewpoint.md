# Viewpoint

Towers, observation decks, and elevated spots for skyline and scenery views, like Tokyo Skytree.

## Popular Spots

- **Tokyo Skytree** — The world's tallest freestanding broadcast tower, with two observation decks over Tokyo.
- **Tokyo Tower** — Tokyo's red-and-white Eiffel Tower-inspired landmark and observation tower.
- **Umeda Sky Building** — Osaka's twin-tower skyscraper connected by a rooftop 'Floating Garden' observatory.
- **Mount Hakodate Ropeway** — A cable car up to Hakodate's famous night skyline view, rated among Japan's best.
