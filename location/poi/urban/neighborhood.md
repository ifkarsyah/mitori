# Neighborhood

Distinctive city neighborhoods known for atmosphere or subculture rather than a single sight, like Shimokitazawa or Yanaka.

## Popular Spots

- **Shimokitazawa** — A Tokyo neighborhood known for vintage shops, indie theaters, and a laid-back bohemian vibe.
- **Yanaka** — One of Tokyo's few areas to survive WWII bombing largely intact, known for its old-town atmosphere.
- **Harajuku** — Tokyo's youth fashion hub, centered on Takeshita Street's quirky boutiques.
- **Nakameguro** — A trendy Tokyo neighborhood known for canal-side cafes and cherry blossoms.
