# Urban

Attractions defined by cityscape and skyline rather than nature or history, including viewpoints, landmarks, and distinctive neighborhoods.

## Subtypes

- **Viewpoint** — Towers, observation decks, and elevated spots for skyline and scenery views, like Tokyo Skytree.
- **Landmark** — Recognizable structures and monuments that define a city's identity, from bridges to iconic buildings.
- **Neighborhood** — Distinctive city neighborhoods known for atmosphere or subculture rather than a single sight, like Shimokitazawa or Yanaka.
