# Temple

Buddhist temples ranging from major pilgrimage sites to quiet mountain retreats, often paired with gardens or historic townscapes.

## Popular Spots

- **Kiyomizu-dera** — A Kyoto temple famous for its wooden stage jutting out over the hillside.
- **Todai-ji** — A Nara temple housing Japan's largest bronze Buddha statue.
- **Senso-ji** — Tokyo's oldest temple, in Asakusa, fronted by the iconic Kaminarimon thunder gate.
- **Kinkaku-ji** — Kyoto's Golden Pavilion, a gold leaf-covered temple reflected in its garden pond.
- **Horyu-ji** — One of the world's oldest surviving wooden structures, in Nara.
