# Castle

Feudal-era castles and their surrounding parks, including a handful of rare original structures like Himeji and Matsumoto.

## Popular Spots

- **Himeji Castle** — Japan's most celebrated original castle, nicknamed the White Heron Castle.
- **Matsumoto Castle** — Known as the Crow Castle for its black exterior, one of Japan's oldest original castle keeps.
- **Osaka Castle** — A rebuilt landmark castle at the center of a large park, tied to warlord Toyotomi Hideyoshi.
- **Kumamoto Castle** — One of Japan's most impressive castles, restored after 2016 earthquake damage.
- **Hikone Castle** — A rare original feudal-era castle overlooking Lake Biwa.
