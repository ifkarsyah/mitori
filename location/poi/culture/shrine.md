# Shrine

Shinto shrines, from grand sites like Ise and Izumo to small neighborhood shrines marked by torii gates.

## Popular Spots

- **Fushimi Inari Taisha** — A Kyoto shrine famous for its thousands of vermillion torii gates climbing the mountainside.
- **Ise Grand Shrine** — Shinto's most sacred shrine complex, in Mie, rebuilt every 20 years in an ancient tradition.
- **Meiji Jingu** — A forested Tokyo shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji, next to Harajuku.
- **Izumo Taisha** — One of Japan's oldest and most important shrines, in Shimane, associated with matchmaking.
- **Itsukushima Shrine** — The shrine behind Miyajima's iconic floating torii gate.
