# Historic District

Preserved streetscapes of merchant houses, samurai residences, or Edo-era townscapes, popular for walking tours.

## Popular Spots

- **Gion** — Kyoto's famous geisha district, known for traditional wooden machiya townhouses.
- **Higashi Chaya District** — Kanazawa's preserved geisha quarter of gold-leaf-accented teahouses.
- **Shirakawa-go** — A UNESCO-listed village in Gifu known for its steep thatched-roof gassho-zukuri farmhouses.
- **Kurashiki Bikan** — A canal-lined merchant quarter in Okayama with preserved white-walled warehouses.
- **Kawagoe** — Known as 'Little Edo' near Tokyo for its well-preserved Edo-era merchant street.
