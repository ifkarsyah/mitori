# Museum

Art, history, and specialty museums, from major national collections to small local heritage exhibits.

## Popular Spots

- **Tokyo National Museum** — Japan's oldest and largest museum, holding the country's biggest collection of art and antiquities.
- **Ghibli Museum** — A whimsical Mitaka museum celebrating Studio Ghibli's animated films.
- **Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum** — A museum documenting the 1945 atomic bombing, next to the Atomic Bomb Dome.
- **teamLab Borderless** — An immersive digital art museum known for its interactive, ever-changing light installations.
- **Adachi Museum of Art** — A Shimane museum famous for its meticulously landscaped garden viewed like a living painting.
