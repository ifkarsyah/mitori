# Garden

Traditional landscape gardens designed for seasonal viewing, including Japan's three great gardens.

## Popular Spots

- **Kenrokuen** — One of Japan's three great gardens, in Kanazawa, celebrated for all-season beauty.
- **Korakuen** — One of Japan's three great gardens, in Okayama, known for its open lawns and borrowed scenery.
- **Ritsurin Garden** — A sprawling Edo-era garden in Takamatsu with ponds, pines, and hills.
- **Kairakuen** — One of Japan's three great gardens, in Mito, famous for its plum blossoms.
- **Shukkeien** — A compact Edo-era garden in Hiroshima built around a landscaped valley theme.
