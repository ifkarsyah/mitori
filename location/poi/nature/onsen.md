# Onsen

Natural hot spring baths and the resort towns built around them, from Beppu's steaming 'hells' to remote mountain ryokan.

## Popular Spots

- **Kusatsu Onsen** — One of Japan's most famous hot spring towns, in Gunma, known for its steaming central yubatake field.
- **Beppu** — Japan's onsen capital, in Oita, famous for its steaming 'hells' hot spring displays and sand baths.
- **Hakone** — A hot spring resort town near Tokyo with Mount Fuji views and traditional ryokan.
- **Kurokawa Onsen** — A rustic mountain hot spring village in Kumamoto known for its outdoor baths and wooden townscape.
- **Nyuto Onsen** — A remote cluster of rustic hot spring inns deep in the Akita mountains.
