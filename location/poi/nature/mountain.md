# Mountain

Peaks, alpine trails, and volcanic summits popular for hiking, climbing, ropeways, and scenic viewpoints, from Mount Fuji to Kyushu's active volcanoes.

## Popular Spots

- **Mount Fuji** — Japan's tallest and most iconic peak, a UNESCO World Heritage site straddling Shizuoka and Yamanashi, popular for summer climbing and views from the surrounding Fuji Five Lakes.
- **Mount Takao** — A forested peak on Tokyo's western edge, popular for an easy cable-car-assisted hike, temple visits, and autumn foliage.
- **Mount Aso** — One of the world's largest volcanic calderas, in Kumamoto, with an active crater visitors can approach and vast grasslands for hiking.
- **Yarigatake** — A dramatic spear-shaped peak in the Northern Japan Alps, a challenging multi-day climb popular with experienced hikers.
- **Mount Daisen** — The tallest peak in the Chugoku region, in Tottori, known for its steep ridgeline hike and shrine at its base.
