# Island

Smaller islands and island chains visited for beaches, diving, or a slower pace of travel, from the Seto Inland Sea to the Okinawan archipelago.

## Popular Spots

- **Miyajima** — Home to Itsukushima Shrine's iconic floating torii gate in Hiroshima Bay.
- **Naoshima** — A small Seto Inland Sea island transformed into a contemporary art destination with the Benesse art museums.
- **Yakushima** — A subtropical, UNESCO-listed island in Kagoshima known for ancient cedar forests and lush rainforest hikes.
- **Ishigaki** — A Yaeyama island in Okinawa known for coral reefs, diving, and manta ray spotting.
- **Sado Island** — Niigata's large island known for a former gold mine and traditional taiko drumming.
