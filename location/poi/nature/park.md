# Park

Public gardens and nature parks known for seasonal scenery like cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, or flower fields.

## Popular Spots

- **Ueno Park** — A large Tokyo park known for cherry blossoms, museums, and Ueno Zoo.
- **Nara Park** — Home to hundreds of free-roaming, bowing sacred deer alongside Nara's major temples.
- **Showa Kinen Park** — A vast park in western Tokyo known for its flower fields, especially poppies and cosmos.
- **Hitachi Seaside Park** — Famous in Ibaraki for its spring nemophila (baby blue eyes) flower fields.
- **Rikugien** — A refined Edo-era landscape garden in Tokyo, especially known for autumn foliage.
