# Lake

Scenic lakes popular for boating, hiking loops, and seasonal views, including volcanic caldera lakes and the Fuji Five Lakes.

## Popular Spots

- **Lake Kawaguchi** — The most accessible of the Fuji Five Lakes, known for reflection views of Mount Fuji.
- **Lake Ashi** — A caldera lake in Hakone offering pirate-ship cruises with views of Mount Fuji and the Hakone Shrine torii gate.
- **Lake Biwa** — Japan's largest lake, in Shiga, ringed by cycling paths and lakeside towns.
- **Lake Toya** — A volcanic caldera lake in Hokkaido known for summer fireworks and nearby hot springs.
- **Lake Shikotsu** — One of Japan's clearest lakes, in Hokkaido, framed by volcanic peaks.
