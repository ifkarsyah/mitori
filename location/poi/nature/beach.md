# Beach

Coastal spots for swimming, surfing, and sunbathing, ranging from Okinawa's coral-reef beaches to Shonan's surf breaks near Tokyo.

## Popular Spots

- **Shirahama Beach** — A white-sand beach in Wakayama backed by a classic hot spring resort town.
- **Kondoi Beach** — A shallow, turquoise-water beach on Taketomi Island in Okinawa's Yaeyama chain, popular for swimming and snorkeling.
- **Zushi Beach** — A convenient beach near Kamakura, popular with Tokyo day-trippers in summer.
- **Katsurahama** — A scenic pebble beach in Kochi known for its pine-lined shore and statue of Ryoma Sakamoto.
- **Sunayama Beach** — A dramatic Miyako Island beach with a natural rock arch and turquoise water.
