# Waterfall

Notable waterfalls valued for scenic hiking and photography, such as Nikko's Kegon Falls.

## Popular Spots

- **Kegon Falls** — A powerful 97-meter waterfall in Nikko, viewable from an elevator-accessed platform.
- **Nachi Falls** — Japan's tallest single-drop waterfall, in Wakayama, sacred to the Kumano pilgrimage.
- **Shiraito Falls** — A wide curtain of countless thin waterfalls near Mount Fuji in Shizuoka.
- **Fukiware Falls** — Known as Gunma's 'Niagara,' a canyon-carved waterfall along the Katashina River.
