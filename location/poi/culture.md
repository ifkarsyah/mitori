# Culture

Attractions rooted in Japan's history, religion, and traditional arts, including sacred sites, feudal architecture, and museums. This covers temples, shrines, castles, gardens, and preserved historic districts.

## Subtypes

- **Temple** — Buddhist temples ranging from major pilgrimage sites to quiet mountain retreats, often paired with gardens or historic townscapes.
- **Shrine** — Shinto shrines, from grand sites like Ise and Izumo to small neighborhood shrines marked by torii gates.
- **Castle** — Feudal-era castles and their surrounding parks, including a handful of rare original structures like Himeji and Matsumoto.
- **Museum** — Art, history, and specialty museums, from major national collections to small local heritage exhibits.
- **Garden** — Traditional landscape gardens designed for seasonal viewing, including Japan's three great gardens.
- **Historic District** — Preserved streetscapes of merchant houses, samurai residences, or Edo-era townscapes, popular for walking tours.
