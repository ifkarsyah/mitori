# Brewery

Sake, beer, and shochu breweries and distilleries, many offering tastings and tours.

## Popular Spots

- **Sapporo Beer Museum** — Japan's only beer museum, in Sapporo, tracing the history of Sapporo Beer.
- **Hakushu Distillery** — Suntory's forested whisky distillery in the Southern Alps foothills of Yamanashi.
- **Nada** — Kobe's historic sake-brewing district, one of Japan's largest sake-producing areas.
- **Saijo** — A Hiroshima town known as a sake-brewing hub with a walkable cluster of historic breweries.
