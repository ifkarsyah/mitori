# Street Food

Casual food-stall culture such as yatai stalls and festival food streets, centered on quick, affordable local dishes.

## Popular Spots

- **Dotonbori** — Osaka's canal-side food street packed with takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and glowing signage.
- **Hakata Yatai** — Fukuoka's open-air food stalls, especially known for late-night tonkotsu ramen.
- **Nakamise-dori** — A traditional shopping street leading to Senso-ji in Asakusa, lined with snack and souvenir stalls.
- **Osu Shopping Street** — A lively Nagoya shopping arcade known for street food and retro shops.
