# Market

Fresh food and seafood markets popular for browsing, tasting, and buying local specialties, like Kyoto's Nishiki Market.

## Popular Spots

- **Nishiki Market** — Kyoto's 'Kitchen,' a narrow covered arcade of food stalls and traditional ingredients.
- **Tsukiji Outer Market** — Tokyo's famous seafood and food market street, still active after the inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu.
- **Omicho Market** — Kanazawa's 'Kitchen,' known for fresh seafood and local produce.
- **Kuromon Ichiba** — Osaka's lively food market known for street-food-style tastings of seafood and wagyu.
