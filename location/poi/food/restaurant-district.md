# Restaurant District

Dense clusters of restaurants and izakaya known for a city's signature food culture, like Osaka's Dotonbori.

## Popular Spots

- **Dotonbori** — Osaka's iconic dining and entertainment strip along the canal, famous for its glowing Glico sign.
- **Yurakucho** — A Tokyo district known for its atmospheric yakitori alleys under the train tracks.
- **Susukino** — Sapporo's largest entertainment and dining district, known for ramen and nightlife.
- **Nakasu** — Fukuoka's riverside entertainment district known for yatai stalls and nightlife.
