# Theme Park

Amusement parks and large entertainment complexes, from Tokyo Disney Resort to regional theme parks.

## Popular Spots

- **Tokyo Disney Resort** — Japan's original Disney park, actually located in Urayasu, Chiba.
- **Universal Studios Japan** — Osaka's major movie-themed park, known for its Harry Potter and Super Nintendo World areas.
- **Fuji-Q Highland** — A Yamanashi amusement park known for record-setting roller coasters and Mount Fuji views.
- **Huis Ten Bosch** — A Dutch-themed park in Nagasaki with canals, windmills, and seasonal illuminations.
