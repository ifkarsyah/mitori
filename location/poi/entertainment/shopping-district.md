# Shopping District

Major shopping streets and districts, from luxury flagship stores to arcades and electronics towns like Akihabara.

## Popular Spots

- **Akihabara** — Tokyo's electronics and anime/manga shopping district.
- **Ginza** — Tokyo's upscale flagship shopping street, home to luxury department stores and boutiques.
- **Shinsaibashi** — Osaka's main covered shopping arcade, connected to Dotonbori.
- **Omotesando** — A tree-lined Tokyo avenue known for high-fashion flagship architecture.
