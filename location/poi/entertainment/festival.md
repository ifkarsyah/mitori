# Festival

Seasonal matsuri and citywide celebrations, from summer fireworks to winter illuminations.

## Popular Spots

- **Gion Matsuri** — Kyoto's month-long July festival, climaxing in a parade of towering wooden floats.
- **Nebuta Matsuri** — Aomori's August festival featuring illuminated warrior-shaped floats paraded at night.
- **Awa Odori** — Tokushima's exuberant August dance festival, one of Japan's largest Bon dance events.
- **Sapporo Snow Festival** — A February festival famous for massive, elaborately carved snow and ice sculptures.
