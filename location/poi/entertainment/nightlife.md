# Nightlife

Bar districts, izakaya alleys, and late-night entertainment areas such as Shinjuku's Kabukicho.

## Popular Spots

- **Kabukicho** — Tokyo's largest entertainment district, in Shinjuku, packed with bars, clubs, and neon signage.
- **Susukino** — Sapporo's main nightlife district, especially lively in winter around the Snow Festival.
- **Roppongi** — A Tokyo district known for international nightlife, clubs, and nearby art museums.
- **Nakasu** — Fukuoka's riverside nightlife hub known for yatai food stalls after dark.
