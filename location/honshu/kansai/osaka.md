# Osaka

Osaka is Japan's food capital, famous for takoyaki and okonomiyaki, the neon nightlife of Dotonbori, historic Osaka Castle, and Universal Studios Japan, all wrapped in a famously outgoing local culture.

## Cities

- **Daito** — An industrial commuter city bordering Nara prefecture.
- **Fujiidera** — Home to ancient kofun burial mounds and Fujii-dera temple.
- **Habikino** — Home to significant ancient kofun burial mounds shared with Fujiidera.
- **Hannan** — Known for its beaches and proximity to Kansai International Airport.
- **Higashiosaka** — Known for small-factory manufacturing and Japan's national rugby stadium, Hanazono.
- **Hirakata** — Home to Hirakata Park, one of Japan's oldest amusement parks.
- **Ibaraki** — A commuter city known for Ibaraki Kasuga shrine and its university campuses.
- **Ikeda** — Birthplace of instant ramen, with a museum dedicated to its inventor.
- **Izumi** — Known for its textile industry and Makimuku-era historical sites.
- **Izumiotsu** — A textile-manufacturing port city south of Osaka.
- **Izumisano** — Gateway city to Kansai International Airport.
- **Kadoma** — Historically the birthplace of Matsushita (Panasonic) Electric.
- **Kaizuka** — Known for its beaches and the historic Ganjoji temple.
- **Kashiwara** — Known for grape and wine cultivation in the Osaka hills.
- **Katano** — Known for the Kisaichi Fudoson temple and its mountain hiking trails.
- **Kawachinagano** — Known for Mount Kongo hiking and Kanshin-ji temple.
- **Kishiwada** — Famous for the Danjiri Matsuri, a festival of wooden floats raced through the streets.
- **Matsubara** — A commuter city known for furniture manufacturing.
- **Minoh** — Famous for the Minoh Falls hiking trail and maple leaf tempura.
- **Moriguchi** — A compact, densely populated commuter city just northeast of Osaka.
- **Neyagawa** — A residential city along the Neya River, known for its rose garden.
- **Osaka** — Japan's food capital, famous for takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and the neon nightlife of Dotonbori.
- **Osakasayama** — A small residential city built around the ancient Sayama-ike reservoir.
- **Sakai** — Historically a major merchant port, known for centuries-old cutlery craftsmanship and ancient kofun burial mounds.
- **Sennan** — A coastal city known for onion farming and beaches.
- **Settsu** — A small industrial city between Osaka and Ibaraki.
- **Shijonawate** — Site of a famous 14th-century battle, now a quiet residential city.
- **Suita** — Home to Expo '70 Commemorative Park and the Panasonic Stadium.
- **Takaishi** — Known for its ancient shrine and proximity to Kansai Airport.
- **Takatsuki** — A commuter city between Osaka and Kyoto known for its castle ruins park.
- **Tondabayashi** — Home to the preserved Edo-era merchant quarter of Jinaimachi.
- **Toyonaka** — A residential city bordering Osaka, home to the former Osaka International Airport terminal area.
- **Yao** — Known for its aviation history and traditional futon craftsmanship.
