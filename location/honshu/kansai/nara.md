# Nara

Japan's first permanent capital, Nara is home to Todai-ji's giant bronze Buddha, Nara Park's famous bowing sacred deer, and the ancient Horyu-ji temple, one of the world's oldest wooden structures.

## Cities

- **Ikoma** — Known for Mount Ikoma's cable car and historic temple trails.
- **Kashihara** — Site of Japan's legendary first imperial capital, Fujiwara-kyo.
- **Nara** — Japan's first permanent capital, home to Todai-ji's giant Buddha and bowing sacred deer.
- **Yamatokoriyama** — Famous for goldfish farming, a local tradition for centuries.
