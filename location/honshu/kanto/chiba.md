# Chiba

Chiba is home to Narita International Airport and the historic Narita-san temple, Tokyo Disney Resort, and the surfing beaches of the Boso Peninsula. It is also a major peanut-growing region.

## Cities

- **Chiba** — The prefectural capital, a port city and business hub on Tokyo Bay.
- **Funabashi** — A commuter city known for its fishing port and Tokyo Bay reclaimed land.
- **Kamogawa** — A coastal city known for the Kamogawa Sea World marine park and surfing beaches.
- **Narita** — Home to Narita International Airport and the historic Narita-san Shinsho-ji temple.
- **Urayasu** — Home to Tokyo Disney Resort, despite the 'Tokyo' name being in Chiba.
