# Chugoku

The westernmost region of Honshu, home to Hiroshima's Peace Memorial Park and Miyajima's floating torii gate. Known for the scenic Seto Inland Sea, Okayama's Korakuen garden, Tottori's sand dunes, and Yamaguchi's fugu cuisine.

## Prefectures

- **Hiroshima** — Hiroshima is defined by the Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome, alongside the floating torii gate of Miyajima and its own style of layered okonomiyaki.
- **Okayama** — Okayama features Korakuen, one of Japan's three great gardens, the canal-lined merchant district of Kurashiki, the folklore of Momotaro, and a growing reputation for denim production.
- **Shimane** — Shimane is home to Izumo Taisha, one of Japan's oldest and most important Shinto shrines, the acclaimed gardens of the Adachi Museum of Art, and the well-preserved Matsue Castle.
- **Tottori** — Tottori is known for Japan's largest sand dunes, sweet pears, views of Mount Daisen, and its ties to manga creators, celebrated with Conan and Kitaro-themed attractions.
- **Yamaguchi** — Yamaguchi is famous for fugu pufferfish cuisine, the arched Kintai Bridge, the vast Akiyoshido limestone cave, and the striking red torii gates of Motonosumi Inari Shrine along the coast.
