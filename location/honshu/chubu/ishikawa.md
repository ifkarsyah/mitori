# Ishikawa

Ishikawa's capital Kanazawa is famed for the Kenrokuen garden, gold leaf crafts, the geisha district of Higashi Chaya, and fresh seafood at Omicho Market, having largely escaped WWII bombing to preserve its Edo-era charm.

## Cities

- **Kanazawa** — The prefectural capital, famous for the Kenrokuen garden and gold leaf craft.
- **Komatsu** — Home to Komatsu Airport and a heritage of Kutani porcelain.
- **Nanao** — A Noto Peninsula port city known for seafood and the Wakura Onsen hot spring.
- **Wajima** — Famous for Wajima-nuri lacquerware and its lively morning market.
