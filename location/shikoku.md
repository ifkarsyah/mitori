# Shikoku

The smallest of Japan's four main islands, best known for the 88-temple Shikoku Pilgrimage linked to the monk Kukai. Visitors come for citrus fruits, the vine bridges of the Iya Valley, the whirlpools off Naruto, and Kagawa's beloved udon noodles.

## Regions

- **Shikoku** — Shikoku region overlaps the island itself, centered on the Ohenro pilgrimage circuit and quiet mountain and coastal scenery.
