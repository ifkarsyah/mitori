# Hokkaido

As both an island and its own administrative region, Hokkaido is popular for Sapporo's ramen and craft beer, the Otaru canal district, and ski towns such as Niseko and Furano. Its cooler climate supports dairy farming, lavender fields, and some of Japan's best seafood markets.

## Prefectures

- **Hokkaido** — Hokkaido Prefecture covers the entire island, centered on the capital Sapporo with its ramen alleys, beer museums, and winter Snow Festival.
