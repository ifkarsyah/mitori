# Hokkaido

Japan's northernmost and second-largest island, famous for vast snowy landscapes, powder skiing resorts like Niseko, and the Sapporo Snow Festival. It offers fresh seafood, dairy products, lavender fields in Furano, and wide-open national parks that draw visitors chasing cooler summers and dramatic winters.

## Regions

- **Hokkaido** — As both an island and its own administrative region, Hokkaido is popular for Sapporo's ramen and craft beer, the Otaru canal district, and ski towns such as Niseko and Furano.
