# JR Osaka Loop Line

A 19-station loop line encircling central Osaka, playing a similar role to Tokyo's Yamanote Line. It connects Osaka's two biggest hubs — Osaka/Umeda in the north and Tennoji in the south — along with Kyobashi and Shin-Imamiya, and ties together nearly every private railway terminal ringing the city center. A full loop covers 21.7 km.
