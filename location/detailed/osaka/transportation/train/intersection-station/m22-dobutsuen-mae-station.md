# Dobutsuen-mae

Connects to the Osaka Metro Sakaisuji Line; named for the adjacent Tennoji Zoo.

## Literal Meaning

動物園前: "in front of the zoo"
- 動物園 (どうぶつえん): zoo
- 前 (まえ): in front of
