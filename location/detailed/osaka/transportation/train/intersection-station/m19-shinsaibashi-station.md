# Shinsaibashi

Connects to the Osaka Metro Nagahori Tsurumi-ryokuchi Line; a major shopping district.

## Literal Meaning

心斎橋: "Shinsai's bridge"
- 心斎 (しんさい): Okawa Shinsaiemon, the merchant who funded the original bridge
- 橋 (ばし): bridge
