# Tamatsukuri

Connects to the Osaka Metro Nagahori Tsurumi-ryokuchi Line.

## Literal Meaning

玉造: "jewel-making"
- 玉 (たま): jewel/bead
- 造 (つくり): making, from ancient magatama bead workshops here
