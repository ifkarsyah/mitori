# Nakamozu

Terminus; connects to the Nankai Koya Line. Written in hiragana on Midosuji Line signage; the formal kanji name is 中百舌鳥.

## Literal Meaning

なかもず: "written in hiragana; kanji form means 'middle shrike (bird)'"
- 中百舌鳥 (なかもず): the underlying kanji form: 中=middle, 百舌鳥=shrike, a type of bird
