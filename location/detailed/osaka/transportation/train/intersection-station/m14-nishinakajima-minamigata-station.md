# Nishinakajima-Minamigata

Connects to the Hankyu Kyoto Line (as Minami-Kata).

## Literal Meaning

西中島南方: "west-middle-island south-direction"
- 西中島 (にしなかじま): west middle island
- 南方 (みなみがた): southern direction — two merged district names
