# Shin-Imamiya

Connects to the Nankai Main Line and the Osaka Metro Midosuji Line — a key transfer point for Kansai Airport access.

## Literal Meaning

新今宮: "new Imamiya"
- 新 (しん): new
- 今宮 (いまみや): Imamiya, 'present-day shrine'
