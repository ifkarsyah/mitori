# Midosuji Line

Osaka's busiest and most important subway line, a 30-station north-south spine running Esaka to Nakamozu. It directly links Osaka's major downtown hubs — Shin-Osaka, Umeda, Namba, and Tennoji — and its Umeda-Yodoyabashi stretch is the single busiest subway segment in Japan. Named for Midosuji Avenue, the wide boulevard it runs beneath through the city center.
