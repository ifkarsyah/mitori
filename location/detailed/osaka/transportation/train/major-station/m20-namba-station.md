# Namba

A major hub: the Nankai Main Line, Kintetsu Namba Line, Hanshin Namba Line, JR Namba, and the Osaka Metro Yotsubashi and Sennichimae lines.

## Literal Meaning

難波・なんば: "difficult waves"
- 難 (なん): difficult
- 波 (ば): waves — from an old shoal here where the sea once reached
