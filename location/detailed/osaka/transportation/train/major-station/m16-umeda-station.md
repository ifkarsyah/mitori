# Umeda

Osaka's largest hub, sharing the same complex as JR Osaka Station: Hankyu, Hanshin, and the Osaka Metro Tanimachi and Yotsubashi lines.

## Literal Meaning

梅田: "plum field"
- 梅 (うめ): plum
- 田 (だ): field — originally reclaimed marshland, 'umeda' also puns on 埋め立て 'landfill'
