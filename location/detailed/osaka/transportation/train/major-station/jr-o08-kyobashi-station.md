# Kyobashi

A major hub: the Keihan Main Line, Osaka Metro Nagahori Tsurumi-ryokuchi Line, and the JR Gakkentoshi (Katamachi) Line.

## Literal Meaning

京橋: "capital bridge"
- 京 (きょう): capital, i.e. Kyoto
- 橋 (ばし): bridge — on the old road toward Kyoto
