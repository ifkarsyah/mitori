# Tennoji

A major southern Osaka hub: also served by the Tanimachi Line, Kintetsu Minami-Osaka Line, and the JR Hanwa Line toward Kansai Airport.

## Literal Meaning

天王寺: "heavenly king temple"
- 天王 (てんのう): heavenly king
- 寺 (じ): temple, from Shitennoji temple nearby
