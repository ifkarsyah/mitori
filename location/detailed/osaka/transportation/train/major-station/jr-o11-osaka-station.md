# Osaka

Osaka's main terminal, adjoining the Umeda complex: Hankyu, Hanshin, and the Osaka Metro Midosuji, Tanimachi, and Yotsubashi lines.

## Literal Meaning

大阪: "big slope"
- 大 (おお): big
- 阪 (さか): slope/hill
