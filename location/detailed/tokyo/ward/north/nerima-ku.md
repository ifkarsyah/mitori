# Nerima

## Literal Meaning

練馬: "training ground for horses" (folk etymology; the exact origin is debated, possibly from 練場 "kneading ground" for leather)
- 練 (ねり): knead, train, polish
- 馬 (ま): horse

## Things to See

### Nature
- Shakujii Park: park centered on two ponds, Sanpoji and Ishii, popular for boating and blossoms

### Touristic Area
- Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo – The Making of Harry Potter: large studio tour built on the former site of Toshimaen amusement park
