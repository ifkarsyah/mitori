# Kita

## Literal Meaning

北: "north"
- 北 (きた): north

## Things to See

### Nature
- Asukayama Park: historic hillside park famous as one of Tokyo's earliest cherry blossom spots

### Touristic Area
- Akabane: lively neighborhood known for its retro shopping street and cheap izakayas
