# Itabashi

## Literal Meaning

板橋: "plank bridge"
- 板 (いた): board, plank
- 橋 (はし): bridge

## Things to See

### Nature
- Arakawa riverside (Toda area): green riverside space along the Arakawa River popular for cycling

### Culture
- Tokyo Daibutsu (Joren-ji): a large seated Buddha statue at a temple in the Akatsuka neighborhood

### Touristic Area
- Itabashi Art Museum: museum focused on Edo-period and modern Japanese art
