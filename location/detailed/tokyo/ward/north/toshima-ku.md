# Toshima

## Literal Meaning

豊島: "abundant island"
- 豊 (とよ): abundant, bountiful
- 島 (しま): island

## Things to See

### Touristic Area
- Ikebukuro: major shopping, dining, and entertainment hub around one of Tokyo's busiest stations
- Sunshine City: large complex with an aquarium, planetarium, and observation deck
