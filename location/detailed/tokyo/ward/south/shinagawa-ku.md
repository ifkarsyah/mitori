# Shinagawa

## Literal Meaning

品川: "goods river" (named after the Shinagawa River)
- 品 (しな): goods, item, kind
- 川 (がわ): river

## Things to See

### Culture
- Shinagawa Shrine: historic Shinto shrine along the old Tokaido road, known for its dragon-carved torii

### Touristic Area
- Tennozu Isle: converted waterfront warehouse district with cafes, galleries, and canal views
- Gotenyama: hillside area known for cherry blossoms, once a shogunate-era garden
