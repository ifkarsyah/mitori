# Ota

## Literal Meaning

大田: a portmanteau formed when Omori (大森) and Kamata (蒲田) merged, taking one kanji from each
- 大 (おお): big, taken from 大森 (Omori)
- 田 (た): rice field, taken from 蒲田 (Kamata)

## Things to See

### Nature
- Tamagawa River: riverside park along Tokyo's border with Kanagawa, popular for walks and fireworks

### Touristic Area
- Haneda Airport: one of Tokyo's two major airports, with an observation deck and shopping/dining area
- Kamata: working-class neighborhood known for its retail arcades and public bathhouses
