# Meguro

## Literal Meaning

目黒: "black eye" (traditionally linked to the black-eyed Fudo statue at Meguro Fudoson temple)
- 目 (め): eye
- 黒 (くろ): black

## Things to See

### Nature
- Meguro River: canal-side walking path famous for its cherry blossom tunnel each spring

### Culture
- Meguro Fudoson: historic temple linked to the ward's name and its black-eyed Fudo statue

### Touristic Area
- Nakameguro: trendy neighborhood along the river with boutique cafes and shops
