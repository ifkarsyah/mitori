# Edogawa

## Literal Meaning

江戸川: "Edo river" (named after the Edogawa River; 江戸/Edo itself means "estuary gate")
- 江戸 (えど): Edo (old name of Tokyo) — 江 inlet/bay + 戸 door/gate
- 川 (がわ): river

## Things to See

### Nature
- Kasai Rinkai Park: large waterfront park with a Ferris wheel, bird sanctuary, and beach

### Touristic Area
- Tokyo Sea Life Park: aquarium within Kasai Rinkai Park known for its tuna tank
- Gyosen Park: park with a small ski slope and playground, popular with local families
