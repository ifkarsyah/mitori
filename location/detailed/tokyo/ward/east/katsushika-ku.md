# Katsushika

## Literal Meaning

葛飾: "kudzu decoration" (the kanji are largely phonetic; the name predates the current characters and its true origin is uncertain)
- 葛 (かつ): kudzu, arrowroot vine
- 飾 (しか): decoration, adornment

## Things to See

### Nature
- Mizumoto Park: large waterside park with wetlands, popular for cherry blossoms and irises

### Culture
- Shibamata Taishakuten: historic temple street that inspired the long-running Tora-san film series

### Touristic Area
- Katsushika Tora-san Museum: museum dedicated to the beloved "Otoko wa Tsurai yo" film character
