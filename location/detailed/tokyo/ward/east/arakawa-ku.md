# Arakawa

## Literal Meaning

荒川: "wild/rough river" (named after the Arakawa River)
- 荒 (あら): rough, wild
- 川 (かわ): river

## Things to See

### Nature
- Arakawa River: riverside park and cycling path along the ward's namesake river

### Touristic Area
- Nippori Fabric Town: shopping street lined with fabric and textile shops
- Arakawa Amusement Park: small, retro neighborhood amusement park (Arakawa Yuen)
