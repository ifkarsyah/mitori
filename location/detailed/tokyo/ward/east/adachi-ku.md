# Adachi

## Literal Meaning

足立: "standing feet" (kanji likely applied phonetically to an older name, 葦立ち/ashi-dachi, "where reeds stand")
- 足 (あ): foot, leg
- 立 (だち): standing

## Things to See

### Nature
- Arakawa riverside: green embankment along the Arakawa River, popular for walks and cycling

### Culture
- Nishi-Arai Daishi: major Buddhist temple known for its flower gardens and fudo statue

### Touristic Area
- Senju: historic post-town district with a lively shopping arcade
