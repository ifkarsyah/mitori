# Sumida

## Literal Meaning

墨田: "ink rice field" (name blends 墨堤/boku-tei, the ink-black river embankment, with 隅田/Sumida, the river's older name)
- 墨 (すみ): ink, ink stick
- 田 (だ): rice field

## Things to See

### Nature
- Sumida River: river lined with walking paths, famous for cherry blossoms and summer fireworks

### Touristic Area
- Tokyo Skytree: tallest structure in Japan, with two observation decks
- Ryogoku Kokugikan: Tokyo's main sumo arena, host of grand sumo tournaments
- Edo-Tokyo Museum: museum covering the history of Edo-era and modern Tokyo
