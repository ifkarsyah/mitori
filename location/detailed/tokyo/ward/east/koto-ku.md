# Koto

## Literal Meaning

江東: "east of the river" (east of the Sumida River)
- 江 (こう): inlet, river
- 東 (とう): east

## Things to See

### Nature
- Kiyosumi Garden: traditional Japanese garden built around a large pond

### Touristic Area
- Tokyo Big Sight: Japan's largest convention and exhibition center
- teamLab Planets: immersive digital art museum with interactive light and water installations
