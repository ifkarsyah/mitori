# Nakano

## Literal Meaning

中野: "middle field/plain"
- 中 (なか): middle
- 野 (の): field, plain

## Things to See

### Touristic Area
- Nakano Broadway: shopping complex famous for its otaku, anime, and collectible shops
- Nakano Sun Plaza: multi-purpose hall used for concerts and events
