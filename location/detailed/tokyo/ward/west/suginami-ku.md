# Suginami

## Literal Meaning

杉並: "row of cedars" (from cedar trees once lined along the old Aome Kaido road)
- 杉 (すぎ): Japanese cedar
- 並 (なみ): row, lined up

## Things to See

### Nature
- Wadabori Park: riverside park along the Zenpukuji River, popular for its ponds and greenery

### Touristic Area
- Koenji: neighborhood known for vintage clothing stores, live music venues, and its summer Awa Odori dance festival
- Suginami Animation Museum: small museum tracing the history of Japanese anime production
