# Chiyoda

## Literal Meaning

千代田: "field of a thousand generations" (a poetic term for eternity, referencing the Imperial Palace grounds)
- 千代 (ちよ): thousand generations, eternity
- 田 (だ): rice field

## Things to See

### Nature
- Imperial Palace East Gardens: former Edo Castle grounds, open to the public with seasonal gardens

### Culture
- Yasukuni Shrine: Shinto shrine honoring Japan's war dead, known for its cherry blossoms and museum

### Touristic Area
- Imperial Palace: primary residence of Japan's Imperial Family, built on the site of Edo Castle
- Akihabara: electronics and anime/manga shopping district, partly within Chiyoda
- Tokyo Station: historic red-brick railway station and major transport hub
