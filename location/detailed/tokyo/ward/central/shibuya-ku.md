# Shibuya

## Literal Meaning

渋谷: "astringent valley"
- 渋 (しぶ): astringent, bitter
- 谷 (や): valley

## Things to See

### Nature
- Meiji Jingu: forested Shinto shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji, right next to Harajuku
- Yoyogi Park: large park bordering Meiji Jingu, popular for picnics, street performers, and weekend cosplay gatherings

### Touristic Area
- Shibuya Scramble Crossing: the world's busiest pedestrian scramble crossing, a symbol of Tokyo's energy
- Hachiko Statue: bronze statue of the loyal Akita dog, a well-known meeting spot right outside Shibuya Station
- Shibuya Sky observation deck: rooftop observation deck atop Shibuya Scramble Square with open-air 360° views of the city
- Shibuya 109: iconic cylindrical department store, long the center of Shibuya's youth fashion scene
