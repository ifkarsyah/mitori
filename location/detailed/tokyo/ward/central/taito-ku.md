# Taito

## Literal Meaning

台東: "east of the plateau" (east of the Ueno terrace; the name was coined when Shitaya and Asakusa wards merged)
- 台 (たい): platform, plateau, terrace
- 東 (とう): east

## Things to See

### Nature
- Ueno Park: large public park home to museums, a zoo, and famous cherry blossoms

### Culture
- Senso-ji: Tokyo's oldest temple, approached via the Kaminarimon gate in Asakusa

### Touristic Area
- Ameyoko Market: crowded market street near Ueno Station selling food, snacks, and discount goods
- Tokyo National Museum: Japan's oldest and largest museum, holding major art and antiquities collections
- Ueno Zoo: Japan's oldest zoo, known for its giant pandas
