# Shinjuku

## Literal Meaning

新宿: "new lodging" (originally a new post station added along the old Koshu Kaido road)
- 新 (しん): new
- 宿 (じゅく): lodging, inn, post station

## Things to See

### Nature
- Shinjuku Gyoen: large landscaped park blending Japanese, French, and English garden styles

### Touristic Area
- Kabukicho: Tokyo's largest entertainment and nightlife district
- Golden Gai: cluster of tiny alleyway bars packed into a few narrow lanes
- Omoide Yokocho: nostalgic alley of small yakitori and izakaya stalls near Shinjuku Station
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building: twin-towered building with a free observation deck
