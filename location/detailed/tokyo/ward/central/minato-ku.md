# Minato

## Literal Meaning

港: "harbor/port"
- 港 (みなと): harbor, port

## Things to See

### Nature
- Shiba Park: park surrounding Tokyo Tower with views of Zojoji Temple

### Culture
- Zojoji Temple: major Buddhist temple beside Tokyo Tower, historically tied to the Tokugawa shogunate

### Touristic Area
- Tokyo Tower: red-and-white communications tower and Tokyo landmark modeled after the Eiffel Tower
- Roppongi Hills: large urban complex with shopping, art museums, and an observation deck
- Odaiba: man-made bay island with shopping malls, entertainment, and waterfront views
