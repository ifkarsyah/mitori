# Chuo

## Literal Meaning

中央: "center"
- 中 (ちゅう): middle
- 央 (おう): center

## Things to See

### Touristic Area
- Ginza: upscale shopping and dining district lined with flagship stores
- Tsukiji Outer Market: bustling market street known for fresh seafood and street food
- Nihonbashi: historic bridge and district marking the starting point of Japan's old highway system
- Kabuki-za: leading theater for traditional kabuki performances
