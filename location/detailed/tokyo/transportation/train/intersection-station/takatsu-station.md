# Takatsu

Served by both the Den-en-toshi Line and the Oimachi Line (shared trackage between Futako-Tamagawa and Mizonokuchi).

## Literal Meaning

高津: "high harbor"
- 高 (たか): high
- 津 (つ): harbor/port
