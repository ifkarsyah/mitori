# Musashi-Mizonokuchi

JR's station at the same transfer point as Tokyu's Mizonokuchi (溝の口) station, a short walk away — same transfer, technically a different station name.

## Literal Meaning

武蔵溝ノ口: "Musashi's ditch-mouth"
- 武蔵 (むさし): Musashi
- 溝ノ口 (みぞのくち): ditch's mouth/opening
