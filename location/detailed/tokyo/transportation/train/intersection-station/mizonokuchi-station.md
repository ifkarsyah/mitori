# Mizonokuchi

Northern terminus of the Oimachi Line and a stop on the Den-en-toshi Line. A short walk from JR's Musashi-Mizonokuchi (武蔵溝ノ口) station on the Nambu Line — same transfer point, different station name.

## Literal Meaning

溝の口: "ditch's mouth"
- 溝 (みぞ): ditch
- の口 (のくち): 's mouth/opening
