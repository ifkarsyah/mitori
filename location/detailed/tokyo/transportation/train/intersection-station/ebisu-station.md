# Ebisu

Connects to the Hibiya subway line.

## Literal Meaning

恵比寿: "Ebisu (lucky god of fishermen)"
- 恵比寿 (えびす): one of the Seven Lucky Gods — the station is actually named after Ebisu Beer, whose brewery once stood here
