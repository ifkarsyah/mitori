# Futako-Tamagawa

Northern terminus of the Oimachi Line and a stop on the Den-en-toshi Line.

## Literal Meaning

二子玉川: "twins' Tama River"
- 二子 (ふたこ): twins, from twin burial mounds nearby
- 玉川 (たまがわ): Tama River
