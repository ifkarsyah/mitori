# Nippori

Connects to the Keisei Main Line (Skyliner) and the Toei Nippori-Toneri Liner.

## Literal Meaning

日暮里: "sunset village"
- 日暮 (ひぐれ): sunset/day's end, a poetic old name
- 里 (り): village
