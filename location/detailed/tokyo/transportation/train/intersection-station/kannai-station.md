# Kannai

Connects to the Yokohama Municipal Subway Blue Line.

## Literal Meaning

関内: "inside the checkpoint"
- 関 (かん): barrier/checkpoint
- 内 (ない): inside — from a former gate guarding the Yokohama foreign settlement
