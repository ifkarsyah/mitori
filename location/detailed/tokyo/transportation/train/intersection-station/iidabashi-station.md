# Iidabashi

Connects to the Tozai, Yurakucho, and Namboku subway lines and the Toei Oedo Line.

## Literal Meaning

飯田橋: "Iida's bridge"
- 飯田 (いいだ): a surname, 'cooked rice field'
- 橋 (ばし): bridge — named after merchant Iida Kyubei, who helped build it
