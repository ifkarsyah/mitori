# Akihabara

Connects to the JR Chuo-Sobu Line, the Hibiya subway line, and the Tsukuba Express.

## Literal Meaning

秋葉原: "Akiba's plain"
- 秋葉 (あきば): from Akiba Daigongen, a fire-prevention deity
- 原 (はら): plain
