# Ochanomizu

Connects to the Marunouchi subway line; also where the Chuo Rapid and Chuo-Sobu local tracks diverge.

## Literal Meaning

御茶ノ水: "tea water"
- 御茶 (おちゃ): honorable tea
- ノ水 (のみず): 's water — named for a spring whose water was offered to the shogun for tea
