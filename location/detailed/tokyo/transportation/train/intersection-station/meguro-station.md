# Meguro

Connects to the Tokyu Meguro Line and the Toei Mita/Namboku subway lines.

## Literal Meaning

目黒: "black eye"
- 目 (め): eye
- 黒 (ぐろ): black — from the black-eyed Fudo statue at Meguro Fudo temple
