# JR Nambu Line

A 26-station commuter line running roughly parallel to the Tama River between Kawasaki and Tachikawa, cutting through Kanagawa and western Tokyo's suburbs without passing through central Tokyo. Its main value is as a connector: it crosses nearly every private railway line radiating out from Shibuya and Shinjuku (Tokyu Toyoko/Meguro at Musashi-Kosugi, Tokyu Den-en-toshi/Oimachi near Mizonokuchi, Odakyu at Noborito, Keio at Bubaigawara), making it a useful way to move between suburbs without detouring into the city center. Line color is yellow.
