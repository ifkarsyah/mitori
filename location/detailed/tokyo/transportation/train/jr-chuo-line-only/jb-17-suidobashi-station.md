# Suidobashi

You can also connect here to: Toei Mita Line (I-11).

## Literal Meaning

水道橋: "waterworks bridge"
- 水道 (すいどう): waterworks, from the historic Kanda Josui aqueduct
- 橋 (ばし): bridge
