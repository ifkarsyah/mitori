# Iidabashi

You can also connect here to: Namboku Line (N-10); Yurakucho Line (Y-13); Toei Oedo Line (E-06).

## Literal Meaning

飯田橋: "Iida's bridge"
- 飯田 (いいだ): a surname, 'cooked rice field'
- 橋 (ばし): bridge — named after merchant Iida Kyubei, who helped build it
