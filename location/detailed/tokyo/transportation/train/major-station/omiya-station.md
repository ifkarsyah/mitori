# Omiya

Also a stop on the Keihin-Tohoku Line, plus a major Shinkansen hub (Tohoku, Joetsu, Hokuriku) and the JR Utsunomiya, Takasaki, and Kawagoe lines.

## Literal Meaning

大宮: "big shrine"
- 大 (おお): big
- 宮 (みや): shrine, from the large Hikawa Shrine here
