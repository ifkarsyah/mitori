# Ueno

A major hub with Shinkansen and limited express lines to Tohoku, the Ginza and Hibiya subway lines, and the Keisei Main Line (Skyliner to Narita).

Also a short walk from Keisei-Ueno (KS-01) on the Keisei Main Line — a differently-named adjacent station, same transfer point.

## Literal Meaning

上野: "upper field"
- 上 (うえ): upper
- 野 (の): field
