# Ikebukuro

A major western Tokyo hub: the Seibu Ikebukuro Line, Tobu Tojo Line, and the Marunouchi, Yurakucho, and Fukutoshin subway lines.

## Literal Meaning

池袋: "bag-shaped pond"
- 池 (いけ): pond
- 袋 (ぶくろ): bag/sack, describing the pond's shape
