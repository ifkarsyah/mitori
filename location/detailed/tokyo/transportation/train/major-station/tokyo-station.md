# Tokyo

The national rail hub: Shinkansen lines, the Chuo/Keihin-Tohoku/Yokosuka/Sobu lines, and the Marunouchi subway line.

## Literal Meaning

東京: "eastern capital"
- 東 (とう): east
- 京 (きょう): capital
