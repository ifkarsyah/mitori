# Tachikawa

Northern terminus; connects to the JR Chuo Line, JR Ome Line, JR Itsukaichi Line, and the Tama Toshi Monorail.

## Literal Meaning

立川: "standing river"
- 立 (たち): standing, likely from a steep riverbank
- 川 (かわ): river
