# Musashi-Kosugi

A major, fast-growing hub: the JR Yokosuka and Shonan-Shinjuku lines, and the Tokyu Toyoko and Meguro lines.

## Literal Meaning

武蔵小杉: "Musashi's small cedar"
- 武蔵 (むさし): Musashi
- 小杉 (こすぎ): small cedar
