# Yokohama

A major hub: the Tokyu Toyoko Line, Sotetsu Main Line, Keikyu Main Line, Minatomirai Line, and the Yokohama Municipal Subway Blue Line.

## Literal Meaning

横浜: "side beach"
- 横 (よこ): side/horizontal
- 浜 (はま): beach
