# Tokyu Oimachi Line

An 18-station line running from Oimachi (on the JR Yamanote/Keihin-Tohoku lines) through Jiyugaoka to Mizonokuchi, sharing track with the Den-en-toshi Line for its final stretch from Futako-Tamagawa to Mizonokuchi. It began as part of the Den-en-toshi Line before splitting off as its own line in 1963. It's a useful way to reach southwestern Tokyo and Kawasaki without routing through the more crowded Shibuya terminus, and it's the line locals use to reach Jiyugaoka's shopping and cafe district.
