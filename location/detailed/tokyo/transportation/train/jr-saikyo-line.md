# JR Saikyo Line

A 19-station commuter line running Osaki–Omiya, sharing the Yamanote Line's central-Tokyo stops (Shibuya, Shinjuku, Ikebukuro) before branching north through Saitama. It runs express relative to the Yamanote loop through its shared stretch, and through-services extend further south onto the Rinkai Line (toward Shin-Kiba) and north onto the Kawagoe Line, though those are treated as separate lines here.
