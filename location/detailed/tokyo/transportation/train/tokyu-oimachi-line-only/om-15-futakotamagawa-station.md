# Futako-Tamagawa

You can also connect here to: Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line (DT-07).

## Literal Meaning

二子玉川: "twins' Tama River"
- 二子 (ふたこ): twins, from twin burial mounds nearby
- 玉川 (たまがわ): Tama River
