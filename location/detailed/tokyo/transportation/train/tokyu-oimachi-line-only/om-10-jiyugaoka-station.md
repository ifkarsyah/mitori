# Jiyugaoka

You can also connect here to: Tokyu Toyoko Line (TY-07).

## Literal Meaning

自由が丘: "freedom hill"
- 自由 (じゆう): freedom/liberty
- が丘 (がおか): hill — renamed in 1932 after the nearby Jiyu Gakuen 'Freedom School'
