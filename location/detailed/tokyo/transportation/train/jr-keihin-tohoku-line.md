# JR Keihin-Tohoku Line

A long commuter line running Omiya–Ofuna (through-servicing onto the Negishi Line south of Yokohama), around 46 stations end to end. Through central Tokyo it runs on the same tracks and stops at the same stations as the Yamanote Line, so the two are easy to conflate — the difference is Keihin-Tohoku continues straight north to Saitama and south through Kawasaki and Yokohama instead of looping back. It's the line most people actually mean when commuting between Tokyo and Yokohama on a "local" JR train.
