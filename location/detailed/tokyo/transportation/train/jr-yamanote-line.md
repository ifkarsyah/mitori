# JR Yamanote Line

A 30-station loop line encircling central Tokyo, and one of the busiest urban rail lines in the world. It ties together nearly every major hub in the city — Tokyo, Ueno, Ikebukuro, Shinjuku, Shibuya, and Shinagawa — which makes it the default backbone for getting anywhere centrally in Tokyo, with most other lines (JR, subway, and private) crossing it at some point. A full loop takes just under an hour, and trains run every 2-4 minutes at peak times. Line color is green.
