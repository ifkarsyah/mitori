# Soshigaya-Okura

## Literal Meaning

祖師ヶ谷大蔵: "patriarch's valley, great storehouse"
- 祖師ヶ谷 (そしがや): founding-master's valley, a Buddhist patriarch reference
- 大蔵 (おおくら): great storehouse — two merged village names
