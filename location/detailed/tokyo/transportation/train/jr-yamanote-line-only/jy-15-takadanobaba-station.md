# Takadanobaba

You can also connect here to: Tozai Line (T-03); Seibu Shinjuku Line (SS-02).

## Literal Meaning

高田馬場: "Takada's horse-riding ground"
- 高田 (たかだ): high field
- 馬場 (ばば): horse-riding ground, an Edo-period archery/horsemanship training ground
