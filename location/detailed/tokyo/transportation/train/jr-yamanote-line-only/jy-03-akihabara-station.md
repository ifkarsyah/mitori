# Akihabara

You can also connect here to: JR Keihin-Tohoku Line (JK-28); Hibiya Line (H-16).

## Literal Meaning

秋葉原: "Akiba's plain"
- 秋葉 (あきば): from Akiba Daigongen, a fire-prevention deity
- 原 (はら): plain
