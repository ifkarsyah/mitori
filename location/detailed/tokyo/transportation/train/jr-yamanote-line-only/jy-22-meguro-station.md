# Meguro

You can also connect here to: Namboku Line (N-01); Toei Mita Line (I-01); Tokyu Meguro Line (MG-01).

## Literal Meaning

目黒: "black eye"
- 目 (め): eye
- 黒 (ぐろ): black — from the black-eyed Fudo statue at Meguro Fudo temple
