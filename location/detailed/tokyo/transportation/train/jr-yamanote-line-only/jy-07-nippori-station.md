# Nippori

You can also connect here to: JR Keihin-Tohoku Line (JK-32); Toei Nippori-Toneri Liner (NT-01); Keisei Main Line (KS-02).

## Literal Meaning

日暮里: "sunset village"
- 日暮 (ひぐれ): sunset/day's end, a poetic old name
- 里 (り): village
