# Okachimachi

You can also connect here to: JR Keihin-Tohoku Line (JK-29).

## Literal Meaning

御徒町: "town of the foot soldiers"
- 御徒 (おかち): low-ranking samurai who walked rather than rode
- 町 (まち): town
