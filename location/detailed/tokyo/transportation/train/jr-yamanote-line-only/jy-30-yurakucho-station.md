# Yurakucho

You can also connect here to: JR Keihin-Tohoku Line (JK-25); Yurakucho Line (Y-18).

## Literal Meaning

有楽町: "Yuraku's town"
- 有楽 (ゆうらく): from Oda Uraku, a tea master whose residence was here
- 町 (ちょう): town
