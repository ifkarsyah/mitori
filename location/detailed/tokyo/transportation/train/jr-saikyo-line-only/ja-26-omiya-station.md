# Omiya

You can also connect here to: JR Keihin-Tohoku Line (JK-47); JR Utsunomiya Line (JU-07).

## Literal Meaning

大宮: "big shrine"
- 大 (おお): big
- 宮 (みや): shrine, from the large Hikawa Shrine here
