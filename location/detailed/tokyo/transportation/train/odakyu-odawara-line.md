# Odakyu Odawara Line

Odakyu's main trunk line, running 47 stations from Shinjuku out to the resort/gateway city of Odawara at the foot of Hakone. The Tokyo stretch through Shimokitazawa and Seijogakuemmae is a dense, well-known commuter corridor; past Machida and Sagami-Ono it opens up into suburban Kanagawa, eventually reaching Odawara where it connects to the Tokaido Shinkansen. Two branch lines split off along the way — the Tama Line at Shin-Yurigaoka and the Enoshima Line at Sagami-Ono — treated here as separate lines, not included in this station list.
