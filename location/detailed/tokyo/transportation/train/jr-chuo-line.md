# JR Chuo Line

The orange line running Tokyo–Takao through the heart of the city, cutting a straight east-west path that Yamanote's loop doesn't cover. Scope here is the Rapid (快速) service and its full physical station list, including stops served only by the parallel local Chuo-Sobu trains between Tokyo and Mitaka (some of which the Rapid itself skips). West of Mitaka it runs as a single well-known commuter line out to the western Tokyo suburbs, continuing past Takao as the Chuo Main Line toward Otsuki and Kofu.
